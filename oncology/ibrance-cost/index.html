Ibrance cost, coupon & copay card

Ibrance (palbociclib) is an oral CDK4/6 inhibitor from Pfizer used to treat adults with HR-positive, HER2-negative breast cancer.

The short answer

Ibrance has a list price of about ~$13,200/mo, but commercially insured patients may pay as little as $0 per prescription with the Ibrance Pfizer Oncology Together Co-pay Card (up to $10,000/yr, 12 uses). People with Medicare Part D can't use the copay card, but their out-of-pocket drug costs are capped at $2,100 for 2026. Uninsured or underinsured patients can apply to Pfizer Oncology Together for help.

Ibrance is taken by mouth, usually alongside hormone (endocrine) therapy, and is often continued for many months as long as it remains effective and tolerated. Because it is a brand-only specialty oncology drug with a high list price, what you actually pay depends mostly on your insurance type, your plan's specialty-tier rules, and whether you qualify for manufacturer support.

The Ibrance copay card

Commercially insured patients may pay as little as $0 per prescription with the Ibrance Pfizer Oncology Together Co-pay Card.

The co-pay card is Pfizer's savings program for people whose Ibrance is covered by private or employer insurance. It can reduce your out-of-pocket cost per prescription, subject to an annual benefit limit and a maximum number of uses. It cannot be used by patients with Medicare, Medicaid, or other government insurance.

Ibrance cost with Medicare

Ibrance does not have a Medicare negotiated (maximum fair) price in effect for 2026. It is typically covered under Medicare Part D, where your costs before reaching the cap vary by plan. In 2026, the Part D out-of-pocket maximum is $2,100 per year, after which you pay nothing more for covered drugs that year. Federal rules bar manufacturer copay cards for Medicare patients, but you may be able to spread costs across the year using the Medicare Prescription Payment Plan, and Pfizer Oncology Together may be able to help.

Patient assistance for Ibrance

Pfizer Oncology Together helps patients prescribed Pfizer cancer medicines, including Ibrance, navigate coverage and find financial support. Depending on eligibility, uninsured or underinsured patients may qualify for help paying for Ibrance, and the program can also connect patients with other resources. Your oncology team or specialty pharmacy can help you apply.

Cash-pay strategies for Ibrance

Without insurance, Ibrance's list price is high, at about ~$13,200/mo, so paying full cash price is rarely practical. Retail discount cards usually offer limited savings on specialty cancer drugs, so the best first step is applying to Pfizer Oncology Together for patient assistance. Independent cancer charities and foundations may also help when funding is available.

Cheaper alternatives to Ibrance

Other oral CDK4/6 inhibitors are also used for HR-positive, HER2-negative breast cancer, and coverage and costs differ by plan. Your oncologist can tell you whether another option in this class is clinically appropriate and preferred on your formulary.

I tell patients to call the Ibrance financial support line or Pfizer Oncology Together before their first fill, not after a large bill arrives. If you have commercial insurance, enroll in the co-pay card right away and track your 12 uses, since the benefit has an annual limit. If you have Medicare, remember the 2026 $2,100 cap limits your yearly out-of-pocket drug costs, and ask about spreading payments over the year.

Ibrance cost — frequently asked questions

How much does Ibrance cost with the copay card?

With commercial insurance, you may pay as little as $0 per prescription with the Ibrance Pfizer Oncology Together Co-pay Card. The benefit is up to $10,000/yr, 12 uses, and it is not available to patients with Medicare or Medicaid.

Is there a generic or biosimilar for Ibrance?

Ibrance (palbociclib) is a small-molecule pill, so a biosimilar does not apply. Generic palbociclib is not widely available in the US as of this writing, so ask your pharmacist about current availability.

How much does Ibrance cost without insurance?

Ibrance's list price is about ~$13,200/mo. If you're uninsured, apply to Pfizer Oncology Together (pfizeroncologytogether.com/patient), which may help eligible patients get the medicine at reduced or no cost.

Does Medicare cover Ibrance, and what will I pay?

Ibrance is generally covered under Medicare Part D, and costs vary by plan. In 2026, your Part D out-of-pocket spending is capped at $2,100 for the year. Medicare patients cannot use the manufacturer copay card, and Ibrance does not have a Medicare negotiated price in effect for 2026.

How do I enroll in the Ibrance copay card?

Visit the official Ibrance financial support page at https://www.ibrance.com/financial-support-resources#Insured to enroll. You must have commercial insurance; Medicare and Medicaid patients are not eligible but can contact Pfizer Oncology Together for other support.
