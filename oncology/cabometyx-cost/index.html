Cabometyx cost, coupon & copay card

Cabometyx (cabozantinib) is a once-daily oral VEGFR/MET kinase inhibitor used to treat certain adults with renal cell carcinoma, hepatocellular carcinoma, neuroendocrine tumors, and thyroid cancer.

The short answer

Cabometyx has a list price of about ~$26,470/mo without insurance. With commercial insurance, you may pay as little as $0 per month with the Cabometyx EASE Co-pay Program. On Medicare Part D, the copay card cannot be used, but your out-of-pocket drug costs are capped at $2,100 in 2026, and the Exelixis Access Services (EASE) Patient Assistance Program may help eligible patients.

Cabometyx is taken by mouth once daily, usually long term for as long as it keeps the cancer controlled and side effects stay manageable. Because it is a brand-only specialty oncology drug with no generic or biosimilar, the list price is high. What you actually pay depends mostly on your insurance type, your plan's specialty-tier rules, and whether you qualify for manufacturer support.

The Cabometyx copay card

Eligible commercially insured patients may pay as little as $0 per month with the Cabometyx EASE Co-pay Program.

The Cabometyx EASE Co-pay Program helps commercially insured patients lower their out-of-pocket cost for each fill. The program has an annual maximum benefit that applies per prescription per year, and costs above that limit are your responsibility. It cannot be used by people with Medicare, Medicaid, or other government insurance. Enrollment and full terms are available on the official Cabometyx cost and financial support page.

Cabometyx cost with Medicare

Cabometyx is not among the drugs with a Medicare-negotiated price. Coverage and cost sharing vary by Part D plan, and it is typically placed on a specialty tier. In 2026, Part D out-of-pocket drug costs are capped at $2,100 per year, which limits total spending for patients taking a high-cost drug like Cabometyx. Federal rules bar manufacturer copay cards for Medicare patients, but Medicare enrollees may qualify for Extra Help or the Exelixis Access Services (EASE) Patient Assistance Program.

Patient assistance for Cabometyx

Exelixis Access Services (EASE) offers a patient assistance program for eligible patients who are uninsured, underinsured, or cannot afford their treatment. EASE can also help with benefits verification and referrals to independent foundations. Eligibility requirements apply, and your oncology team or specialty pharmacy can usually help start the application.

Cash-pay strategies for Cabometyx

Without insurance, the list price of Cabometyx is about ~$26,470/mo, and there is no generic or biosimilar to fall back on. Retail discount cards usually make little difference for specialty oncology drugs like this one. The most useful step is to contact Exelixis Access Services (EASE) to see whether you qualify for patient assistance.

Cheaper alternatives to Cabometyx

Other kinase inhibitors and immunotherapy regimens are used for kidney, liver, thyroid, and neuroendocrine cancers, but the right choice depends on tumor type, prior treatment, and your overall health. Do not switch for cost reasons without talking with your oncologist.

I tell patients to call their specialty pharmacy and Exelixis Access Services before the first fill, not after a large bill arrives, because benefits checks and enrollment take time. Dose reductions for side effects are common with cabozantinib, so I remind patients to ask whether a new tablet strength changes their copay or needs a new prior authorization. If you have Medicare, plan for the 2026 $2,100 cap, since you will often reach it early in the year on a drug this expensive.

Cabometyx cost — frequently asked questions

How much does Cabometyx cost with the copay card?

With commercial insurance, you may pay as little as $0 per month with the Cabometyx EASE Co-pay Program. An annual maximum applies per prescription per year, and the program is not available to Medicare or Medicaid patients.

Is there a generic or biosimilar for Cabometyx?

No. Cabometyx (cabozantinib) is available only as a brand-name drug, and no biosimilar or generic is currently available.

How much is Cabometyx without insurance?

The list price is about ~$26,470/mo. Uninsured patients should contact Exelixis Access Services (EASE) to see if they qualify for the patient assistance program.

Does Medicare cover Cabometyx, and what will I pay?

Most Medicare Part D plans cover Cabometyx, but cost sharing varies by plan. In 2026, out-of-pocket Part D drug costs are capped at $2,100. Cabometyx does not have a Medicare-negotiated price, and the copay card cannot be used with Medicare.

How do I enroll in the Cabometyx EASE Co-pay Program?

You can enroll through the official Cabometyx cost and financial support page at https://www.cabometyx.com/cost-financial-support. Your oncology office or specialty pharmacy can also help you enroll.
