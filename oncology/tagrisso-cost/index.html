Tagrisso cost, coupon & copay card

Tagrisso (osimertinib) is an oral EGFR tyrosine kinase inhibitor from AstraZeneca for adults with EGFR-mutated non-small cell lung cancer.

The short answer

Tagrisso has a list price of ~$18,278/mo, but commercially insured patients may pay as little as $0 per month with the Tagrisso Access 360 Co-pay Program (monthly and annual limits apply). People with Medicare Part D cannot use the copay card, but their 2026 out-of-pocket drug costs are capped at $2,100 for the year, and uninsured or underinsured patients can apply to the AZ&Me Prescription Savings Program.

Tagrisso is taken by mouth once daily and is prescribed only after tumor testing confirms an EGFR mutation; it is often continued for months to years, so monthly costs add up quickly. Its price is driven by its status as a brand-only targeted cancer therapy, and what you actually pay depends heavily on your insurance type, plan tier, and specialty pharmacy rules.

The Tagrisso copay card

Commercially insured patients may pay as little as $0 per month with the Tagrisso Access 360 Co-pay Program.

The Tagrisso Access 360 Co-pay Program helps cover out-of-pocket costs left after your commercial or private insurance pays its share. Monthly and annual limits apply, so check the program terms for your plan year. The program is for commercial insurance only and cannot be used with Medicare, Medicaid, or other government insurance.

Tagrisso cost with Medicare

Tagrisso does not currently have a Medicare negotiated price. In 2026, Medicare Part D caps annual out-of-pocket prescription costs at $2,100, and because Tagrisso is expensive, many patients reach that cap early in the year. Federal rules bar manufacturer copay cards for Medicare beneficiaries, but you can ask your plan about spreading payments across the year, and lower-income patients may qualify for Extra Help or manufacturer assistance.

Patient assistance for Tagrisso

AstraZeneca's AZ&Me Prescription Savings Program helps eligible patients who are uninsured or have limited coverage get AstraZeneca medicines at reduced or no cost. Eligibility depends on income and insurance status, so review the criteria and apply online, ideally with help from your oncology team or specialty pharmacy.

Cash-pay strategies for Tagrisso

Without insurance, Tagrisso's list price is ~$18,278/mo, which is out of reach for most people. Pharmacy discount cards rarely make a meaningful dent in specialty cancer drug prices, so the AZ&Me Prescription Savings Program is usually the most realistic path for uninsured patients. Ask your cancer center's financial counselor about foundation grants and other assistance as well.

Cheaper alternatives to Tagrisso

There is no generic or biosimilar for Tagrisso available in the US at this time. Other EGFR-targeted therapies exist, but the choice depends on your specific mutation, disease stage, and treatment history, so any switch should be discussed with your oncologist rather than made for cost alone.

As a pathologist, I confirm the EGFR mutation results that determine whether a drug like Tagrisso is appropriate, and I tell patients that a positive result is the start of an access process, not just a treatment decision. I encourage them to bring in their insurance card early so the clinic can run benefits checks and enroll them in the co-pay program or AZ&Me before the first fill. Delays in a targeted lung cancer therapy are usually about paperwork, and those can almost always be solved.

Tagrisso cost — frequently asked questions

How much does Tagrisso cost with the copay card?

With commercial insurance, you may pay as little as $0 per month with the Tagrisso Access 360 Co-pay Program. Monthly and annual limits apply.

Is there a generic or biosimilar for Tagrisso?

No. Tagrisso (osimertinib) is a brand-only drug in the US at this time, and there is no biosimilar.

How much does Tagrisso cost without insurance?

The list price is ~$18,278/mo. Uninsured patients should look into the AZ&Me Prescription Savings Program, which may lower costs for eligible patients.

Does Medicare cover Tagrisso, and can I use the copay card?

Medicare Part D plans may cover Tagrisso, and in 2026 your out-of-pocket drug costs are capped at $2,100 for the year. The Access 360 copay card cannot be used with Medicare or Medicaid.

How do I enroll in the Tagrisso copay program?

Commercially insured patients can enroll through AstraZeneca's patient support site at https://www.azpatientsupport.com/home/commercially-insured.html?brand=TAGRISSO&role=Patient. Your oncology clinic or specialty pharmacy can help with enrollment.
