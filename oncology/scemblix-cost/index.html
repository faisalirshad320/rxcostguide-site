Scemblix cost, coupon & copay card

Scemblix (asciminib) is an oral BCR-ABL1 (STAMP) inhibitor from Novartis used to treat adults with Philadelphia chromosome-positive chronic myeloid leukemia (CML).

The short answer

Scemblix has a list price of about ~$21,854/mo before insurance. With commercial insurance, the Scemblix Co-Pay Plus offer can bring your cost to as little as $0 per prescription, with savings of up to $15,000/yr. On Medicare Part D, copay cards can't be used, but your out-of-pocket drug costs are capped at $2,100 in 2026, and the Novartis Patient Assistance Foundation may help if you qualify.

Scemblix is a daily oral targeted therapy for CML, typically taken long term, so cost adds up month after month. It is a brand-only specialty medication usually dispensed through a specialty pharmacy, and what you actually pay depends mainly on your insurance type, your plan's specialty-tier cost-sharing, and whether you can use the manufacturer's copay offer or patient assistance.

The Scemblix copay card

The Scemblix Co-Pay Plus offer can lower your cost to as little as $0 per prescription if you have commercial insurance.

Novartis's Scemblix Co-Pay Plus offer covers much of your out-of-pocket cost for Scemblix when you have commercial (private or employer) insurance, up to an annual maximum. Once the yearly savings limit is reached, you pay your plan's normal cost-sharing for the rest of the year. The offer cannot be used with Medicare, Medicaid, or other government insurance. Scemblix cost with Medicare

Scemblix is not one of the drugs with a Medicare negotiated price, so your Part D cost depends on your plan's formulary and specialty-tier cost-sharing. In 2026, total out-of-pocket spending on covered Part D drugs is capped at $2,100 for the year, which limits what you can pay for a high-cost medicine like Scemblix. Federal rules bar manufacturer copay cards for Medicare patients, but you can ask your plan about the Medicare Prescription Payment Plan to spread costs over the year, and you may be able to apply to the Novartis Patient Assistance Foundation.

Patient assistance for Scemblix

The Novartis Patient Assistance Foundation may provide Scemblix at no cost to eligible patients who are uninsured or whose insurance doesn't adequately cover it. Eligibility depends on income, insurance status, and other criteria, and the application usually requires input from your prescriber. Check the foundation's website for current requirements.

Cash-pay strategies for Scemblix

Without insurance, Scemblix lists at about ~$21,854/mo, and pharmacy discount cards typically offer limited savings on brand-only specialty drugs like this one. The best option for most uninsured patients is applying to the Novartis Patient Assistance Foundation. If cost is a barrier, ask your oncologist whether another CML treatment, including an older tyrosine kinase inhibitor available as a generic, is clinically appropriate for you.

Cheaper alternatives to Scemblix

Other BCR-ABL1 tyrosine kinase inhibitors are used for CML, and at least one older option is available as a lower-cost generic. The right choice depends on disease phase, prior treatment, mutation status, and side-effect profile, so any switch should be decided with your oncologist.

I tell patients to start the copay or assistance paperwork as soon as Scemblix is prescribed, because specialty-pharmacy approvals and prior authorizations can delay the first fill. If you're on Medicare, budget around the $2,100 annual cap and ask about spreading payments over the year rather than abandoning a prescription at the counter. Because CML treatment is long term, revisit your coverage every open-enrollment season, since formulary and tier changes can shift your costs significantly.

Scemblix cost — frequently asked questions

How much is Scemblix with the copay card?

With commercial insurance, the Scemblix Co-Pay Plus offer can lower your cost to as little as $0 per prescription, with savings of up to $15,000/yr. After you reach the annual maximum, you pay your plan's usual cost-sharing.

Is there a generic or biosimilar for Scemblix?

No. Scemblix (asciminib) is currently available only as a brand-name drug. As a small-molecule pill, it would eventually have a generic, not a biosimilar, but none is available yet.

How much does Scemblix cost without insurance?

The list price is about ~$21,854/mo. Uninsured patients should look into the Novartis Patient Assistance Foundation, which may provide the drug at no cost to those who qualify.

Does Medicare cover Scemblix, and can I use the copay card?

Many Medicare Part D plans cover Scemblix, but cost varies by plan. Your 2026 out-of-pocket Part D spending is capped at $2,100. The Co-Pay Plus card cannot be used with Medicare or Medicaid, but you may apply to the Novartis Patient Assistance Foundation.

How do I enroll in the Scemblix Co-Pay Plus program?

Go to the official Scemblix savings & support page at https://us.scemblix.com/patient-support/savings-support to check eligibility and enroll. You must have commercial insurance; Medicare and Medicaid patients are not eligible.
