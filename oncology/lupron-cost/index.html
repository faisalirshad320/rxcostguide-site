Lupron Depot cost, coupon & copay card

Lupron Depot (leuprolide acetate) is a long-acting GnRH agonist injection used to lower testosterone in prostate cancer and estrogen in endometriosis and uterine fibroids.

The short answer

Lupron Depot's list price is about ~$1,855–13,216 per injection (dosing-interval dependent). With commercial insurance, you may pay as little as $10 per dose for endometriosis with the Lupron Depot Savings Card (commercial). Medicare patients cannot use the card. Because Lupron Depot is given in a clinic, Medicare usually covers it under Part B, so the $2,100 2026 Part D out-of-pocket cap often does not apply. Patients without adequate coverage can apply to myAbbVie Assist.

Lupron Depot is injected by a clinician at intervals that range from monthly to every 6 months, depending on the formulation and the condition being treated. Price depends heavily on the dosing interval: longer-acting doses cost more per injection but are given less often. Your actual out-of-pocket cost depends mostly on your insurance type and on whether the drug is billed as a medical (clinic-administered) or pharmacy benefit.

The Lupron Depot copay card

The Lupron Depot Savings Card can sharply lower out-of-pocket costs for commercially insured patients.

Eligible patients with commercial insurance may pay as little as $10 per dose for endometriosis with the Lupron Depot Savings Card (commercial). Program terms apply, and the card cannot be used with Medicare, Medicaid, or other government insurance. Patients using Lupron Depot for other conditions should check the official savings page for the terms that apply to their indication.

Lupron Depot cost with Medicare

Lupron Depot is not on the list of drugs with a Medicare-negotiated price. Because a clinician administers it, Medicare usually covers it under Part B, where your share depends on your supplemental or Medicare Advantage coverage. If your plan covers it under Part D instead, your 2026 out-of-pocket spending on covered drugs is capped at $2,100. Federal rules bar manufacturer copay cards for Medicare patients, so the Lupron Depot Savings Card cannot be used.

Cash-pay strategies for Lupron Depot

Without insurance, Lupron Depot costs about ~$1,855–13,216 per injection (dosing-interval dependent). Pharmacy discount cards typically help little with clinic-administered injections, so myAbbVie Assist is usually the best option for uninsured patients. Ask your doctor whether a different leuprolide formulation or another hormone therapy would cost less in your situation.

Cheaper alternatives to Lupron Depot

Lupron Depot is brand-only and has no biosimilar. Other leuprolide formulations and other hormone-suppressing therapies (GnRH agonists or antagonists) exist, and your doctor can tell you whether one suits your condition and coverage.

I tell patients to confirm before the first injection whether their plan bills Lupron Depot as a medical or a pharmacy benefit, because that determines both the price and which savings options apply. I also remind them that a longer dosing interval costs more per shot but means fewer visits, so it is worth comparing total yearly cost with their insurer. If they are uninsured or on Medicare and struggling with costs, I point them to myAbbVie Assist early rather than waiting until a dose is due.

Lupron Depot cost — frequently asked questions

How much is Lupron Depot with the savings card?

Eligible patients with commercial insurance may pay as little as $10 per dose for endometriosis with the Lupron Depot Savings Card (commercial). Program terms apply.

Is there a generic or biosimilar for Lupron Depot?

There is no biosimilar for Lupron Depot. Other leuprolide formulations exist, so ask your doctor whether one is appropriate and covered by your plan.

How much does Lupron Depot cost without insurance?

The list price is about ~$1,855–13,216 per injection (dosing-interval dependent). Longer-acting doses cost more per injection. Uninsured patients can check eligibility for myAbbVie Assist.

Does Medicare cover Lupron Depot, and can I use the copay card?

Medicare typically covers Lupron Depot under Part B because it is given in a clinic. Your cost depends on your plan. If it is covered under Part D, the 2026 out-of-pocket cap is $2,100. The savings card is for commercial insurance only and cannot be used with Medicare or Medicaid.

How do I enroll in the Lupron Depot Savings Card?

Enroll through the official savings and support page at https://www.luprongyn.com/savings-and-support. You must have commercial insurance, not Medicare or Medicaid.
