Xtandi cost, coupon & copay card

Xtandi (enzalutamide) is an oral androgen receptor inhibitor prescribed for men with prostate cancer.

The short answer

Xtandi has a list price of about ~$15,000/mo, but commercially insured patients may pay as little as $0 per month with the Xtandi Patient Savings Program, which covers up to $7,000/yr. On Medicare Part D, the copay card cannot be used, but 2026 out-of-pocket drug costs are capped at $2,100 for the year. Uninsured or underinsured patients can apply to Astellas Pharma Support Solutions for help.

Enzalutamide is a daily oral pill that blocks androgen receptor signaling, and it is used across several stages of prostate cancer, often for long periods alongside hormone therapy. Because treatment can continue for months to years, the high monthly list price adds up quickly. What you actually pay depends mostly on your insurance type, your plan's specialty-tier cost sharing, and whether you qualify for manufacturer support.

The Xtandi copay card

Commercially insured patients may pay as little as $0 per month with the Xtandi Patient Savings Program.

The Xtandi Patient Savings Program helps cover out-of-pocket costs for patients with commercial (private or employer) insurance, with assistance up to $7,000/yr. Once the annual maximum is reached, you are responsible for your plan's remaining cost sharing. The program is not available to patients with Medicare, Medicaid, or other government insurance.

Xtandi cost with Medicare

Xtandi is not currently on the list of drugs with a Medicare negotiated price. It is typically covered under Medicare Part D, where your cost depends on your plan's formulary and cost-sharing tier. In 2026, Part D out-of-pocket drug spending is capped at $2,100 for the year, which limits total costs for high-priced drugs like Xtandi. Federal rules bar Medicare beneficiaries from using manufacturer copay cards, but you may still apply for patient assistance or Extra Help (Low-Income Subsidy) if eligible.

Patient assistance for Xtandi

Astellas Pharma Support Solutions offers benefits verification, help navigating insurance, and assistance options for eligible patients who are uninsured or cannot afford their medication. Eligibility criteria apply, and your care team can help you complete the application.

Cash-pay strategies for Xtandi

Without insurance, Xtandi lists at about ~$15,000/mo, so paying full cash price is rarely practical. Discount cards may lower the pharmacy price somewhat, but the most meaningful help usually comes from applying to Astellas Pharma Support Solutions. A generic enzalutamide is FDA-approved but not widely available, so ask your pharmacist whether it can be sourced.

Cheaper alternatives to Xtandi

Other androgen receptor inhibitors and hormone-based therapies are used in prostate cancer, and the best choice depends on disease stage, other medical conditions, and drug interactions. Talk with your oncologist before switching, since these drugs are not always interchangeable.

I tell patients to involve their oncology pharmacy team before the first fill, because benefits verification and savings enrollment can prevent a surprise bill at the counter. For commercially insured patients, I remind them the savings program has an annual maximum, so it is worth planning for costs later in the year. For Medicare patients, I point out that the 2026 Part D cap means their yearly out-of-pocket drug spending has a ceiling, and assistance programs may still help.

Xtandi cost — frequently asked questions

How much does Xtandi cost with the copay card?

Commercially insured patients may pay as little as $0 per month with the Xtandi Patient Savings Program, with assistance up to $7,000/yr. The program is for commercial insurance only — not Medicare or Medicaid.

Is there a generic version of Xtandi?

A generic enzalutamide is FDA-approved but not widely available. Ask your pharmacist whether it can be obtained and whether your plan covers it.

How much does Xtandi cost without insurance?

The list price of Xtandi is about ~$15,000/mo. Uninsured patients should consider applying to Astellas Pharma Support Solutions, which may provide medication at reduced or no cost to eligible patients.

Does Medicare cover Xtandi, and what will I pay?

Xtandi is generally covered under Medicare Part D, and your cost varies by plan. In 2026, Part D out-of-pocket drug costs are capped at $2,100 for the year. Medicare patients cannot use the manufacturer copay card but may qualify for patient assistance or Extra Help.

How do I enroll in the Xtandi Patient Savings Program?

You can enroll through the official Xtandi financial support page at https://www.xtandi.com/financial-support. You must have commercial insurance; Medicare and Medicaid patients are not eligible.
