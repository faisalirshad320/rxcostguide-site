Orgovyx cost, coupon & copay card

Orgovyx (relugolix) is a once-daily oral GnRH receptor antagonist that lowers testosterone in adults with advanced prostate cancer.

The short answer

Orgovyx has a list price of about ~$2,787/mo before insurance. With commercial insurance, the Orgovyx Copay Assistance Program can bring your cost down to as little as $10 per month (not $0), with a maximum benefit of up to $10,000/yr, 12 uses. On Medicare Part D, the copay card cannot be used, but your out-of-pocket drug costs are capped at $2,100 for 2026.

Orgovyx is taken by mouth once a day as androgen deprivation therapy, offering an alternative to injectable hormone treatments for advanced prostate cancer. Because it is a branded specialty medication, the list price is high, and what you actually pay depends mostly on your insurance type, your plan's formulary tier, and whether you qualify for manufacturer support.

The Orgovyx copay card

Commercially insured patients may pay as little as $10 per month with the Orgovyx Copay Assistance Program (not $0).

The copay program covers much of your out-of-pocket cost for Orgovyx after insurance, up to an annual maximum. It is available only to patients with commercial (private or employer) insurance. People with Medicare, Medicaid, or other government coverage are not eligible. Enroll through the official Orgovyx cost and support page before your first fill if possible.

Orgovyx cost with Medicare

Orgovyx is not among the drugs with a Medicare negotiated price. Coverage and cost-sharing vary by Part D plan, but in 2026 your total out-of-pocket spending on covered Part D drugs is capped at $2,100 for the year. Federal rules bar manufacturer copay cards for Medicare beneficiaries, so the Orgovyx Copay Assistance Program cannot be used. Ask about the Medicare Prescription Payment Plan to spread costs across the year, and contact the Orgovyx Support Program to see whether other help is available.

Patient assistance for Orgovyx

The Orgovyx Support Program helps patients with insurance questions, benefits verification, and financial assistance options. If you are uninsured, underinsured, or on Medicare and struggling with costs, contact the program to learn what you may qualify for. Eligibility criteria are set by the program.

Cash-pay strategies for Orgovyx

Without insurance, Orgovyx costs about ~$2,787/mo at list price. Pharmacy discount cards may reduce the cash price somewhat, but savings on specialty brand drugs are usually limited. Your best next step is to contact the Orgovyx Support Program about assistance and to ask your oncologist or urologist whether another form of hormone therapy might cost less under your situation.

Cheaper alternatives to Orgovyx

Other androgen deprivation options for advanced prostate cancer include injectable GnRH agonists and injectable GnRH antagonists, which may be covered differently by your plan. Your oncologist or urologist can help weigh effectiveness, side effects, and cost when choosing among them.

I tell patients that Orgovyx works only if it is taken every day, so a cost barrier that causes missed doses is a clinical problem, not just a financial one. If you have commercial insurance, I encourage you to enroll in the copay program before your first fill, and remember it is $10 a month, not free. If you are on Medicare, the $2,100 annual cap in 2026 makes costs more predictable, and the support program is worth a call.

Orgovyx cost — frequently asked questions

How much does Orgovyx cost with the copay card?

With commercial insurance, you may pay as little as $10 per month with the Orgovyx Copay Assistance Program. It is not $0. The program covers up to $10,000/yr, 12 uses.

Is there a generic or biosimilar for Orgovyx?

No. Relugolix is a small-molecule drug, so biosimilars do not apply, and there is no FDA-approved generic relugolix available in the US at this time. Orgovyx is sold only as a brand-name product.

How much is Orgovyx without insurance?

The list price is about ~$2,787/mo. Discount cards may lower the cash price slightly. Contact the Orgovyx Support Program to ask about financial assistance.

Does Medicare cover Orgovyx, and can I use the copay card?

Many Medicare Part D plans cover Orgovyx, but costs vary by plan. Your 2026 out-of-pocket spending on covered Part D drugs is capped at $2,100. The manufacturer copay card cannot be used with Medicare or Medicaid.

How do I enroll in the Orgovyx copay program?

Visit the official Orgovyx cost and support page at https://www.orgovyx.com/cost-and-support/#commercial to enroll. You must have commercial insurance. Your pharmacy or the Orgovyx Support Program can also help you get started.
