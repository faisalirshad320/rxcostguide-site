Xywav cost, coupon & copay card

Xywav (calcium, magnesium, potassium, and sodium oxybates) is a prescription oxybate oral solution used to treat cataplexy and excessive daytime sleepiness in narcolepsy, as well as idiopathic hypersomnia.

The short answer

Xywav has a list price of about ~$7,000 per 180 mL, so cost without insurance is very high. If you have commercial insurance, the Xywav JazzCares Coupon Program can lower your cost to as little as $0 per prescription (monthly and annual limits apply). With Medicare Part D, the copay program can't be used, but your out-of-pocket drug costs are capped at $2,100 in 2026.

Xywav is taken as a liquid at bedtime, with a second dose later in the night for many patients, and it must be prescribed and dispensed through a REMS program because it is a potent CNS depressant. Its cost is driven by a high brand-name list price, ongoing nightly use, and dose size, since higher doses mean more solution per month. What you actually pay depends heavily on your insurance type, plan formulary, and whether you qualify for manufacturer support.

The Xywav copay card

Commercially insured patients may pay as little as $0 per prescription with the Xywav JazzCares Coupon Program.

The JazzCares coupon helps cover out-of-pocket costs for eligible patients whose commercial insurance covers Xywav. Monthly and annual limits apply, so if your plan has a high deductible or coinsurance you may eventually owe a share once the program limits are reached. The program is not available to patients with Medicare, Medicaid, or other government insurance.

Xywav cost with Medicare

Xywav is not among the drugs with a Medicare-negotiated price. Coverage and cost-sharing vary by Part D plan, and federal rules bar the use of manufacturer copay coupons with Medicare. However, the 2026 Part D out-of-pocket cap of $2,100 limits how much you pay for covered drugs in a year, and the Medicare Prescription Payment Plan can spread those costs into monthly payments.

Patient assistance for Xywav

JazzCares is Jazz Pharmaceuticals' patient support program for Xywav. It can help with insurance navigation, coverage questions, and financial support options, which may include help for patients who are uninsured or underinsured. Eligibility requirements apply, so review the current criteria on the JazzCares website or ask your prescriber's office to help you apply.

Cash-pay strategies for Xywav

Paying cash for Xywav is expensive given its list price of ~$7,000 per 180 mL, and pharmacy discount cards typically have limited value for specialty REMS medications dispensed through a certified pharmacy. If you are uninsured, contact JazzCares to ask about financial assistance before filling. Your prescriber may also discuss whether a different, lower-cost treatment is appropriate for your condition.

Cheaper alternatives to Xywav

Other treatment options for narcolepsy and idiopathic hypersomnia include other oxybate formulations and non-oxybate wake-promoting or anticataplectic medications, which may differ in cost and insurance coverage. Talk with your sleep specialist about whether an alternative fits your clinical needs and budget.

I tell patients that the price on paper for Xywav looks alarming, but very few commercially insured people end up paying anything close to list price once JazzCares is involved. Because Xywav requires REMS enrollment and ships from a certified pharmacy, I recommend starting the insurance and coupon paperwork as soon as it is prescribed to avoid gaps. For my Medicare patients, I point out that the $2,100 annual cap makes the cost far more predictable, even though the coupon can't be used.

Xywav cost — frequently asked questions

How much does Xywav cost with the copay card?

With commercial insurance, the Xywav JazzCares Coupon Program can bring your cost down to as little as $0 per prescription. Monthly and annual limits apply.

Is there a generic or biosimilar for Xywav?

Xywav is a brand-name product made by Jazz Pharmaceuticals. It is not a biologic, so biosimilars do not apply. Ask your pharmacist or prescriber about current lower-cost options.

How much is Xywav without insurance?

The list price of Xywav is about ~$7,000 per 180 mL. Your actual cost depends on your prescribed dose, and uninsured patients can contact JazzCares to ask about financial assistance.

Does Medicare cover Xywav, and can I use the coupon?

Coverage depends on your Part D plan, and Xywav does not have a Medicare-negotiated price. Manufacturer copay coupons cannot be used with Medicare, but your out-of-pocket costs for covered drugs are capped at $2,100 in 2026.

How do I enroll in the Xywav JazzCares program?

You can enroll through JazzCares at https://www.jazzcares.com/patients/xywav. The coupon is for patients with commercial insurance only, not Medicare or Medicaid, and Xywav also requires enrollment in a REMS program.
