Xcopri cost, coupon & copay card

Xcopri (cenobamate) is a brand-name antiseizure medication from SK Life Science used to treat partial-onset (focal) seizures in adults.

The short answer

Xcopri has a list price of about ~$1,230/mo, and there is no manufacturer copay card. Eligible new patients can get a one-time 28-day free trial, and SK Life Science Navigator helps uninsured or underinsured patients find ways to afford it. On Medicare Part D, your total out-of-pocket drug costs are capped at $2,100 in 2026.

Xcopri is taken by mouth as an add-on or standalone treatment for focal seizures in adults, and it is started at a low dose and increased gradually over several weeks. Because it is a brand-only medication, the list price is high, and what you actually pay depends heavily on your insurance plan's formulary tier, deductible, and any prior authorization requirements.

The Xcopri copay card

There is no Xcopri copay card.

SK Life Science does not offer a manufacturer copay card for Xcopri, so be cautious of any site advertising a $0 Xcopri copay card. Instead, a one-time 28-day free trial is offered to eligible new patients, and SK Life Science Navigator helps uninsured or underinsured patients. The free trial offer is for commercially insured patients only and is not available to people with Medicare or Medicaid.

Xcopri cost with Medicare

Xcopri is not among the drugs with a Medicare-negotiated price. Coverage and cost-sharing vary by Part D plan, but in 2026 your total out-of-pocket spending on covered Part D drugs is capped at $2,100 for the year. Federal rules bar manufacturer copay cards and similar offers for Medicare beneficiaries, so the Xcopri free trial cannot be used with Medicare; SK Life Science Navigator may still be worth contacting about other options.

Patient assistance for Xcopri

SK Life Science Navigator is the manufacturer's support program for patients who are uninsured or underinsured. It can help you understand your coverage, work through access barriers, and identify ways to lower your cost for Xcopri. Contact the program directly to check what you may qualify for.

Cash-pay strategies for Xcopri

Without insurance, Xcopri's list price is about ~$1,230/mo, and pharmacy discount cards may lower the cash price somewhat, so compare prices between pharmacies. Uninsured patients should contact SK Life Science Navigator, and eligible new patients may ask about the one-time 28-day free trial. If cost remains a barrier, ask your neurologist whether a lower-cost antiseizure medication is appropriate for you.

Cheaper alternatives to Xcopri

Xcopri has no generic or biosimilar version available in the US. Several other antiseizure medications are used for focal seizures, and many are available as lower-cost generics, so talk with your neurologist about whether one could be an appropriate option.

I tell patients starting Xcopri to ask about the free trial before the first fill, because the dose is increased slowly and it takes time to see whether it works for them. I also encourage them to have the prescriber's office start any prior authorization early, since there is no copay card to absorb a high commercial copay. Most importantly, I remind them never to stop an antiseizure medication abruptly because of cost—call the office first so we can find a safe plan.

Xcopri cost — frequently asked questions

How much does Xcopri cost with a copay card?

There is no manufacturer copay card for Xcopri. SK Life Science offers a one-time 28-day free trial to eligible new patients with commercial insurance, and SK Life Science Navigator helps uninsured or underinsured patients. Beyond that, your cost depends on your insurance plan.

Is there a generic or biosimilar for Xcopri?

No. Xcopri (cenobamate) is available only as a brand-name product in the US, with no generic or biosimilar version. Ask your doctor whether a different, generic antiseizure medication could be suitable if cost is a concern.

How much is Xcopri without insurance?

The list price of Xcopri is about ~$1,230/mo. Actual cash prices vary by pharmacy, and discount cards may help. Uninsured patients should contact SK Life Science Navigator at https://www.sklsinavigator.com/ for help.

Does Medicare cover Xcopri?

Many Medicare Part D plans may cover Xcopri, but cost-sharing varies by plan. In 2026, Part D out-of-pocket drug costs are capped at $2,100 per year. Xcopri does not have a Medicare-negotiated price, and the free trial offer cannot be used by Medicare or Medicaid patients.

How do I sign up for Xcopri savings?

Visit the official Xcopri resources page at https://www.xcopri.com/epilepsy-resources/ to learn about the one-time 28-day free trial for eligible new, commercially insured patients. If you are uninsured or underinsured, contact SK Life Science Navigator at https://www.sklsinavigator.com/.
