Vumerity cost, coupon & copay card

Vumerity (diroximel fumarate) is a twice-daily oral fumarate from Biogen used to treat adults with relapsing forms of multiple sclerosis.

The short answer

Vumerity has a list price of about ~$9,400/mo, but commercially insured patients may pay as little as $0 with the Vumerity Biogen Copay Program (annual limits apply). People with Medicare Part D cannot use the copay card, but their out-of-pocket drug costs are capped at $2,100 in 2026. Uninsured or underinsured patients can apply to Biogen Support Services for help.

Vumerity is taken by mouth twice daily as a long-term disease-modifying therapy to reduce relapses in relapsing MS. Because it is a brand-name specialty drug with a high list price and is taken continuously, what you actually pay depends mostly on your insurance type, your plan's specialty tier, and whether you qualify for the manufacturer copay program or patient assistance. A generic (diroximel fumarate) is FDA-approved but not widely available, so most patients still receive the brand.

The Vumerity copay card

Commercially insured patients may pay as little as $0 with the Vumerity Biogen Copay Program.

The Vumerity Biogen Copay Program helps cover out-of-pocket costs for patients whose commercial (private or employer) insurance covers Vumerity. Annual limits apply, so very high deductibles or coinsurance may not be fully covered for the whole year. The program cannot be used by anyone with Medicare, Medicaid, or other government insurance.

Vumerity cost with Medicare

Vumerity is not among the drugs with a Medicare-negotiated (maximum fair) price. Coverage and cost-sharing vary by Part D plan, but in 2026 total out-of-pocket spending on covered Part D drugs is capped at $2,100 per year, which limits annual costs for a high-priced specialty drug like this. Federal rules bar Medicare beneficiaries from using manufacturer copay cards, so Medicare patients who still cannot afford their share should look into Biogen Support Services or Medicare's Extra Help program.

Patient assistance for Vumerity

Biogen Support Services offers access and affordability help for patients prescribed Vumerity, including benefits investigation and support for those who are uninsured or underinsured. Eligibility criteria are set by Biogen; you and your prescriber can start an application through the program website.

Cash-pay strategies for Vumerity

Without insurance, Vumerity lists at about ~$9,400/mo, and pharmacy discount cards typically take only a modest amount off specialty-brand prices like this. The most meaningful option for uninsured patients is usually applying to Biogen Support Services. A generic diroximel fumarate is FDA-approved but not widely available, so ask your pharmacist whether it can be sourced, and ask your neurologist whether a lower-cost oral MS therapy would be appropriate.

Cheaper alternatives to Vumerity

Other disease-modifying therapies for relapsing MS, including other oral fumarates and medications from different drug classes, may be options depending on your insurance formulary and clinical history. Your neurologist can help weigh cost against efficacy, safety monitoring, and tolerability.

I tell patients to settle insurance and copay enrollment before the first fill, because specialty pharmacy and prior authorization delays are common with MS drugs. I also remind them that fumarates require periodic blood count monitoring, so keeping lab appointments matters as much as keeping the prescription affordable. If someone moves onto Medicare, I warn them early that their copay card will stop working, so we can plan around the Part D cap or assistance programs.

Vumerity cost — frequently asked questions

How much does Vumerity cost with the copay card?

Commercially insured patients may pay as little as $0 with the Vumerity Biogen Copay Program. Annual limits apply, so coverage may not extend to every dollar of out-of-pocket cost over the year.

Is there a generic or biosimilar for Vumerity?

A generic (diroximel fumarate) is FDA-approved but not widely available, so most patients still fill the brand. Vumerity is a small-molecule drug, so no biosimilar applies.

How much is Vumerity without insurance?

The list price is about ~$9,400/mo. Uninsured patients should contact Biogen Support Services, which may provide the drug at reduced or no cost to those who qualify.

Does Medicare cover Vumerity, and can I use the copay card?

Many Part D plans cover Vumerity, though costs vary by plan. The copay card cannot be used with Medicare or Medicaid, but in 2026 your Part D out-of-pocket costs are capped at $2,100 for the year. Vumerity is not on the Medicare negotiated-price list.

How do I enroll in the Vumerity copay program?

Enroll online at https://biogencopayprogram.com/Home/Enrollment?br=vumerity. You must have commercial insurance; Medicare and Medicaid patients are not eligible but can explore Biogen Support Services instead.
