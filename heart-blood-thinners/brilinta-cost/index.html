Brilinta cost & generic ticagrelor

Brilinta (ticagrelor) is a twice-daily antiplatelet used after a heart attack, stent or acute coronary syndrome — and it is now available as a cheaper generic.

The short answer

Brand Brilinta lists around $550 a month, but generic ticagrelor is now available and is usually the cheapest route — often well under $100 a month with a discount card. Commercially insured patients may also use the Brilinta savings card; Medicare patients pay plan cost-share under the $2,100 cap.

Brilinta is usually prescribed for a defined period (often 12 months) after a cardiac event, alongside aspirin. The arrival of generic ticagrelor is the key cost development: it undercuts the brand substantially and is widely interchangeable.

The Brilinta copay card

The Brilinta savings card can lower brand cost for commercially insured patients, but generic ticagrelor is usually cheaper.

The brand card is for commercial insurance only and cannot be used with Medicare or Medicaid. Before using it, ask the pharmacist to price generic ticagrelor — for most patients the generic wins.

Brilinta cost with Medicare

Brilinta has no Medicare-negotiated price, but generic ticagrelor sits on low-cost tiers of most Part D plans, so Medicare patients usually pay little. The $2,100 annual out-of-pocket cap applies on top.

Patient assistance for Brilinta

AstraZeneca provides brand Brilinta free to uninsured, income-eligible patients through AZ&Me, though for most people generic ticagrelor with a discount card is already inexpensive.

Cash-pay strategies for Brilinta

Ask the pharmacist for generic ticagrelor and price it with GoodRx or SingleCare — it is far cheaper than brand Brilinta. Do not stop an antiplatelet early to save money after a stent or heart attack; stopping prematurely can cause a dangerous clot. If cost is a barrier, tell your cardiologist rather than skipping doses.

Cheaper alternatives to Brilinta

Generic clopidogrel (Plavix) is an older, very inexpensive antiplatelet used for similar indications, though not identical clinically. Prasugrel (Effient) is another option, also generic. Which antiplatelet and for how long is strictly a cardiology decision — never switch or stop on your own.

Brilinta is one where I am emphatic that cost must never drive stopping the drug early. After a stent, the antiplatelet period is protecting you from a clot in that stent, and stopping a few months early to save money is a genuinely dangerous trade. The good news is that generic ticagrelor has made that trade-off largely unnecessary — and where it has not, generic clopidogrel is a cheap alternative the cardiologist can consider.

Brilinta cost — frequently asked questions

Is there a generic for Brilinta?

Yes — generic ticagrelor is available and usually much cheaper than brand Brilinta, often under $100 a month with a discount card. Ask your pharmacist.

How much is generic ticagrelor?

It varies by pharmacy and discount card but is generally well below the ~$550 brand list price. Price it with GoodRx or SingleCare.

Can I stop Brilinta to save money?

No — not without your cardiologist. Stopping an antiplatelet early after a stent or heart attack can cause a dangerous clot. If cost is a problem, ask about generic ticagrelor or clopidogrel instead of skipping doses.

What does Brilinta cost without insurance?

Brand Brilinta is about $550 a month; generic ticagrelor is far cheaper. Income-eligible uninsured patients can also apply to AZ&Me for free brand.

Is Brilinta better than clopidogrel?

Brilinta (ticagrelor) is more potent and taken twice daily; clopidogrel (Plavix) is cheaper and once daily. Which is right depends on your situation and is a cardiology decision.
