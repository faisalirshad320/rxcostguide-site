Camzyos cost, coupon & copay card

Camzyos (mavacamten) is an oral cardiac myosin inhibitor used to treat adults with symptomatic obstructive hypertrophic cardiomyopathy.

The short answer

Camzyos has a list price of about ~$8,537/mo before insurance. With commercial insurance, the Camzyos Co-pay Program can lower your cost to $10 per monthly prescription (not $0), subject to monthly and annual limits. On Medicare Part D, the copay card cannot be used, but your 2026 out-of-pocket drug costs are capped at $2,100 for the year.

Camzyos is taken by mouth once daily and works by reducing excessive heart muscle contraction, which can ease the outflow obstruction that causes symptoms in obstructive hypertrophic cardiomyopathy. Because it can weaken the heart's pumping function, it is available only through a REMS program that requires prescriber and pharmacy certification, patient enrollment, and regular echocardiogram monitoring. What you pay depends mainly on your insurance type, your plan's specialty-drug tier, and whether you qualify for the manufacturer's copay or support programs.

The Camzyos copay card

With commercial insurance, the Camzyos Co-pay Program can bring your cost down to $10 per monthly prescription.

The Camzyos Co-pay Program is for patients with commercial (private or employer) insurance. Eligible patients pay $10 per monthly prescription with the Camzyos Co-pay Program (not $0), and monthly and annual limits apply. The program cannot be used by patients with Medicare, Medicaid, or other government insurance. You can activate the program online through the official Camzyos website.

Camzyos cost with Medicare

Camzyos has not been selected for Medicare drug price negotiation, so there is no Maximum Fair Price. It may be covered under Medicare Part D, with costs that vary by plan and formulary tier. In 2026, Part D out-of-pocket spending on covered drugs is capped at $2,100 per year, which can substantially limit costs for a high-priced specialty drug like Camzyos. Federal rules bar Medicare beneficiaries from using manufacturer copay cards, including the Camzyos Co-pay Program.

Patient assistance for Camzyos

The MyCAMZYOS Patient Support Program helps patients navigate enrollment, insurance coverage, and access to treatment. If you are uninsured, underinsured, or have trouble affording Camzyos, contact the program to ask what support options you may qualify for. Eligibility and available help vary by individual circumstances.

Cash-pay strategies for Camzyos

Without insurance, Camzyos is expensive, with a list price of about ~$8,537/mo, and because it is dispensed only through REMS-certified specialty pharmacies, retail discount cards are unlikely to help much. Your best first step is to contact the MyCAMZYOS Patient Support Program about assistance. Ask your cardiologist whether older, generic treatment options might be appropriate if cost is a barrier.

Cheaper alternatives to Camzyos

There is no generic or biosimilar version of Camzyos. Other treatments for obstructive hypertrophic cardiomyopathy include generic beta blockers, non-dihydropyridine calcium channel blockers, and disopyramide, as well as septal reduction procedures for selected patients; your cardiologist can advise which options fit your situation.

I tell patients to activate the copay program or call the support program before their first fill, because specialty pharmacy and REMS paperwork can delay a start if coverage isn't sorted out early. I also remind them that the required echocardiograms are part of safe use, not optional extras, so it is worth confirming that those visits are covered as well. For Medicare patients, I point out that the 2026 Part D cap means costs become predictable for the rest of the year once the cap is reached.

Camzyos cost — frequently asked questions

How much does Camzyos cost with the copay card?

Eligible patients with commercial insurance pay $10 per monthly prescription with the Camzyos Co-pay Program (not $0). Monthly and annual limits apply.

Is there a generic or biosimilar for Camzyos?

No. There is currently no generic version of mavacamten and no biosimilar available, so Camzyos is available only as the brand-name product.

How much is Camzyos without insurance?

The list price of Camzyos is about ~$8,537/mo, and actual pharmacy cash prices may vary. If you are uninsured, contact the MyCAMZYOS Patient Support Program to ask about assistance.

Does Medicare cover Camzyos, and can I use the copay card?

Camzyos may be covered under Medicare Part D, with costs that vary by plan, and in 2026 your Part D out-of-pocket costs are capped at $2,100 per year. The Camzyos Co-pay Program cannot be used with Medicare or Medicaid. Camzyos has not been selected for Medicare price negotiation.

How do I enroll in the Camzyos copay program?

You can activate the Camzyos Co-pay Program online at https://www.camzyos.com/support/mycamzyos-patient-support-sign-up/activate-co-pay if you have commercial insurance. Because Camzyos requires enrollment in a REMS program, you will also need to be enrolled through your prescriber before treatment begins.
