Entresto cost, coupon & copay card

Entresto (sacubitril/valsartan) is a cornerstone heart-failure medication — and one of the first ten drugs to receive a Medicare-negotiated price in 2026.

The short answer

Entresto costs about $600 a month at list price. Commercially insured patients can pay as little as $10 a month with the Entresto copay card. From 1 January 2026 the Medicare-negotiated price is about $295 per 30-day supply, applied automatically for Part D members.

Entresto reduces the risk of hospitalization and death in chronic heart failure and is taken indefinitely, so annual cost matters. As with the anticoagulants on this page, your insurance type decides the cheapest route.

Entresto cost with Medicare

Entresto was one of the first ten Medicare-negotiated drugs. From 1 January 2026 its Maximum Fair Price is about $295 per 30-day supply, down from roughly $628. The price applies automatically at the pharmacy for Part D members, and the 2026 Part D out-of-pocket cap of $2,100 also applies.

Patient assistance for Entresto

Novartis provides Entresto free to uninsured patients who meet income limits through its patient-assistance foundation. The application requires proof of income and residency.

Cash-pay strategies for Entresto

Compare discount-card prices against the pharmacy cash price and request a 90-day supply. A generic sacubitril/valsartan is beginning to reach the US market; ask your pharmacist whether it is available, as it will be the cheapest route once widely stocked.

Heart-failure patients are often on several medications at once, so the Part D cap is a bigger deal for them than for most — it caps the *combined* out-of-pocket total. When I counsel patients juggling multiple prescriptions, I point them to that cap first, because it changes the whole yearly picture, not just what they pay for Entresto.

Entresto cost — frequently asked questions

How much does Entresto cost with Medicare in 2026?

From 1 January 2026 the negotiated price is about $295 per 30-day supply, applied automatically at the pharmacy. Your total Part D out-of-pocket spending is also capped at $2,100 for the year.

Is there a generic for Entresto?

A generic sacubitril/valsartan has begun entering the US market. Availability varies by pharmacy; ask your pharmacist, as the generic is the cheapest option once stocked.

Can I use the Entresto copay card with Medicare?

No. Like all manufacturer copay cards it is limited to commercial insurance and cannot be used with Medicare or Medicaid.

What is the cheapest way to get Entresto?

Commercially insured: the copay card (~$10/mo). Medicare: the negotiated price plus the Part D cap. Uninsured: Novartis patient assistance if income-eligible, or a discount card plus a 90-day supply.

Why is Entresto prescribed instead of a cheaper ACE inhibitor?

Entresto has been shown to reduce heart-failure hospitalizations and deaths more than older ACE inhibitors in key trials, which is why guidelines favor it despite the higher cost. Whether it is right for you is a clinical decision for your cardiologist.
