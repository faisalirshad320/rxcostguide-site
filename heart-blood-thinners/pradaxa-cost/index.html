Pradaxa cost, coupon & generic options

Pradaxa (dabigatran) is a direct thrombin inhibitor used to prevent strokes in atrial fibrillation and to treat blood clots.

Manufacturer copay cardPatient assistance

The short answer

Branded Pradaxa costs about $550 a month at list price, but generic dabigatran is available and much cheaper — often well under $100 a month with a discount card. Commercially insured patients can also use the Pradaxa savings card.

Pradaxa cost with Medicare

Pradaxa is not on the Medicare negotiated-price list. Medicare patients pay their plan cost-share up to the $2,100 Part D cap — and for many, generic dabigatran on a Part D plan is inexpensive. Copay cards cannot be used with Medicare.

Patient assistance for Pradaxa

Boehringer Ingelheim provides Pradaxa to uninsured, income-eligible patients through its patient-assistance foundation.

Cash-pay strategies for Pradaxa

The single biggest lever here is the generic: ask your pharmacist for generic dabigatran and price it with GoodRx or SingleCare — it is usually a fraction of the brand. Request a 90-day supply to lower per-fill costs further.

Cheaper alternatives to Pradaxa

Eliquis and Xarelto are the more commonly prescribed Factor Xa inhibitors and both have 2026 Medicare-negotiated prices. Warfarin remains the cheapest option but requires monitoring. The choice among these is clinical.

Pradaxa is a good reminder that a generic changes everything. Patients sometimes keep paying brand prices out of habit when an equivalent generic is sitting on the same shelf. Always ask the pharmacist the plain question: "is there a generic, and is it cheaper for me?"

Pradaxa cost — frequently asked questions

Is there a generic for Pradaxa?

Yes. Generic dabigatran is available in the US and is usually much cheaper than branded Pradaxa — often under $100 a month with a discount card.

How much is Pradaxa without insurance?

Branded Pradaxa is about $550 a month at list price, but generic dabigatran without insurance is far less. Ask your pharmacist for the generic and compare discount-card prices.

Can I use the Pradaxa savings card with Medicare?

No. The card is for commercial insurance only. Medicare patients rely on their Part D coverage and the $2,100 annual cap.

Is generic dabigatran as good as Pradaxa?

Generic dabigatran contains the same active ingredient and must meet the FDA's bioequivalence standards. Clinically it is considered equivalent; discuss any switch with your prescriber.

What is the cheapest blood thinner overall?

Warfarin is the cheapest at a few dollars a month but requires regular blood-test monitoring. Among the newer agents, generic dabigatran is currently the most affordable that does not need routine monitoring.
