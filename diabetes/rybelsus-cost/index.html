Rybelsus cost, coupon & savings card

Rybelsus (oral semaglutide) is the only GLP-1 pill for type 2 diabetes — the same molecule as Ozempic and Wegovy, taken by mouth once daily.

The short answer

Rybelsus costs about $1,000 a month at list price. Commercially insured patients can pay as little as $10 a month with the Rybelsus savings card (if covered). There is no generic. As a semaglutide product it is in the second Medicare negotiation cycle, with a negotiated price effective 2027.

Rybelsus cost with Medicare

Semaglutide — the molecule in Rybelsus, Ozempic and Wegovy — was selected in the second round of Medicare negotiation, with a Maximum Fair Price effective 1 January 2027. For 2026, Medicare patients pay plan cost-share under the $2,100 Part D cap. Part D covers semaglutide for diabetes, not weight loss.

Patient assistance for Rybelsus

Novo Nordisk provides Rybelsus free to uninsured, income-eligible patients through NovoCare / the Novo Nordisk Patient Assistance Program. Proof of income is required.

Novo Nordisk Patient Assistance Program

Cash-pay strategies for Rybelsus

There is no generic oral semaglutide, so cash payers should compare discount-card prices and ask about NovoCare self-pay options. Income-eligible uninsured patients can apply to the Novo Nordisk Patient Assistance Program. Take Rybelsus on an empty stomach with a small sip of water, 30 minutes before food or other pills, or absorption drops sharply.

Cheaper alternatives to Rybelsus

Injectable semaglutide (Ozempic) is the same molecule once weekly; Trulicity (dulaglutide) and Mounjaro (tirzepatide) are other GLP-1 options. See our Mounjaro vs Ozempic and Trulicity vs Ozempic comparisons. Switching is a clinical decision.

The detail I always stress with Rybelsus is how it must be taken: on an empty stomach, with no more than a sip of water, then nothing else for 30 minutes. Taken wrong, the body absorbs very little — so a patient paying $1,000 a month could be getting a fraction of the drug. For an expensive pill, dosing technique is part of the cost conversation.

Rybelsus cost — frequently asked questions

How much is Rybelsus with the savings card?

As little as $10 a month if your commercial plan covers it. The card cannot be used with Medicare or Medicaid.

Is Rybelsus the same as Ozempic?

Same molecule (semaglutide), different form — Rybelsus is a daily pill, Ozempic is a weekly injection. Rybelsus doses are not directly interchangeable with Ozempic doses.

Does Rybelsus get a Medicare-negotiated price?

Yes — semaglutide is in the second negotiation cycle, with a price effective 2027. For 2026, Medicare patients pay plan cost-share under the $2,100 cap.

What does Rybelsus cost without insurance?

About $1,000 a month at list price. Uninsured, income-eligible patients can get it free through the Novo Nordisk Patient Assistance Program.

How should Rybelsus be taken?

On an empty stomach with up to a sip of water, at least 30 minutes before any food, drink or other medication — otherwise much less is absorbed.
