Farxiga cost, coupon & copay card

Farxiga (dapagliflozin) is an SGLT2 inhibitor for type 2 diabetes, heart failure and chronic kidney disease — and one of the first ten Medicare-negotiated drugs.

The short answer

Farxiga costs about $600 a month at list price. Commercially insured patients can pay as little as $0 a month with the Farxiga savings card. From 1 January 2026 the Medicare-negotiated price is about $178.50 per 30-day supply.

Like other SGLT2 inhibitors, Farxiga is a long-term medication where the annual figure matters. Your insurance type decides the cheapest route, and the savings card is capped per fill — so 90-day supplies stretch it further.

Farxiga cost with Medicare

Farxiga was one of the first ten Medicare-negotiated drugs. From 1 January 2026 its Maximum Fair Price is about $178.50 per 30-day supply, down from roughly $556 — applied automatically at the pharmacy for Part D members, with the $2,100 Part D out-of-pocket cap on top.

Patient assistance for Farxiga

AstraZeneca provides Farxiga free to uninsured, income-eligible patients through the AZ&Me program. Proof of income is required.

Cash-pay strategies for Farxiga

Compare discount-card prices against the pharmacy cash price and ask for a 90-day supply. A generic dapagliflozin is beginning to reach the market; ask your pharmacist, as it will be the cheapest route once widely stocked.

Cheaper alternatives to Farxiga

Jardiance (empagliflozin) is the closest SGLT2 alternative — similar cost, also Medicare-negotiated in 2026. See our [Jardiance vs Farxiga comparison](/diabetes/jardiance-vs-farxiga/).

Farxiga and Jardiance are close enough that cost and coverage often decide between them. For a Medicare patient, Farxiga's negotiated price is slightly lower; for a commercial patient, the Farxiga card can reach $0. As always, confirm which your plan prefers before assuming — formulary placement can flip the maths.

Farxiga cost — frequently asked questions

How much is Farxiga with Medicare in 2026?

About $178.50 per 30-day supply — the negotiated price, applied automatically. The $2,100 Part D annual cap also applies.

Is Farxiga cheaper than Jardiance?

On the 2026 Medicare price, slightly — Farxiga ~$178.50 vs Jardiance ~$197. For commercial patients both cards can reach near $0, so the practical difference is small.

Is there a generic dapagliflozin?

A generic is beginning to reach the US market. Ask your pharmacist whether it is available, as it will be the cheapest option.

Can I use the Farxiga card on Medicare?

No — commercial insurance only. Medicare patients use the negotiated price and Part D cap.

What is the cheapest way to get Farxiga?

Commercial: the savings card (~$0). Medicare: the negotiated price plus the cap. Uninsured: AZ&Me patient assistance if income-eligible, or a discount card with a 90-day supply.
