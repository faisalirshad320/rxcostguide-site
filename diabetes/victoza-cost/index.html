Victoza cost & generic liraglutide

Victoza (liraglutide) is a once-daily GLP-1 injection for type 2 diabetes — and, unlike newer GLP-1s, it now has a cheaper generic (authorized) liraglutide.

The short answer

Branded Victoza lists around $820 a month. A cheaper authorized generic liraglutide is available and is usually the better-value route. Commercially insured patients may also use the Victoza savings card; Medicare patients pay plan cost-share under the $2,100 cap; the uninsured can apply to NovoCare.

The Victoza copay card

The Victoza savings card can lower cost for commercially insured patients, but the authorized generic may be cheaper.

The brand savings card is for commercial insurance only and cannot be used with Medicare or Medicaid. Before using it, ask your pharmacist to price the authorized generic liraglutide — for many patients it is the cheaper route.

Victoza cost with Medicare

Victoza has no Medicare-negotiated price. For 2026, Medicare patients pay their Part D plan cost-share, capped by the $2,100 annual out-of-pocket maximum. Where the authorized generic liraglutide is covered on a lower tier, a Part D plan may price it less.

Patient assistance for Victoza

Novo Nordisk provides Victoza free to uninsured, income-eligible patients through NovoCare. Proof of income is required.

Novo Nordisk Patient Assistance Program

Cash-pay strategies for Victoza

Ask the pharmacist for the authorized generic liraglutide and price it with GoodRx or SingleCare — it is usually cheaper than brand Victoza. Request a 90-day supply. Income-eligible uninsured patients can apply to NovoCare.

Cheaper alternatives to Victoza

Newer once-weekly GLP-1s (Ozempic, Trulicity) and the dual agonist Mounjaro are more convenient but mostly brand-only and pricier. For cost, generic liraglutide is attractive; for efficacy or dosing convenience, your prescriber may still prefer a newer agent.

Victoza quietly became one of the few GLP-1s with a generic, and many patients and even prescribers do not realise it. For someone paying cash or facing a high copay, asking specifically for authorized generic liraglutide is often the fastest saving in this whole category — though the once-daily injection is less convenient than the weekly options.

Victoza cost — frequently asked questions

Is there a generic for Victoza?

Yes — an authorized generic liraglutide is available and usually cheaper than brand Victoza. Ask your pharmacist for it.

How much is generic liraglutide?

It varies by pharmacy and discount card but is generally below the ~$820 brand list price. Price it with GoodRx or SingleCare.

What does Victoza cost without insurance?

Brand Victoza is about $820 a month; the authorized generic is cheaper. Uninsured, income-eligible patients can get it free through NovoCare.

Is Victoza as good as Ozempic?

They are both GLP-1s but different molecules and dosing (Victoza is daily, Ozempic weekly). Newer agents may lower blood sugar and weight more; which is right for you is a clinical decision.

Can I use the Victoza card on Medicare?

No — commercial insurance only. Medicare patients use their plan and the $2,100 Part D cap.
