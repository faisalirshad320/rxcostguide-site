Jardiance vs Farxiga: cost, coverage & which is right for you

Two closely related SGLT2 inhibitors, compared on what actually drives your out-of-pocket cost in 2026.

Reviewed by Dr. Faisal Irshad, MBBS, M.Phil Chemical Pathology PMDC 54440-SUpdated 2026-10-01
The short answer

At list price they are close — Jardiance about $640/month, Farxiga about $600/month. On Medicare, Farxiga's 2026 negotiated price (~$178.50) is a little lower than Jardiance's (~$197). For commercially insured patients, both cards can reach near $0–$10, so coverage placement on your plan usually decides it, not the sticker price.

When Jardiance tends to be the choice

Jardiance is often chosen on the strength of its specific cardiovascular and kidney trial evidence and its familiarity to prescribers. Its savings card's clear per-fill structure ($450 per 90-day fill) rewards 90-day prescriptions.

When Farxiga tends to be the choice

Farxiga has a slightly lower 2026 Medicare price and a card that can reach $0 for commercial patients. If your plan places Farxiga on a better formulary tier, it may simply cost you less.

Which is cheaper for you?

Medicare: Farxiga is marginally cheaper on the negotiated price. Commercial insurance: whichever your plan places on the better tier — check both cards. Uninsured: similar list prices; apply to the manufacturer's assistance program, and watch for generic dapagliflozin, which may arrive first. Never switch SGLT2 inhibitors for a small price gap without your doctor.

FAQ

Is Jardiance or Farxiga cheaper on Medicare?

Farxiga's 2026 negotiated price (~$178.50) is slightly lower than Jardiance's (~$197) per 30-day supply. Both are far below their old list prices.

Are Jardiance and Farxiga interchangeable?

They are the same class (SGLT2 inhibitors) with very similar effects, but they are different drugs and not automatically substitutable. Switching is a clinical decision for your doctor.

Which has a generic first?

Generic dapagliflozin (Farxiga) is emerging first; generic empagliflozin (Jardiance) is not yet widely available. A generic is usually the cheapest option once stocked.
