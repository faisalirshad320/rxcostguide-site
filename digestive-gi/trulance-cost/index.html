Trulance cost, coupon & copay card

Trulance (plecanatide) is an oral guanylate cyclase-C agonist prescribed for adults with chronic idiopathic constipation or irritable bowel syndrome with constipation (IBS-C).

The short answer

Trulance has a list price of about ~$620/mo without insurance. If you have commercial insurance, the Trulance Savings Card can lower your cost to as little as $25 per prescription (not $0), for up to 12 fills/yr. The card cannot be used with Medicare or Medicaid; Medicare Part D enrollees instead benefit from the $2,100 annual out-of-pocket cap in 2026.

Trulance is taken by mouth once daily to increase intestinal fluid secretion and help restore more regular bowel movements in chronic idiopathic constipation and IBS-C. Because it is a brand-name medicine with a high list price, what you actually pay depends mostly on your insurance type, your plan's formulary placement, and whether you qualify for the manufacturer savings card.

The Trulance copay card

With commercial insurance, the Trulance Savings Card can bring your cost down to as little as $25 per prescription.

The Trulance Savings Card is offered by the manufacturer for eligible patients with commercial (private or employer) insurance. The out-of-pocket cost is as little as $25 per prescription with the Trulance Savings Card (not $0), and it can be used for up to 12 fills/yr. It is not valid for anyone with Medicare, Medicaid, or other government insurance. Enroll on the official Trulance savings page and present the card at your pharmacy.

Trulance cost with Medicare

Trulance is not among the drugs with a Medicare-negotiated price, so your cost depends on your Part D plan's formulary tier and deductible. In 2026, total annual out-of-pocket spending on covered Part D drugs is capped at $2,100, which limits your exposure if you take Trulance along with other medications. Federal rules bar manufacturer copay cards for Medicare beneficiaries, so the Trulance Savings Card cannot be used.

Cash-pay strategies for Trulance

Without insurance, Trulance costs about ~$620/mo at list price, and the manufacturer savings card does not apply to uninsured patients. Pharmacy discount cards may lower the cash price somewhat, and no manufacturer patient-assistance program is listed. Ask your clinician whether a lower-cost constipation treatment could work for you.

Cheaper alternatives to Trulance

Other options for chronic idiopathic constipation and IBS-C include another guanylate cyclase-C agonist, prescription drugs from different classes, and over-the-counter laxatives or fiber, some of which may cost less depending on your coverage.

I tell patients with commercial insurance to enroll in the savings card before their first fill, because the price difference at the counter is substantial. For patients on Medicare, I remind them the copay card won't work but that the 2026 Part D out-of-pocket cap protects them from unlimited costs. Diarrhea is the most common side effect, so I ask patients to let me know early if it is bothersome rather than simply stopping the medicine.

Trulance cost — frequently asked questions

How much does Trulance cost with the savings card?

With commercial insurance, the Trulance Savings Card lowers your cost to as little as $25 per prescription (not $0), for up to 12 fills/yr.

Is there a generic or biosimilar for Trulance?

Trulance (plecanatide) has no biosimilar, and our verified pricing data does not list a lower-cost generic version. Ask your pharmacist or prescriber about current options and lower-cost alternatives.

How much is Trulance without insurance?

The list price is about ~$620/mo. The savings card requires commercial insurance, and no manufacturer patient-assistance program is listed, so uninsured patients may want to compare pharmacy discount card prices or ask about alternatives.

Does Medicare cover Trulance, and can I use the copay card?

Coverage and cost vary by Part D plan. Trulance does not have a Medicare-negotiated price, but 2026 Part D out-of-pocket costs are capped at $2,100. The Trulance Savings Card cannot be used with Medicare or Medicaid.

How do I enroll in the Trulance Savings Card?

Visit the official Trulance savings page at https://www.trulance.com/savings, confirm you have commercial insurance, and present the card to your pharmacy when you fill your prescription.
