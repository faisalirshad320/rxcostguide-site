Eylea cost, coupon & copay card

Eylea (aflibercept) is an anti-VEGF medicine that a retina specialist injects into the eye to treat wet age-related macular degeneration, diabetic macular edema, and diabetic retinopathy.

The short answer

Eylea has a list price of ~$1,999 per injection, before the clinic's administration fees. With commercial insurance, the Eylea4U Commercial Copay Card can bring your cost down to as little as $0 per treatment, with assistance of up to $20,000/yr plus $1,000 administration. Medicare usually covers Eylea under Part B because it is given in the doctor's office, so the $2,100 2026 Part D out-of-pocket cap generally does not apply. Medicare and Medicaid patients cannot use the copay card but may qualify for EYLEA4U Patient Assistance.

Eylea is not picked up at a pharmacy. A retina specialist injects it into the eye in the office, usually on a repeating schedule that can last for years. Your total cost depends on the drug's high list price, how often you need injections, the clinic's administration fees, and whether your plan covers it under the medical benefit or the pharmacy benefit.

The Eylea copay card

The Eylea4U Commercial Copay Card can lower your cost to as little as $0 per treatment with the Eylea4U Commercial Copay Card.

This card is for patients whose Eylea is covered by commercial (employer or private) insurance. It helps with the out-of-pocket cost of the drug and also offers a separate amount toward administration. You cannot use it with Medicare, Medicaid, or other government insurance. Your retina practice's billing staff often help with enrollment, and you can also sign up through the manufacturer's patient support site.

Eylea cost with Medicare

Eylea is not on the list of drugs with a Medicare negotiated price. Because it is injected in the doctor's office, Medicare usually covers it under Part B rather than Part D. That means the $2,100 2026 Part D out-of-pocket cap generally does not limit your Eylea costs. Your Part B cost-sharing applies instead, and a Medigap or other supplemental plan may cover much of it. Federal rules bar manufacturer copay cards for Medicare patients, so the Eylea4U card cannot be used. Ask the practice to check whether a biosimilar is preferred by your plan, or apply to EYLEA4U Patient Assistance.

Patient assistance for Eylea

EYLEA4U is Regeneron's patient support program. It helps with insurance benefit checks, prior authorization, and financial assistance for eligible patients, including some who are uninsured or have government coverage. Eligibility and terms vary, so contact the program or apply through the official patient support page.

Cash-pay strategies for Eylea

Without insurance, Eylea's list price is ~$1,999 per injection, and clinic administration fees are added on top. Retail pharmacy discount cards rarely help because the drug is bought and billed by the eye clinic. The best options are to apply to EYLEA4U Patient Assistance and to ask your retina specialist whether a lower-cost aflibercept biosimilar or another anti-VEGF treatment is appropriate.

Cheaper alternatives to Eylea

Aflibercept biosimilars, such as Pavblu and Yesafili, are now available and may cost less or be preferred by your insurer. Other anti-VEGF injections are also used for these eye conditions, and your retina specialist can advise whether switching makes sense for you.

I tell patients that the drug is only part of the bill. The visit, the injection fee, and how often you need treatment all add up, so ask the practice for an estimate up front. I also encourage patients to let the office's benefits coordinator handle enrollment in Eylea4U, because they deal with these programs every day. If cost is a barrier, say so early. A biosimilar or a patient assistance application can often keep treatment on schedule, and that matters for protecting vision.

Eylea cost — frequently asked questions

How much does Eylea cost with the copay card?

With commercial insurance, the Eylea4U Commercial Copay Card can lower your cost to as little as $0 per treatment. Assistance is capped at up to $20,000/yr plus $1,000 administration.

Is there a generic or biosimilar for Eylea?

There is no generic, but aflibercept biosimilars, such as Pavblu and Yesafili, are now available. Ask your retina specialist and your insurer whether a biosimilar is an option and what it would cost you.

How much is Eylea without insurance?

Eylea's list price is ~$1,999 per injection. Clinic administration fees are extra. Uninsured patients should apply to EYLEA4U Patient Assistance.

Does Medicare cover Eylea, and can I use the copay card?

Medicare usually covers Eylea under Part B because it is given in the doctor's office. Because it is not a Part D drug, the $2,100 2026 Part D cap generally does not apply. Federal rules bar Medicare patients from using manufacturer copay cards, but they may qualify for EYLEA4U Patient Assistance.

How do I enroll in the Eylea4U copay program?

Enroll through the official patient support site at https://www.eylea.us/s/patient-support, or ask your retina practice's billing staff to help. You must have commercial insurance. Medicare and Medicaid patients are not eligible for the copay card.
