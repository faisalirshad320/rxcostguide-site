Orilissa cost, coupon & copay card

Orilissa (elagolix) is an oral GnRH receptor antagonist from AbbVie, prescribed for women with moderate to severe endometriosis pain.

The short answer

Orilissa has a list price of about ~$1,250/mo without insurance. Patients with commercial insurance can pay as little as $5 per month with the Orilissa Savings Card (not $0), with the card covering up to $5,000/yr. Medicare Part D patients cannot use the card, but their annual out-of-pocket drug costs are capped at $2,100 in 2026, and uninsured or underinsured patients can apply to myAbbVie Assist.

Orilissa is a daily oral tablet that lowers estrogen levels by blocking GnRH receptors in the pituitary, which reduces endometriosis-related pain. Because it is a brand-name drug, its list price is high, and what you actually pay depends mostly on your insurance type, your plan's formulary placement, and whether you qualify for the manufacturer's savings card or assistance program. Many plans require prior authorization, often after trying first-line hormonal therapies.

The Orilissa copay card

Commercially insured patients may pay as little as $5 per month with the Orilissa Savings Card (not $0).

The Orilissa Savings Card from AbbVie reduces out-of-pocket costs for patients whose commercial (private or employer) insurance covers Orilissa. The card has an annual maximum benefit, after which you pay your plan's normal cost-sharing. It is commercial insurance only — not Medicare or Medicaid, or other government-funded insurance. Enroll on the official Orilissa savings page and present the card at your pharmacy with each fill.

Orilissa cost with Medicare

Orilissa is not among the drugs with a Medicare-negotiated price, so your cost depends on your Part D plan's formulary tier and coverage rules. In 2026, total out-of-pocket spending on covered Part D drugs is capped at $2,100 for the year. Federal rules bar manufacturer copay cards, including the Orilissa Savings Card, from being used with Medicare; patients with limited income can check eligibility for Extra Help or myAbbVie Assist.

Patient assistance for Orilissa

myAbbVie Assist is AbbVie's patient assistance program for eligible patients who are uninsured or whose insurance does not adequately cover their medication. Eligibility is based on criteria such as income and insurance status; review the requirements and application steps on the AbbVie eligibility page.

Cash-pay strategies for Orilissa

Without insurance, Orilissa's list price is about ~$1,250/mo, so paying full cash price is expensive. Pharmacy discount cards may reduce the price modestly, but the larger savings usually come from applying to myAbbVie Assist if you qualify. Ask your clinician whether a lower-cost hormonal option is appropriate before committing to cash pay.

Cheaper alternatives to Orilissa

Other endometriosis treatments include progestins, combined hormonal contraceptives, and other GnRH-targeting medications, several of which are available as lower-cost generics. Your clinician can help weigh effectiveness, side effects, and cost for your situation.

I tell patients that Orilissa is generally used for a limited duration because lowering estrogen can reduce bone mineral density, so the treatment plan should include a defined time frame. I also remind them to confirm prior authorization requirements with their insurer before the first fill, since denials are a common reason for unexpected costs. If you have commercial insurance, enroll in the savings card before going to the pharmacy so the discount applies at the counter.

Orilissa cost — frequently asked questions

How much does Orilissa cost with the savings card?

Patients with commercial insurance may pay as little as $5 per month with the Orilissa Savings Card (not $0). The card covers up to $5,000/yr in savings.

Is there a generic or biosimilar for Orilissa?

Orilissa (elagolix) is a small-molecule oral drug, so biosimilars do not apply, and no biosimilar exists. Ask your pharmacist about current generic availability and about lower-cost alternative treatments for endometriosis.

How much is Orilissa without insurance?

The list price of Orilissa is about ~$1,250/mo. Uninsured patients may be able to reduce costs through pharmacy discount cards or by applying to AbbVie's myAbbVie Assist program.

Does Medicare cover Orilissa, and can I use the copay card?

Coverage varies by Part D plan. The Orilissa Savings Card is for commercial insurance only — not Medicare or Medicaid. In 2026, Medicare Part D caps annual out-of-pocket drug costs at $2,100.

How do I enroll in the Orilissa Savings Card?

Enroll through the official Orilissa savings page at https://www.orilissa.com/savings-card, then present the card at your pharmacy along with your commercial insurance information.
