Premarin cost, coupon & copay card

Premarin (conjugated estrogens) is an oral estrogen hormone therapy from Pfizer used mainly to treat menopausal hot flashes, night sweats, and vulvar and vaginal atrophy.

The short answer

Premarin's list price runs about ~$200+/mo without insurance. With commercial insurance and the Premarin Savings Card, most patients pay $25 per prescription (not $0), with the card covering up to $120/fill, $1,440/yr. Medicare Part D enrollees can't use the card, but their yearly out-of-pocket drug costs are capped at $2,100 in 2026.

Premarin is taken as a daily tablet, usually at the lowest effective dose for the shortest time that controls symptoms, and people with a uterus generally also need a progestogen. Because it is a brand-name product with a high list price, what you actually pay depends mostly on your insurance type, your plan's formulary tier, and whether you use the manufacturer savings card. A generic conjugated estrogens is available, which may lower costs for some patients.

The Premarin copay card

Eligible commercially insured patients pay $25 per prescription with the Premarin Savings Card — not $0.

The Premarin Savings Card is Pfizer's official copay program. It reduces your out-of-pocket cost to $25 per prescription, with Pfizer covering up to $120/fill and $1,440/yr. If your plan's copay exceeds what the card covers, you pay the difference. The card is for commercial insurance only and cannot be used with Medicare, Medicaid, or other government insurance.

Premarin cost with Medicare

Premarin is not on the list of drugs with a Medicare-negotiated price. Your cost under Part D depends on your plan's formulary tier and deductible, and federal rules bar manufacturer copay cards for Medicare enrollees. In 2026, Part D out-of-pocket drug spending is capped at $2,100 per year, and the Medicare Prescription Payment Plan can spread those costs into monthly payments.

Patient assistance for Premarin

Pfizer PAP Connect is Pfizer's patient assistance program for eligible patients who are uninsured or underinsured and meet income requirements. Eligibility and the medicines covered can change, so check the program site or ask your prescriber's office to help you apply.

Cash-pay strategies for Premarin

Without insurance, Premarin's list price is about ~$200+/mo, and pharmacy prices vary. Pharmacy discount cards, comparing prices across pharmacies, and asking about a generic conjugated estrogens can lower your cost. If you have limited income, Pfizer PAP Connect may help.

Cheaper alternatives to Premarin

A generic conjugated estrogens is available, and other estrogen therapies in different forms (oral, transdermal, or vaginal) may be options depending on your symptoms and coverage. Ask your clinician which choice fits your health history and your formulary.

I tell patients to call their insurer before the first fill, because estrogen products are often on different tiers and a covered alternative can cost far less than brand Premarin. If you have commercial insurance, activate the savings card before you go to the pharmacy, and remember it brings your cost to $25, not zero. If you still have your uterus, expect your clinician to pair estrogen with a progestogen, and review the plan at least once a year.

Premarin cost — frequently asked questions

How much does Premarin cost with the savings card?

With commercial insurance and the Premarin Savings Card, eligible patients pay $25 per prescription. This is not $0. The card covers up to $120/fill and $1,440/yr.

Is there a generic or biosimilar for Premarin?

A generic conjugated estrogens is available. Biosimilars apply only to biologic drugs, so there is no Premarin biosimilar. Ask your pharmacist whether the generic is covered by your plan.

How much is Premarin without insurance?

Premarin's list price is about ~$200+/mo. Pharmacy discount cards, the generic, or Pfizer PAP Connect for eligible patients may lower what you pay.

Does Medicare cover Premarin, and can I use the copay card?

Many Part D plans cover estrogen therapy, but your cost varies by plan. The Premarin Savings Card cannot be used with Medicare or Medicaid. In 2026, Part D out-of-pocket drug costs are capped at $2,100 per year.

How do I enroll in the Premarin Savings Card?

Sign up on Pfizer's official site at https://www.pfizermenopause.com/savings-card/#premarin. You must have commercial insurance. Present the card at your pharmacy with your prescription.
