What menopause & women's-health medications cost, and how to pay less

Newer treatments for menopausal hot flashes, uterine fibroids, endometriosis and postmenopausal osteoporosis are effective but often expensive without help.

Reviewed by Dr. Faisal Irshad, MBBS, M.Phil Chemical Pathology PMDC 54440-SUpdated 2026-10-13
The short answer

These branded drugs run roughly $580 to $2,900 a month at list price. Commercially insured patients can usually pay $0 to $30 a month with the manufacturer savings card. Medicare patients pay plan cost-share under the $2,100 Part D cap; uninsured patients can apply for patient assistance.

Read the savings-card fine print

Several women’s-health savings cards are not a flat $0 — for example one may be $0 the first month and then a fixed amount after, or a small monthly copay. Always check the per-fill amount and annual cap when you enroll.
