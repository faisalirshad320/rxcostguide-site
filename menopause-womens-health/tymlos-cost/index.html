Tymlos cost, coupon & copay card

Tymlos (abaloparatide) is a once-daily self-injected bone-building medicine for postmenopausal women and men with osteoporosis who are at high risk of fracture.

The short answer

Tymlos has a list price of about ~$2,935/mo, but commercially insured patients may pay as little as $0 per month with the Tymlos Savings Card, subject to a limit of 12 fills/yr and an annual cap. On Medicare Part D, the copay card cannot be used, but 2026 out-of-pocket drug costs are capped at $2,100 for the year. Patients without adequate coverage can apply to Radius Assist, the manufacturer's patient assistance program.

Tymlos is an anabolic (bone-forming) osteoporosis treatment given as a daily injection under the skin with a prefilled pen. It is used for a limited course, after which patients usually switch to a medicine that maintains bone density. Because it is a brand-only drug with no generic or biosimilar, your cost depends heavily on your insurance plan's formulary tier, prior authorization rules, and whether you qualify for the manufacturer's savings card or assistance program.

The Tymlos copay card

Commercially insured patients may pay as little as $0 per month with the Tymlos Savings Card.

The Tymlos Savings Card helps cover out-of-pocket costs for patients whose commercial or private insurance covers Tymlos. The card covers up to 12 fills per year, and an annual cap applies, so check the program terms for your remaining benefit. It cannot be used with Medicare, Medicaid, or other government insurance. Tymlos cost with Medicare

Tymlos is not among the drugs with a Medicare-negotiated price, so your cost depends on your Part D plan's formulary tier and coverage rules. In 2026, Part D out-of-pocket drug spending is capped at $2,100 for the year, which limits your total annual cost for a high-priced drug like Tymlos. Federal rules bar manufacturer copay cards for Medicare beneficiaries. Patients with limited income can ask about Medicare Extra Help or apply to Radius Assist.

Patient assistance for Tymlos

Radius Assist is the manufacturer's support program. It may help with insurance coverage questions and offer medicine at reduced or no cost to eligible patients who are uninsured or underinsured. Eligibility criteria and required documents are listed on the program site, and your prescriber's office can help you apply.

Cash-pay strategies for Tymlos

Without insurance, Tymlos costs about ~$2,935/mo at list price, and pharmacy discount cards usually offer only modest savings on specialty brand drugs. Uninsured patients should apply to Radius Assist first. Ask your clinician whether another osteoporosis treatment that costs less would be appropriate for you.

Cheaper alternatives to Tymlos

Other osteoporosis options include teriparatide, another daily bone-building injection. Antiresorptive drugs such as oral or IV bisphosphonates and denosumab are often far cheaper and may be appropriate depending on your fracture risk and history.

I tell patients that Tymlos is a time-limited course, generally no more than 2 years of parathyroid hormone analog use in a lifetime, so it is worth securing coverage and the savings card before the first fill. I also remind them that stopping without a follow-up medicine can undo much of the bone gained. We plan the transition to a maintenance drug from the start. If insurance requires prior authorization, a recent bone density scan and documented fracture history usually speed approval.

Tymlos cost — frequently asked questions

How much does Tymlos cost with the savings card?

Patients with commercial insurance may pay as little as $0 per month with the Tymlos Savings Card. The card covers up to 12 fills per year, and an annual cap applies.

Is there a generic or biosimilar for Tymlos?

No. There is no generic or biosimilar version of Tymlos (abaloparatide). It is available only as the brand-name product.

How much is Tymlos without insurance?

The approximate list price is ~$2,935/mo. Uninsured patients can apply to Radius Assist, the manufacturer's patient assistance program, for possible reduced-cost or free medicine.

Does Medicare cover Tymlos, and can I use the copay card?

Many Medicare Part D plans cover Tymlos, but your cost varies by plan. Tymlos does not have a Medicare-negotiated price. The savings card cannot be used with Medicare or Medicaid. In 2026, Part D out-of-pocket costs are capped at $2,100 for the year.

How do I enroll in the Tymlos Savings Card?

Enroll through the official Tymlos access and savings page at https://www.tymlos.com/access-and-savings/. You must have commercial insurance; Medicare and Medicaid patients are not eligible.
