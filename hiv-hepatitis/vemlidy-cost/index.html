Vemlidy cost, coupon & copay card

Vemlidy (tenofovir alafenamide) is a once-daily antiviral tablet from Gilead used to treat chronic hepatitis B.

The short answer

Vemlidy has a list price of about ~$1,587/mo. With commercial insurance, the Vemlidy Support Path Copay Coupon can bring your cost to as little as $0 per prescription (up to $6,000/yr, no monthly limit). On Medicare Part D, the coupon can't be used, but your yearly out-of-pocket drug spending is capped at $2,100 in 2026, and Gilead Support Path may help if you are uninsured or underinsured.

Vemlidy is taken once daily to keep the hepatitis B virus suppressed, and most people take it long term, often for years. Because it is a brand-name drug with a high list price and no widely available generic, what you actually pay depends mostly on your insurance coverage and whether you use the manufacturer's savings or assistance programs. A generic is FDA-approved but not widely available.

The Vemlidy copay card

Commercially insured patients may pay as little as $0 per prescription with the Vemlidy Support Path Copay Coupon.

The coupon covers out-of-pocket costs for Vemlidy up to $6,000/yr, with no monthly limit. It is for patients with commercial (private or employer) insurance only. Government insurance such as Medicare and Medicaid is not eligible. Enroll through the official Vemlidy savings page, then give the coupon information to your pharmacy.

Vemlidy cost with Medicare

Vemlidy is not among the drugs with a Medicare-negotiated price, so your cost depends on your Part D plan's formulary and tier. In 2026, total out-of-pocket spending on covered Part D drugs is capped at $2,100 for the year, which limits what you pay for a long-term medicine like Vemlidy. Federal rules bar manufacturer copay coupons for Medicare patients. If costs are a burden, ask about Extra Help (Low-Income Subsidy) or contact Gilead Support Path.

Patient assistance for Vemlidy

Gilead Support Path helps patients with insurance navigation, benefits checks, and access to financial support options for Vemlidy. Uninsured or underinsured patients may qualify for help based on eligibility criteria set by the program. You or your prescriber can contact Support Path to check your options.

Cash-pay strategies for Vemlidy

Without insurance, Vemlidy's list price is about ~$1,587/mo, and pharmacy cash prices vary. A discount card may reduce the price somewhat, but Gilead Support Path is usually the most important first call for uninsured patients. Ask your prescriber whether an older generic hepatitis B antiviral would be appropriate if cost remains a barrier.

Cheaper alternatives to Vemlidy

Other antivirals for chronic hepatitis B, including older options available as generics such as entecavir and tenofovir disoproxil fumarate, may cost less. Whether a switch is appropriate depends on your kidney function, bone health, and treatment history, so it should be decided with your prescriber.

I tell patients that hepatitis B treatment is usually a long-term commitment, so it is worth securing a reliable way to pay before the first fill. I also stress never to stop Vemlidy on your own or let a refill lapse because of cost, since stopping can cause a serious flare of hepatitis. Call your clinic or Gilead Support Path first. If you have commercial insurance, enroll in the copay coupon right away so it is active when you pick up your prescription.

Vemlidy cost — frequently asked questions

How much does Vemlidy cost with the copay card?

With commercial insurance, the Vemlidy Support Path Copay Coupon can lower your cost to as little as $0 per prescription. It covers up to $6,000/yr, with no monthly limit.

Is there a generic for Vemlidy?

A generic tenofovir alafenamide is FDA-approved but not widely available. Vemlidy has no biosimilar because it is a small-molecule drug, not a biologic.

How much is Vemlidy without insurance?

Vemlidy's list price is about ~$1,587/mo. Uninsured patients should contact Gilead Support Path to see if they qualify for assistance. Discount cards and pharmacy cash prices also vary.

Does Medicare cover Vemlidy, and can I use the copay card?

Many Medicare Part D plans cover Vemlidy, but costs vary by plan. In 2026, out-of-pocket Part D drug costs are capped at $2,100 for the year. The copay coupon cannot be used with Medicare or Medicaid.

How do I enroll in the Vemlidy savings program?

Enroll at the official Vemlidy savings page (https://www.vemlidy.com/savings) if you have commercial insurance. For other help, contact Gilead Support Path at https://www.mysupportpath.com/.
