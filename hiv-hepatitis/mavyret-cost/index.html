Mavyret cost, coupon & copay card

Mavyret (glecaprevir/pibrentasvir) is a pan-genotypic hepatitis C cure, often taken as an 8-week course.

The short answer

Mavyret lists around $13,000 for an 8-week course at list price. Commercially insured patients can pay as little as $5 with the Mavyret copay card. Uninsured, income-eligible patients can get it free through AbbVie; Medicare patients are protected by the $2,100 Part D cap.

Like Epclusa, Mavyret is a time-limited cure rather than a lifelong medication, so its price is a one-time course cost. Its shorter 8-week course (for many patients) is a distinguishing feature.

Mavyret cost with Medicare

Mavyret is not on the Medicare negotiated-price list. Part D members pay plan cost-share up to the $2,100 annual cap — which, for a one-time course, often covers the whole cure. Copay cards cannot be used with Medicare.

Patient assistance for Mavyret

AbbVie provides Mavyret free to uninsured, income-eligible patients through its patient-assistance program.

AbbVie myAbbVie Assist / ProCeed

Cash-pay strategies for Mavyret

Uninsured, income-eligible patients should apply to AbbVie's program rather than face the list price. Many state Medicaid and public-health programs also fund hepatitis C cures, so ask your clinic about local options if you don't qualify for manufacturer assistance.

Cheaper alternatives to Mavyret

Epclusa (sofosbuvir/velpatasvir) is the main alternative pan-genotypic cure and has an authorized generic that can be very cheap for cash payers. Both are highly effective; genotype, cirrhosis status and prior treatment guide the choice.

The eight-week course is Mavyret's calling card — a shorter cure for many patients. As with Epclusa, the headline price should never deter treatment: between the copay card, AbbVie assistance and public programs, curing hepatitis C is within reach for essentially everyone. The clinical win of a cure vastly outweighs the cost hurdles, which are almost always solvable.

Mavyret cost — frequently asked questions

How much does Mavyret cost?

Around $13,000 for an 8-week course at list price, but commercially insured patients can pay as little as $5 with the copay card, and uninsured income-eligible patients can get it free through AbbVie.

How long is Mavyret treatment?

Often 8 weeks for many patients (longer in some cases). Because it's curative, the cost is a one-time course, not an ongoing bill.

How do I get Mavyret cheaper?

With commercial insurance, the copay card brings it to about $5. On Medicare, the $2,100 cap often covers the course. Uninsured: AbbVie patient assistance or state hepatitis C programs.

Is Mavyret or Epclusa cheaper?

For cash payers, Epclusa's authorized generic can be the cheapest. With commercial insurance, both copay cards bring costs very low. The clinical choice depends on your genotype and history.

Does Medicare cover Mavyret?

Yes, under Part D. The $2,100 annual cap often covers the entire one-time course. Copay cards are not usable on Medicare.
