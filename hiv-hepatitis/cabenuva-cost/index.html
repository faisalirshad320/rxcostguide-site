Cabenuva cost, coupon & copay card

Cabenuva (cabotegravir/rilpivirine) is a long-acting injectable regimen given by a healthcare provider monthly or every 2 months to treat HIV-1 infection in eligible people whose virus is already suppressed.

The short answer

Cabenuva's list price is about ~$4,769–7,150 per injection set, but commercially insured patients can pay as little as $0 per prescription with the ViiV Connect Savings Program (up to $13,000/yr plus $100/treatment administration). If Cabenuva is covered under your Medicare Part D plan, your 2026 out-of-pocket drug costs are capped at $2,100, and uninsured or underinsured patients can apply to ViiV Connect for assistance.

Cabenuva replaces daily HIV pills with injections given in a clinic, either once a month or every 2 months, for people who are virologically suppressed on a stable regimen. Cost is driven by the high per-injection list price, how often you are dosed, whether your plan covers it under the pharmacy or medical benefit, and clinic administration charges.

The Cabenuva copay card

Commercially insured patients can pay as little as $0 per prescription with the ViiV Connect Savings Program.

The ViiV Connect Savings Program helps cover out-of-pocket costs for Cabenuva, with a benefit of up to $13,000/yr plus $100/treatment administration to help with the cost of getting your injections. It is for commercial insurance only and cannot be used with Medicare, Medicaid, or other government insurance.

Cabenuva cost with Medicare

Cabenuva has not been selected for Medicare drug price negotiation, so there is no Maximum Fair Price. If it is covered under your Part D plan, your total out-of-pocket drug spending is capped at $2,100 in 2026, and you can ask about spreading costs over the year with the Medicare Prescription Payment Plan. Because it is administered in a clinic, some patients have it covered under Part B instead, where the Part D cap does not apply. Manufacturer copay cards are barred on Medicare, but ViiV Connect may be able to help eligible beneficiaries.

Patient assistance for Cabenuva

ViiV Connect is ViiV Healthcare's access and support program. It can help you check your insurance coverage and may provide Cabenuva at reduced or no cost to eligible patients who are uninsured or underinsured, including some Medicare beneficiaries. Eligibility rules apply, so apply with your HIV care team.

Cash-pay strategies for Cabenuva

Without insurance, Cabenuva's list price is high at about ~$4,769–7,150 per injection set, and retail discount cards typically make little difference for a clinic-administered specialty drug. Your best option is to apply to ViiV Connect for patient assistance and ask your clinic about Ryan White/ADAP programs, which commonly cover HIV medicines. If injections are not affordable, ask your clinician about effective daily oral regimens, some of which cost much less.

Cheaper alternatives to Cabenuva

There is no generic or biosimilar version of Cabenuva. Alternatives are daily oral HIV-1 regimens, including single-tablet options. Your HIV clinician can help you choose based on resistance history, coverage, and preference.

I tell patients that Cabenuva only works if you show up for every injection on schedule, so we plan the visits as carefully as the paperwork. Before the first dose, I have the clinic confirm whether the plan covers it under the pharmacy or medical benefit, because that determines both your copay and whether the savings program applies. If coverage stalls, I'd rather keep you on a suppressive oral regimen than risk a gap that could lead to resistance.

Cabenuva cost — frequently asked questions

How much does Cabenuva cost with the copay card?

With commercial insurance, you may pay as little as $0 per prescription with the ViiV Connect Savings Program. It covers up to $13,000/yr plus $100/treatment administration.

Is there a generic or biosimilar for Cabenuva?

No. There is no generic or biosimilar version of Cabenuva available. Patients who need lower-cost options can talk with their clinician about daily oral HIV regimens.

How much is Cabenuva without insurance?

The list price is about ~$4,769–7,150 per injection set, and administration fees may be extra. Uninsured patients should apply to ViiV Connect at https://www.viivconnect.com for possible assistance.

Does Medicare cover Cabenuva, and can I use the copay card?

Medicare may cover Cabenuva under Part D or Part B, depending on how it is supplied and billed. Under Part D, your 2026 out-of-pocket drug costs are capped at $2,100. The copay card cannot be used with Medicare or Medicaid, but ViiV Connect may help eligible beneficiaries.

How do I enroll in the Cabenuva savings program?

Enroll through the official Cabenuva savings and support page at https://www.cabenuva.com/savings-and-support/cabenuva-support/. You must have commercial insurance, and your clinic can often help with enrollment.
