Dovato cost, copay card & assistance

Dovato (dolutegravir/lamivudine) is a once-daily, two-drug complete HIV regimen — a full treatment in a single tablet using fewer medicines than older three-drug regimens.

The short answer

Dovato costs about $3,100 a month at list price, but almost no one pays that. Commercially insured patients can pay as little as $0 with the ViiVConnect copay card; uninsured patients use ADAP or ViiV patient assistance; Medicare patients pay plan cost-share under the $2,100 Part D cap. There is no generic.

Dovato’s appeal is using two drugs instead of the traditional three while remaining a complete regimen. Its cost, like other HIV drugs, is managed almost entirely through copay cards, assistance programs and the robust safety net around HIV treatment.

Dovato cost with Medicare

Dovato has no Medicare-negotiated price. For 2026, Medicare patients pay their Part D plan cost-share, capped by the $2,100 annual out-of-pocket maximum. HIV drugs are a protected class on Part D, so coverage is broad. Copay cards cannot be used with Medicare.

Patient assistance for Dovato

ViiV provides Dovato free to uninsured, income-eligible patients through its patient-assistance program. State AIDS Drug Assistance Programs (ADAP) also cover it for eligible people.

ViiVConnect patient assistance / ADAP

Cash-pay strategies for Dovato

Almost no one should pay cash for Dovato. Commercially insured patients use the ViiVConnect card ($0); uninsured patients use ADAP or ViiV assistance. If you are between coverage, ADAP and the manufacturer program can bridge the gap so therapy is never interrupted — which matters clinically as much as financially.

Cheaper alternatives to Dovato

Biktarvy (a three-drug single tablet) is the main alternative first-line regimen; see our Biktarvy vs Dovato comparison. Which regimen fits depends on resistance, other conditions and your provider’s judgment — never switch HIV therapy for cost without specialist input.

Dovato reflects a real shift toward two-drug regimens, and patients often ask whether fewer drugs means cheaper — it does not, really; cost is driven by brand status, not pill count. What matters most is continuity: the HIV safety net (ADAP, manufacturer programs, protected-class Part D coverage) is strong precisely so that nobody has to stop treatment over money. Use it.

Dovato cost — frequently asked questions

How much is Dovato with insurance?

Commercially insured patients can pay as little as $0 with the ViiVConnect card. Medicare patients pay plan cost-share under the $2,100 Part D cap.

Is there a generic for Dovato?

No — dolutegravir/lamivudine is brand-only. Cost is managed through the copay card (commercial), ADAP and ViiV assistance (uninsured), or Part D (Medicare).

What does Dovato cost without insurance?

About $3,100 a month at list price, but uninsured people rarely pay that — ADAP and ViiV patient assistance provide it free to eligible patients.

Is Dovato as good as a three-drug regimen?

For many people Dovato (two drugs) is as effective as traditional three-drug regimens, but suitability depends on resistance testing and other factors. That is a specialist decision.

Can I use the ViiVConnect card on Medicare?

No — commercial insurance only. Medicare patients use their Part D plan and the $2,100 cap.
