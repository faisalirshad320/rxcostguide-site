Delstrigo cost, coupon & copay card

Delstrigo (doravirine/lamivudine/tenofovir disoproxil fumarate) is a complete, once-daily single-tablet regimen used to treat HIV-1 infection in adults.

The short answer

Delstrigo has a list price of ~$2,812/mo. With commercial insurance, you may pay as little as $0 per prescription with the Delstrigo Savings Coupon (up to $6,800 total). On Medicare Part D, the coupon can't be used, but your yearly out-of-pocket drug costs are capped at $2,100 in 2026. Uninsured or underinsured patients can apply to the Merck Patient Assistance Program.

Delstrigo is taken as one tablet once daily and is intended to be continued long term to keep HIV-1 suppressed, so its cost recurs every month. Because it is a brand-name combination with no generic equivalent, what you actually pay depends mostly on your insurance type, your plan's formulary tier and deductible, and whether you qualify for manufacturer savings or assistance.

The Delstrigo copay card

Commercially insured patients may pay as little as $0 per prescription with the Delstrigo Savings Coupon.

The Delstrigo Savings Coupon from Merck helps cover out-of-pocket costs for patients whose commercial (private or employer) insurance covers Delstrigo. The benefit is up to $6,800 total, so patients with high deductibles or coinsurance should track how much of the benefit has been used. The coupon is not valid with Medicare, Medicaid, or other government insurance. Enrollment and full terms are available on the official Delstrigo website.

Delstrigo cost with Medicare

Delstrigo is not among the drugs with a Medicare-negotiated price. It may be covered under Medicare Part D, with costs that vary by plan, formulary tier, and deductible. In 2026, Part D out-of-pocket spending on covered drugs is capped at $2,100 for the year, which limits total annual exposure. Federal rules bar manufacturer copay coupons, including the Delstrigo Savings Coupon, for Medicare beneficiaries; patients who cannot afford their share can ask about the Merck Patient Assistance Program or spreading payments over the year through their Part D plan.

Patient assistance for Delstrigo

The Merck Patient Assistance Program may provide Delstrigo at reduced or no cost to eligible patients who are uninsured or cannot afford their medication. Eligibility is generally based on income and insurance status; review requirements and apply at merckhelps.com, often with help from your prescriber's office.

Cash-pay strategies for Delstrigo

Without insurance, Delstrigo has a list price of ~$2,812/mo, and pharmacy cash prices can vary. Prescription discount cards may reduce the cash price somewhat, but the Merck Patient Assistance Program is usually the most meaningful option for uninsured patients. Ask your clinician whether other HIV-1 regimens on your plan's formulary or available through assistance programs would be appropriate and more affordable.

Cheaper alternatives to Delstrigo

Several other single-tablet and multi-tablet HIV-1 regimens exist, and their costs and coverage vary by plan. Any switch should be decided with your HIV clinician based on resistance testing, kidney and bone health, hepatitis B status, and drug interactions.

I tell patients that the most expensive outcome with an HIV regimen is a gap in therapy, so plan refills ahead and never let your supply run out while sorting out insurance or coupon issues. If you have commercial insurance, enroll in the savings coupon before your first fill and keep an eye on how much of the total benefit you've used, especially if you have a high deductible. If you're on Medicare or uninsured, start the Merck Patient Assistance Program application early, since paperwork can take time.

Delstrigo cost — frequently asked questions

How much does Delstrigo cost with the savings coupon?

Commercially insured patients may pay as little as $0 per prescription with the Delstrigo Savings Coupon, up to $6,800 total. The coupon is for commercial insurance only — not Medicare or Medicaid.

Is there a generic version of Delstrigo?

No. Delstrigo is available only as a brand-name product from Merck, and there is no generic equivalent of this combination tablet.

How much is Delstrigo without insurance?

The list price is ~$2,812/mo. Cash prices vary by pharmacy, and discount cards may lower the price somewhat. Uninsured patients should consider applying to the Merck Patient Assistance Program at https://www.merckhelps.com.

Does Medicare cover Delstrigo, and can I use the coupon?

Delstrigo may be covered under Medicare Part D, with costs varying by plan. The Delstrigo Savings Coupon cannot be used with Medicare, but Part D out-of-pocket costs are capped at $2,100 in 2026. Delstrigo does not have a Medicare-negotiated price.

How do I enroll in the Delstrigo Savings Coupon?

Visit the official Delstrigo website at https://www.delstrigo.com to review eligibility and enroll. You must have commercial insurance; patients with Medicare or Medicaid are not eligible.
