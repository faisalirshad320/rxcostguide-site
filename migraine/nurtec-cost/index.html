Nurtec ODT cost, coupon & copay card

Nurtec ODT (rimegepant) is an oral CGRP gepant approved for both stopping migraines and preventing them.

The short answer

Nurtec ODT costs about $1,000 a month at list price. Commercially insured patients can pay as little as $0 with the Nurtec Savings Card. Medicare patients pay plan cost-share up to the $2,100 Part D cap.

Nurtec ODT cost with Medicare

Nurtec is not on the Medicare negotiated-price list. Part D members pay plan cost-share up to the $2,100 annual out-of-pocket cap. Copay cards cannot be used with Medicare.

Patient assistance for Nurtec ODT

Pfizer RxPathways provides Nurtec free to uninsured, income-eligible patients.

Pfizer Patient Assistance Program

Cash-pay strategies for Nurtec ODT

Without insurance, compare discount-card prices and apply to Pfizer RxPathways if income-eligible. Because cost scales with quantity, ask your prescriber whether an acute-only or lower-frequency regimen fits your migraine pattern.

Cheaper alternatives to Nurtec ODT

Ubrelvy (ubrogepant) is a comparable acute gepant; Qulipta (atogepant) is the preventive gepant from the same family. Which fits depends on whether you need acute relief, prevention, or both.

Quantity limits trip up more migraine patients than price does. A copay card can make the drug $0, but if the plan only authorizes eight tablets a month and you need more, that limit — not the sticker price — is your real constraint. Sorting the quantity limit with your prescriber usually matters more than the coupon.

Nurtec ODT cost — frequently asked questions

How much is Nurtec without insurance?

About $1,000 a month at list price, though it varies with how many tablets you use. Uninsured, income-eligible patients can apply to Pfizer RxPathways for free medication.

How do I get Nurtec for $0?

With commercial insurance, the Nurtec Savings Card can bring your cost to as little as $0. It cannot be used with Medicare or Medicaid.

Does Medicare cover Nurtec?

Generally yes under Part D, subject to your plan and often prior authorization. You pay plan cost-share up to the $2,100 annual cap; the copay card is not usable on Medicare.

Can Nurtec be used for prevention?

Yes — Nurtec is approved for both acute treatment and prevention of migraine, which is unusual among these drugs. Your covered quantity depends on your plan.

Is there a generic for Nurtec?

Not yet. Until a generic rimegepant arrives, the copay card (commercial), Part D (Medicare), or patient assistance (uninsured) are the main ways to lower cost.
