Aimovig cost, coupon & copay card

Aimovig (erenumab) is a once-monthly injectable CGRP antibody for migraine prevention.

The short answer

Aimovig costs about $700 a month at list price. Commercially insured patients can pay as little as $0 with the Aimovig Ally copay program. Medicare patients pay plan cost-share up to the $2,100 Part D cap.

Aimovig cost with Medicare

Aimovig is not on the Medicare negotiated-price list. Part D members pay plan cost-share up to the $2,100 annual cap. Copay programs cannot be used with Medicare.

Patient assistance for Aimovig

Amgen provides Aimovig free to uninsured, income-eligible patients through its Safety Net Foundation.

Amgen Safety Net Foundation

Cash-pay strategies for Aimovig

Without insurance, apply to the Amgen Safety Net Foundation if income-eligible, or compare discount cards. As with all CGRP preventives, insurers usually require documented trials of cheaper generic preventives first.

Cheaper alternatives to Aimovig

Ajovy and Emgality are comparable monthly CGRP antibodies (Ajovy also offers quarterly dosing); Qulipta is the oral option. Generic oral preventives are the inexpensive first-line choice insurers typically require.

Aimovig Ally, like Amgen's other support programs, is genuinely useful for the paperwork, not just the copay. These preventives fail to get approved far more often over missing step-therapy documentation than over price. Let the manufacturer's support team help your clinic push the prior authorization through — that's what it's there for.

Aimovig cost — frequently asked questions

How much is Aimovig without insurance?

About $700 per monthly dose at list price. Income-eligible uninsured patients can get it free through the Amgen Safety Net Foundation.

How do I get Aimovig for $0?

With commercial insurance, the Aimovig Ally program can bring you to as little as $0. Not usable with Medicare or Medicaid.

Does Medicare cover Aimovig?

Generally under Part D with prior authorization. You pay plan cost-share up to the $2,100 cap; copay programs are not usable on Medicare.

Which CGRP antibody is best?

Aimovig, Ajovy and Emgality are all effective monthly preventives; response varies by individual, and the choice is your doctor's. On cost, all copay programs can reach $0 for commercial patients.

Do I need to try other preventives first?

Usually yes — insurers typically require documented trials of cheaper generic preventives before covering Aimovig.
