Ajovy cost, coupon & copay card

Ajovy (fremanezumab) is an injectable CGRP antibody for migraine prevention, dosed monthly or quarterly.

The short answer

Ajovy costs about $700 a month at list price. Commercially insured patients can pay as little as $0 with the Ajovy copay card. Medicare patients pay plan cost-share up to the $2,100 Part D cap.

The Ajovy copay card can bring commercially insured patients to as little as $0 per month.

Ajovy cost with Medicare

Ajovy is not on the Medicare negotiated-price list. Part D members pay plan cost-share up to the $2,100 annual cap. Copay cards cannot be used with Medicare.

Patient assistance for Ajovy

Teva provides Ajovy free to uninsured, income-eligible patients through the Teva Cares Foundation.

Teva Shared Solutions / Teva Cares Foundation

Cash-pay strategies for Ajovy

Without insurance, compare discount cards and apply to the Teva Cares Foundation if income-eligible. The quarterly dosing option can reduce the number of fills, which may lower total cost slightly for cash payers.

Cheaper alternatives to Ajovy

Aimovig (erenumab) and Emgality (galcanezumab) are comparable monthly CGRP antibodies; Qulipta is the oral alternative. All aim to prevent migraine — the choice is about route, dosing and what your plan covers.

The quarterly option is a genuinely useful, underused feature. A patient who dislikes monthly injections can dose Ajovy every three months. It doesn't change the copay-card economics much, but for adherence and convenience it's worth asking your prescriber about.

Ajovy cost — frequently asked questions

How much is Ajovy without insurance?

About $700 per monthly dose at list price. Income-eligible uninsured patients can get it free through the Teva Cares Foundation.

How do I get Ajovy for $0?

With commercial insurance, the Ajovy copay card can bring you to as little as $0. Not usable with Medicare or Medicaid.

Does Medicare cover Ajovy?

Generally under Part D with prior authorization. You pay plan cost-share up to the $2,100 cap; the copay card is not usable on Medicare.

Can Ajovy be dosed quarterly?

Yes — Ajovy offers a quarterly option (one dose every three months) as well as monthly, which some patients find more convenient.

Ajovy, Aimovig or Emgality — which is cheapest?

All three copay cards can reach $0 for commercial patients, so cost is usually similar; coverage on your specific plan and dosing preference decide it.
