Ubrelvy cost, coupon & copay card

Ubrelvy (ubrogepant) is an oral CGRP gepant taken to stop a migraine attack once it starts.

The short answer

Ubrelvy costs about $1,000 a month at list price (cost scales with how many tablets you use). Commercially insured patients can pay as little as $0 with the Ubrelvy Savings Card. Medicare patients pay plan cost-share up to the $2,100 cap.

Ubrelvy is an acute-only migraine drug — taken at onset, not daily. Because it's dosed per attack, your plan's monthly quantity limit and your copay card together determine what you actually pay.

The Ubrelvy copay card

The Ubrelvy Savings Card can bring commercially insured patients to as little as $0 per month.

Commercial insurance only — not valid with Medicare or Medicaid. Ubrelvy is acute-only, so plans set a monthly quantity limit; the card covers your share up to that limit and an annual maximum.

Ubrelvy cost with Medicare

Ubrelvy is not on the Medicare negotiated-price list. Part D members pay plan cost-share up to the $2,100 annual cap. Copay cards cannot be used with Medicare.

Patient assistance for Ubrelvy

AbbVie provides Ubrelvy free to uninsured, income-eligible patients through myAbbVie Assist.

Cash-pay strategies for Ubrelvy

Without insurance, compare discount cards and apply to myAbbVie Assist if income-eligible. Ask your prescriber how many tablets you realistically need per month, since cost tracks quantity directly.

Cheaper alternatives to Ubrelvy

Nurtec ODT (rimegepant) is a comparable gepant that also works preventively. Older triptans (sumatriptan) are inexpensive generics for acute migraine and worth discussing if cost is the priority.

For acute migraine, I always mention that generic triptans exist and cost very little. The gepants like Ubrelvy are genuinely better tolerated for many people, but if someone is uninsured and struggling, a generic triptan may control attacks at a fraction of the price. It's a conversation worth having with the prescriber before assuming you need the newest drug.

Ubrelvy cost — frequently asked questions

How much is Ubrelvy without insurance?

About $1,000 a month at list price, depending on how many tablets you use. Income-eligible uninsured patients can get it free through myAbbVie Assist.

How do I get Ubrelvy cheaper?

With commercial insurance, the Ubrelvy Savings Card can bring you to as little as $0. On Medicare, rely on Part D and the $2,100 cap. Uninsured: myAbbVie Assist or discount cards.

Does Medicare cover Ubrelvy?

Generally under Part D, subject to plan and quantity limits. The copay card cannot be used with Medicare.

Is Ubrelvy better than a triptan?

Gepants like Ubrelvy suit people who can't take triptans or don't respond to them, but generic triptans are far cheaper. Which is right is a clinical decision for your doctor.

Is there a generic for Ubrelvy?

Not yet. The copay card, Part D, or patient assistance are the main cost levers until a generic ubrogepant arrives.
