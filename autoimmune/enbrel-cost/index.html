Enbrel cost, copay card & Medicare price

Enbrel (etanercept) is a TNF inhibitor biologic for rheumatoid arthritis, psoriasis and related conditions — and one of the first ten Medicare-negotiated drugs.

The short answer

Enbrel costs about $7,100 a month at list price. Commercially insured patients can pay as little as $0 with the Enbrel copay card. From 1 January 2026 the Medicare-negotiated price is about $2,355 per month-equivalent — a two-thirds cut. Etanercept biosimilars are also available and cheaper.

Enbrel is one of the oldest biologics and now sits at the intersection of two cost forces: a Medicare-negotiated price effective 2026, and etanercept biosimilars that undercut the brand. Both push the real cost well below the list price.

Enbrel cost with Medicare

Enbrel was one of the first ten Medicare-negotiated drugs. From 1 January 2026 its Maximum Fair Price is about $2,355 per month-equivalent, down from roughly $7,106 — a two-thirds cut, applied automatically for Part D members. Your own out-of-pocket is then capped at $2,100 for the year.

Patient assistance for Enbrel

Amgen provides Enbrel free to uninsured, income-eligible patients through its Safety Net Foundation.

Amgen Safety Net Foundation / Enbrel Support

Cash-pay strategies for Enbrel

The two cheapest levers are the biosimilar and, for the uninsured, Amgen's Safety Net Foundation. Ask your pharmacist about etanercept biosimilars and whether one is cheaper for you than the brand, even with the copay card.

Cheaper alternatives to Enbrel

Etanercept biosimilars are the direct lower-cost substitute. Other TNF inhibitors include Humira (now with many biosimilars). IL-17 and IL-23 inhibitors are alternatives for some conditions. See our [biosimilars guide](/guides/biosimilars/).

Enbrel is a rare case where a patient benefits from both a negotiated Medicare price and biosimilar competition at once. For a Medicare patient the negotiated price plus the $2,100 cap is the story; for others, the biosimilar is often the cheapest route. Ask the plain question: is a biosimilar cheaper for me than brand Enbrel with my card?

Enbrel cost — frequently asked questions

How much is Enbrel with Medicare in 2026?

The negotiated price is about $2,355 per month-equivalent, applied automatically. Your total out-of-pocket is then capped at $2,100 for the year under Part D.

Is there a biosimilar for Enbrel?

Yes — etanercept biosimilars are available in the US and are cheaper than the brand. Ask your pharmacist and plan which version is preferred.

How do I get Enbrel for $0?

With commercial insurance, the Enbrel copay card can bring you to as little as $0. Not usable with Medicare or Medicaid.

What does Enbrel cost without insurance?

About $7,100 a month for the brand at list price. A biosimilar is cheaper, and uninsured income-eligible patients can get Enbrel free through the Amgen Safety Net Foundation.

Is the biosimilar as good as Enbrel?

Yes. Etanercept biosimilars meet the FDA's strict standards and are used interchangeably under a prescriber's guidance.
