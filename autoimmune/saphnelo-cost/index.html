Saphnelo cost, coupon & copay card

Saphnelo (anifrolumab) is an intravenous type I interferon receptor antagonist used for adults with moderate to severe systemic lupus erythematosus who are already on standard lupus therapy.

The short answer

Saphnelo has a list price of about ~$5,483 per infusion, before clinic administration fees. With commercial insurance, the Saphnelo Access 360 Co-pay Program can bring your cost to as little as $0 per infusion, with assistance of up to $16,500/yr plus $150/infusion administration. Medicare usually covers clinic-given infusions like Saphnelo under Part B, so the $2,100 2026 Part D out-of-pocket cap generally does not apply, and uninsured or underinsured patients can apply to the AZ&Me Prescription Savings Program.

Saphnelo is given as an IV infusion in a clinic or infusion center on a recurring schedule, added to standard lupus treatment. It is not a pill you pick up at the pharmacy. Your total cost depends on the drug price, infusion-center administration charges, and whether your plan bills it under the medical benefit, as well as your deductible and coinsurance. Because it is a brand-only biologic with no biosimilar, the list price is high and insurance coverage, often with prior authorization, matters a great deal.

The Saphnelo copay card

With commercial insurance, you may pay as little as $0 per infusion with the Saphnelo Access 360 Co-pay Program.

The Saphnelo Access 360 Co-pay Program helps commercially insured patients cover out-of-pocket costs for the drug. It also helps with infusion administration costs, up to $16,500/yr plus $150/infusion administration. The program is for commercial insurance only. People with Medicare, Medicaid, or other government coverage cannot use it. Enroll through AstraZeneca's patient support site, and ask your infusion center to bill the program after your insurance processes the claim.

Saphnelo cost with Medicare

Saphnelo is not among the drugs with a Medicare-negotiated price. Because it is given by infusion in a clinic, Medicare usually covers it under Part B rather than Part D. Your share is therefore Part B coinsurance after the deductible, which supplemental coverage such as Medigap may help pay. The $2,100 2026 Part D out-of-pocket cap applies only to Part D–covered drugs, so it generally does not limit costs for clinic-administered Saphnelo. Federal rules bar manufacturer copay cards for Medicare patients, but you may still apply for AstraZeneca's patient assistance program.

Patient assistance for Saphnelo

AstraZeneca's AZ&Me Prescription Savings Program helps eligible patients who have no insurance or cannot afford their medicines. Eligibility depends on factors such as income and coverage status, so check the program site or ask your rheumatology office for help applying.

Cash-pay strategies for Saphnelo

Without insurance, Saphnelo costs about ~$5,483 per infusion, and infusion-center administration fees are added on top. Retail pharmacy discount cards rarely help with clinic-administered infusions, so the AZ&Me Prescription Savings Program is usually the most useful option for uninsured patients. If cost remains a barrier, ask your rheumatologist whether other lupus treatments would be appropriate.

Cheaper alternatives to Saphnelo

Saphnelo has no generic or biosimilar. Other lupus treatments include standard oral therapies and other biologic medications, and your rheumatologist can help weigh their effectiveness, coverage, and cost against Saphnelo for your situation.

I tell patients that the price of Saphnelo depends heavily on where it is infused and how it is billed. Hospital-based infusion centers often charge more than office-based ones. I recommend enrolling in the Access 360 program before your first infusion if you have commercial insurance, and confirming that prior authorization is approved. If you have Medicare, ask your plan how Part B coinsurance will apply, since the copay card is not an option.

Saphnelo cost — frequently asked questions

How much does Saphnelo cost with the copay card?

With commercial insurance, the Saphnelo Access 360 Co-pay Program can lower your cost to as little as $0 per infusion. The program covers up to $16,500/yr plus $150/infusion administration.

Is there a generic or biosimilar for Saphnelo?

No. Saphnelo (anifrolumab) is available only as a brand-name biologic, and no biosimilar is available.

How much is Saphnelo without insurance?

The list price is about ~$5,483 per infusion, and infusion administration fees are extra. Uninsured patients can apply to the AZ&Me Prescription Savings Program for help.

Does Medicare cover Saphnelo, and can I use the copay card?

Medicare usually covers clinic-given infusions like Saphnelo under Part B, so your cost depends on your Part B coinsurance and any supplemental coverage. The $2,100 2026 Part D cap generally does not apply to it. Manufacturer copay cards cannot be used with Medicare, but you may apply to AZ&Me.

How do I enroll in the Saphnelo copay program?

Commercially insured patients can enroll through AstraZeneca's patient support site at https://www.azpatientsupport.com/home/commercially-insured.html?brand=SAPHNELO&role=Patient. Ask your infusion center to bill the program after your insurance pays its share.
