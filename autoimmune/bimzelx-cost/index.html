Bimzelx cost, coupon & copay card

Bimzelx (bimekizumab) is a self-injected biologic from UCB that blocks both IL-17A and IL-17F. It is used for adults with plaque psoriasis, psoriatic arthritis, ankylosing spondylitis, or hidradenitis suppurativa.

The short answer

Bimzelx has a list price of ~$8,165 per dose, but commercially insured patients can pay as little as $5 per dose with the Bimzelx Savings Program (not $0). Medicare Part D patients cannot use the copay card, but their 2026 out-of-pocket drug costs are capped at $2,100. Uninsured or underinsured patients can apply to UCBCares for financial assistance.

Bimzelx is a self-administered injection prescribed by dermatologists and rheumatologists for chronic inflammatory conditions, usually after other treatments have been tried. Because it is a brand-only biologic with no biosimilar, the list price is high. What you actually pay depends mainly on your insurance type, your plan's formulary and prior-authorization rules, and whether you qualify for the manufacturer's savings or assistance programs.

The Bimzelx copay card

With commercial insurance, the Bimzelx Savings Program can lower your cost to as little as $5 per dose (not $0).

The Bimzelx Savings Program is UCB's copay support for patients whose commercial or employer insurance covers Bimzelx. It is not available to people with Medicare, Medicaid, or other government insurance. Your plan may still require prior authorization before the card can be applied, so enroll early and ask your prescriber's office to help with coverage paperwork.

Bimzelx cost with Medicare

Bimzelx is not among the drugs with a Medicare negotiated price, so there is no maximum fair price for it. Coverage and cost-sharing vary by Part D plan, but in 2026 your total annual out-of-pocket spending on covered Part D drugs is capped at $2,100. Federal rules bar manufacturer copay cards for Medicare beneficiaries, so the Bimzelx Savings Program cannot be used. Medicare patients who still cannot afford treatment can check whether they qualify for UCBCares.

Patient assistance for Bimzelx

UCBCares is UCB's patient support and financial assistance program. Patients who are uninsured, underinsured, or unable to afford Bimzelx may qualify for help. Eligibility depends on program criteria, so review the requirements and apply through the UCBCares website, ideally with help from your prescriber's office.

Cash-pay strategies for Bimzelx

Without insurance, Bimzelx has a list price of about $8,165 per dose, which puts it out of reach for most people paying cash. Pharmacy discount cards typically save little on specialty biologics like this one, so applying to UCBCares is usually the better first step. If cost remains a barrier, ask your prescriber whether a different covered biologic or a non-biologic therapy is an option.

Cheaper alternatives to Bimzelx

There is no generic or biosimilar for Bimzelx. Depending on your condition and coverage, your clinician may consider other biologics, such as other IL-17 inhibitors, IL-23 inhibitors, or TNF inhibitors, some of which may be preferred on your plan's formulary.

I tell patients to start the savings program enrollment and the prior-authorization process at the same time, because a pending approval is the most common reason a first fill is delayed. I also remind them that the card brings the cost down to $5 per dose, not $0, and that it only works with commercial insurance. Anyone on Medicare should plan around the 2026 out-of-pocket cap and ask about UCBCares instead.

Bimzelx cost — frequently asked questions

How much does Bimzelx cost with the savings card?

With commercial insurance, the Bimzelx Savings Program can lower your cost to as little as $5 per dose. It is not $0, and program terms apply.

Is there a generic or biosimilar for Bimzelx?

No. Bimzelx (bimekizumab) is only available as the brand-name product from UCB, and no biosimilar is currently available.

How much is Bimzelx without insurance?

The list price is about $8,165 per dose. If you are uninsured, you can apply for financial assistance through UCBCares at https://www.ucb-usa.com/Patients/Financial-Assistance.

Does Medicare cover Bimzelx, and can I use the copay card?

Part D coverage and cost-sharing vary by plan, and Bimzelx does not have a Medicare negotiated price. In 2026, out-of-pocket costs for covered Part D drugs are capped at $2,100. Medicare patients cannot use the Bimzelx Savings Program, which is for commercial insurance only.

How do I enroll in the Bimzelx Savings Program?

Enroll through the official Bimzelx Navigate Benefits page at https://www.bimzelx.com/bimzelx-navigate-benefits. You must have commercial insurance; people with Medicare or Medicaid are not eligible.
