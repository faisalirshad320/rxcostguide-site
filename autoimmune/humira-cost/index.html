Humira cost, biosimilars & copay card

Humira (adalimumab) is the original TNF-inhibitor biologic for rheumatoid arthritis, psoriasis, Crohn’s and more — and since 2023 it faces many cheaper adalimumab biosimilars.

The short answer

Brand Humira lists around $7,000 a month (two pens). Since 2023 several adalimumab biosimilars (Amjevita, Cyltezo, Hyrimoz, Yuflyma, Simlandi and others) are available at far lower prices — some through low-cost channels for a few hundred dollars a month. Commercially insured patients can still reach $0 with the Humira Complete card; the biggest savings lever is the biosimilar.

Humira was for years the best-selling drug in the world, and its price reflected that. The arrival of biosimilars in 2023 changed the category: these are near-identical, FDA-approved versions of adalimumab, several designated interchangeable, often at a fraction of brand cost. For most patients the real cost question is now which adalimumab product their plan prefers.

Humira cost with Medicare

Humira itself has no Medicare-negotiated price. For 2026, Medicare patients pay their Part D plan cost-share, capped by the $2,100 annual out-of-pocket maximum. Many Part D plans now place an adalimumab biosimilar on a lower tier than brand Humira, which can reduce cost-share — ask your plan which adalimumab product it prefers.

Cash-pay strategies for Humira

Do not pay brand cash price. Ask your prescriber and pharmacist about an adalimumab biosimilar — several are interchangeable, and some are sold through low-cost channels for a few hundred dollars a month. If uninsured and income-eligible, myAbbVie Assist can provide brand Humira free.

Cheaper alternatives to Humira

The cheapest route is usually an adalimumab biosimilar (Amjevita, Cyltezo, Hyrimoz, Yuflyma, Simlandi and others). Other biologic classes — Rinvoq (oral JAK), Skyrizi and Stelara — treat overlapping conditions differently. See our Rinvoq vs Humira comparison. Switching is a clinical decision.

Humira is the clearest example of why biosimilars matter. They are not generics in the loose sense — they are rigorously evaluated, highly similar biologics, and several are formally interchangeable with Humira. When a patient tells me their Humira is unaffordable, the first move is almost always to ask the prescriber whether a biosimilar the plan prefers will do, because the cost difference can be enormous.

Humira cost — frequently asked questions

Is there a generic or biosimilar for Humira?

There is no generic, but several adalimumab biosimilars have been available since 2023 (Amjevita, Cyltezo, Hyrimoz, Yuflyma, Simlandi and more). Some are interchangeable and much cheaper than brand Humira.

How much does Humira cost with insurance?

Commercially insured patients can pay as little as $0 with the Humira Complete card, but your plan may prefer a cheaper biosimilar. Medicare patients pay plan cost-share under the $2,100 cap.

What is the cheapest way to get adalimumab?

Usually a biosimilar your plan prefers, or a biosimilar through a low-cost pharmacy channel (some around $550/month). For brand, the Complete card (commercial) or myAbbVie Assist (uninsured).

Are Humira biosimilars as effective?

Biosimilars are FDA-approved as highly similar to Humira with no clinically meaningful differences; several are designated interchangeable. Whether to use one is a decision for your prescriber.

What does Humira cost without insurance?

Brand Humira is about $7,000 a month. A biosimilar is far cheaper, and income-eligible uninsured patients can get brand Humira free through myAbbVie Assist.
