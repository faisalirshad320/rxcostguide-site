Opzelura cost, coupon & copay card

Opzelura (ruxolitinib cream) is a topical JAK inhibitor from Incyte, prescribed for atopic dermatitis (eczema) and nonsegmental vitiligo.

The short answer

Opzelura has a list price of ~$2,094 per 60 g tube. With commercial insurance, you may pay as little as $0 per tube with the Opzelura Copay Savings Program. On Medicare Part D, the card can't be used, but your total out-of-pocket drug costs are capped at $2,100 in 2026. If you can't afford it, IncyteCARES for Opzelura may be able to help.

Opzelura is a cream applied to affected skin. It is used for atopic dermatitis and for repigmentation in nonsegmental vitiligo. Your cost depends mainly on your insurance type, your plan's formulary tier and prior authorization rules, and how many tubes you need, which depends on how much skin you treat. There is no generic or biosimilar, so the brand list price drives most out-of-pocket costs.

The Opzelura copay card

Commercially insured patients may pay as little as $0 per tube with the Opzelura Copay Savings Program.

The Opzelura Copay Savings Program from Incyte lowers what you pay at the pharmacy when you have commercial (private or employer) insurance. The program can be used once per 25 days. It isn't available to people with Medicare, Medicaid, or other government insurance. Your plan may still need a prior authorization before Opzelura is covered. The card then reduces your remaining copay or coinsurance.

Opzelura cost with Medicare

Opzelura isn't one of the drugs with a Medicare-negotiated price, so your Part D cost depends on your plan's formulary tier and coinsurance. In 2026, total Part D out-of-pocket drug spending is capped at $2,100 a year, which limits your yearly cost if your plan covers it. Federal rules bar manufacturer copay cards for Medicare patients. If you have Medicare and can't afford it, ask about IncyteCARES for Opzelura or the Medicare Prescription Payment Plan, which spreads costs across monthly payments.

Patient assistance for Opzelura

IncyteCARES for Opzelura is Incyte's patient support program. It helps with insurance benefits checks and prior authorization support. It also offers financial assistance options, including help for eligible uninsured or underinsured patients. Eligibility and income criteria apply, so check the IncyteCARES site or ask your dermatology office to start an application.

Cash-pay strategies for Opzelura

Without insurance, Opzelura is expensive at a list price of ~$2,094 per 60 g tube. Pharmacy discount cards may lower the cash price a little. For most uninsured patients, though, applying to IncyteCARES for Opzelura is the more meaningful option. There is no generic version. Ask your clinician whether a lower-cost topical treatment could be appropriate for your condition.

Cheaper alternatives to Opzelura

There is no generic or biosimilar version of Opzelura. Depending on the condition, alternatives may include generic topical corticosteroids, topical calcineurin inhibitors, other nonsteroidal topicals, phototherapy, or systemic therapies. Your clinician can help weigh cost against effectiveness and safety.

I tell patients to apply only a thin layer to the affected areas. That follows the label and safety guidance, and it also makes each tube last longer, which matters at this price. I also suggest checking prior authorization status before the first fill, because a denial or delay is the most common reason people face a surprise bill at the pharmacy. For commercially insured patients, I recommend enrolling in the copay program before going to the pharmacy. For Medicare patients, I point them to IncyteCARES.

Opzelura cost — frequently asked questions

How much does Opzelura cost with the copay card?

With commercial insurance, you may pay as little as $0 per tube with the Opzelura Copay Savings Program. The card can be used once per 25 days, and program terms apply.

Is there a generic or biosimilar for Opzelura?

No. There is currently no generic or biosimilar for Opzelura (ruxolitinib cream), so only the brand-name product is available.

How much is Opzelura without insurance?

The list price is ~$2,094 per 60 g tube. Discount cards may lower the cash price somewhat. Uninsured patients should look into IncyteCARES for Opzelura for financial assistance.

Does Medicare cover Opzelura, and can I use the copay card?

Coverage and cost vary by Part D plan. Medicare patients can't use the manufacturer copay card. In 2026, your total Part D out-of-pocket costs are capped at $2,100 for the year, and IncyteCARES may offer extra help if you qualify.

How do I enroll in the Opzelura Copay Savings Program?

You can enroll online at https://www.opzelura.com/atopic-dermatitis/copay-savings-program. You must have commercial insurance; the program is not available with Medicare or Medicaid. After enrolling, give the card information to your pharmacy when you fill your prescription.
