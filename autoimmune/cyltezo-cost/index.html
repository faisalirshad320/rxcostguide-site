Cyltezo cost, coupon & copay card

Cyltezo (adalimumab-adbm) is an interchangeable Humira biosimilar from Boehringer Ingelheim, used for rheumatoid arthritis, psoriatic arthritis, Crohn's disease, plaque psoriasis, and ulcerative colitis.

The short answer

Cyltezo's list price is about ~$3,500 per 2-pen kit, which is below Humira's list price. With commercial insurance, you may pay as little as $0 per prescription through the CARECONNECT4ME Copay Program. On Medicare Part D, the copay card cannot be used, but your total out-of-pocket drug spending is capped at $2,100 in 2026.

Cyltezo is a self-injected TNF inhibitor that works the same way as Humira and is FDA-designated as interchangeable, so pharmacists may be able to substitute it for Humira depending on state rules. As a biologic, it is expensive, and what you actually pay depends mostly on your insurance type, your plan's formulary placement, and whether you qualify for the manufacturer's copay program.

The Cyltezo copay card

Boehringer Ingelheim's CARECONNECT4ME Copay Program helps lower out-of-pocket costs for eligible patients with commercial or private insurance. Maximum per-fill and annual benefit limits are not specified here, so confirm the current terms when you enroll. The program cannot be used with Medicare, Medicaid, or other government insurance.

Cyltezo cost with Medicare

Cyltezo is not on the list of drugs with a Medicare-negotiated price. Coverage and cost-sharing vary by Part D plan, and federal rules bar manufacturer copay cards for Medicare beneficiaries. In 2026, Part D caps your total annual out-of-pocket spending on covered drugs at $2,100, which limits what you pay for an expensive biologic like Cyltezo.

Cash-pay strategies for Cyltezo

Without insurance, Cyltezo's list price is about ~$3,500 per 2-pen kit, which is below Humira's list price but still high. No manufacturer free-drug program is listed, so it's worth comparing pharmacy cash prices, checking discount cards, and asking your prescriber whether another adalimumab product would cost you less.

Cheaper alternatives to Cyltezo

Alternatives include brand-name Humira and other adalimumab biosimilars, as well as other biologic or targeted therapies for autoimmune disease; which option is cheapest for you depends largely on your plan's formulary.

I tell patients that Cyltezo is the same molecule as Humira and is designated interchangeable, so switching for cost reasons is usually reasonable. I also remind commercially insured patients to enroll in the copay program before their first fill, and Medicare patients to plan around the annual out-of-pocket cap rather than look for a coupon.

Cyltezo cost — frequently asked questions

How much does Cyltezo cost with the copay card?

With commercial insurance, you may pay as little as $0 per prescription with the CARECONNECT4ME Copay Program. The program is not available to people with Medicare or Medicaid.

Is Cyltezo a generic or a biosimilar?

Cyltezo (adalimumab-adbm) is a biosimilar, not a generic. It is an interchangeable biosimilar to Humira, which means a pharmacist may be able to substitute it for Humira depending on state law.

How much is Cyltezo without insurance?

The list price is about ~$3,500 per 2-pen kit, which is below Humira's list price. No manufacturer free-drug program is listed, so compare pharmacy cash prices and ask your prescriber about lower-cost options.

Does Medicare cover Cyltezo, and can I use the copay card?

Coverage depends on your Part D plan. Manufacturer copay cards cannot be used with Medicare, but in 2026 your total out-of-pocket spending on covered Part D drugs is capped at $2,100. Cyltezo does not have a Medicare-negotiated price.

How do I enroll in the Cyltezo copay program?

You can enroll in the CARECONNECT4ME Copay Program through Boehringer Ingelheim's patient support page at https://patient.boehringer-ingelheim.com/us/products/cyltezo/patient-support/accessing-patient-support. You must have commercial insurance to qualify.
