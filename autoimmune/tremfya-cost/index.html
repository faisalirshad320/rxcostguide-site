Tremfya cost, coupon & copay card

Tremfya (guselkumab) is an injectable IL-23 inhibitor biologic used to treat adults with plaque psoriasis, psoriatic arthritis, Crohn's disease, and ulcerative colitis.

The short answer

Tremfya's list price is about ~$13,000 per dose (every 8 weeks maintenance), but people with commercial insurance can pay as little as $0 per dose with Tremfya withMe. If you have Medicare Part D, the copay card cannot be used, but your total out-of-pocket drug spending is capped at $2,100 in 2026. Uninsured or underinsured patients can apply to the Johnson & Johnson Patient Assistance Foundation (J&J withMe).

After initial dosing, Tremfya is typically given as a subcutaneous injection every 8 weeks for maintenance, and many patients can self-inject at home. Because it is a brand-name biologic with no biosimilar available, the list price is high and what you actually pay depends mostly on your insurance type, your plan's specialty-tier rules, and whether you qualify for manufacturer support.

The Tremfya copay card

With commercial insurance, the Tremfya withMe program can lower your cost to as little as $0 per dose.

Tremfya withMe is Johnson & Johnson's cost-support program for patients with commercial or private insurance, including employer and marketplace plans. The program helps pay your deductible, copay, or coinsurance for Tremfya, subject to program maximums and terms. It cannot be used with Medicare, Medicaid, or other government insurance. Enroll online before your next fill, and confirm your specialty pharmacy has your program information on file.

Tremfya cost with Medicare

Tremfya is not among the drugs with a Medicare-negotiated price, so your cost depends on your Part D plan's formulary and specialty-tier cost-sharing. In 2026, total out-of-pocket spending on covered Part D drugs is capped at $2,100 for the year, and because Tremfya is expensive, many beneficiaries will reach that cap early in the year. You can also ask your plan about spreading payments across the year through the Medicare Prescription Payment Plan. Federal rules bar manufacturer copay cards for Medicare patients, but you may still apply to the Johnson & Johnson Patient Assistance Foundation if you meet its criteria.

Patient assistance for Tremfya

The Johnson & Johnson Patient Assistance Foundation may provide Tremfya at no cost to eligible patients who are uninsured or whose insurance does not adequately cover the drug, based on household income and other criteria. Applications usually require income documentation and a section completed by your prescriber. Start at J&J withMe, which can also check your benefits and point you to other support options.

Cash-pay strategies for Tremfya

Without insurance, Tremfya's list price is about ~$13,000 per dose (every 8 weeks maintenance), and retail discount cards rarely reduce specialty biologic prices meaningfully. The best route is usually the Johnson & Johnson Patient Assistance Foundation, and it is also worth checking whether you qualify for marketplace or Medicaid coverage. No biosimilar of Tremfya is available, so ask your clinician whether a lower-cost treatment option fits your condition.

Cheaper alternatives to Tremfya

Other options include different IL-23 inhibitors, IL-17 inhibitors, and TNF inhibitors. Some TNF inhibitors, such as adalimumab, now have biosimilars that may cost less depending on your plan. The right choice depends on your diagnosis, prior treatments, and formulary coverage.

I tell patients not to start a biologic like Tremfya until the cost support is set up, because the first specialty pharmacy bill can be startling if the copay card or prior authorization isn't in place. For those with commercial insurance, I ask them to enroll in Tremfya withMe the same day we send the prescription. For my Medicare patients, I remind them that the $2,100 annual cap is real protection, and we plan for an early-year spending peak.

Tremfya cost — frequently asked questions

How much does Tremfya cost with the copay card?

With commercial insurance, the Tremfya withMe program can lower your cost to as little as $0 per dose. Program maximums apply per prescription per year, and the card cannot be used with Medicare or Medicaid.

Is there a generic or biosimilar for Tremfya?

No. There is currently no generic or biosimilar version of Tremfya (guselkumab) available, so it is sold only as the brand-name product.

How much is Tremfya without insurance?

Tremfya's list price is about ~$13,000 per dose (every 8 weeks maintenance). Uninsured patients should apply to the Johnson & Johnson Patient Assistance Foundation through J&J withMe, which may provide the drug at no cost to people who qualify.

Does Medicare cover Tremfya, and what will I pay?

Many Medicare Part D plans cover Tremfya, but cost-sharing varies by plan. Tremfya is not a Medicare-negotiated drug. In 2026, your total out-of-pocket Part D spending is capped at $2,100 for the year. Manufacturer copay cards cannot be used with Medicare.

How do I enroll in the Tremfya withMe savings program?

If you have commercial insurance, enroll online at https://www.jnjwithme.com/patient/tremfya/withme/cost-support/. Medicare and Medicaid patients are not eligible for the copay program but may apply for patient assistance at https://www.jnjwithme.com/.
