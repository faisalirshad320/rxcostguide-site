Inflectra cost, coupon & copay card

Inflectra (infliximab-dyyb) is a Pfizer biosimilar of Remicade, an IV-infused TNF inhibitor used for rheumatoid arthritis, Crohn's disease, ulcerative colitis, ankylosing spondylitis, psoriatic arthritis and plaque psoriasis.

The short answer

Inflectra's list price is about ~$932 per 100 mg vial (biosimilar), and most infusions need more than one vial, plus facility and administration fees. With commercial insurance, the Inflectra Pfizer enCompass Co-Pay Program can bring your cost to as little as $0 per prescription, with a program maximum of $3,000–$12,000/yr. Medicare patients cannot use the co-pay card; because Inflectra is an infusion, it is usually billed under Medicare Part B, so the $2,100 2026 Part D out-of-pocket cap generally does not apply to it.

Inflectra is given as an intravenous infusion in a clinic, hospital outpatient department or infusion center, usually on a loading schedule followed by maintenance doses. The total cost depends on your weight-based dose (which sets how many vials you need), the infusion site's administration and facility charges, and your insurance plan's deductible and coinsurance. Because it is a biosimilar, some insurers prefer it over the reference product, Remicade.

The Inflectra copay card

Commercially insured patients may pay as little as $0 per prescription with the Inflectra Pfizer enCompass Co-Pay Program.

The program helps cover out-of-pocket drug costs for eligible patients with commercial or private insurance, up to an annual program maximum. It is not available to patients with Medicare, Medicaid or other government insurance. Ask your infusion center whether the program also applies to administration costs, and check the official program site for current terms.

Inflectra cost with Medicare

Inflectra has not been selected for Medicare drug price negotiation. Because it is infused in a clinical setting, it is usually covered under Medicare Part B rather than Part D, so your cost depends on Part B coinsurance and any supplemental (Medigap) or Medicare Advantage coverage. The $2,100 2026 Part D out-of-pocket cap applies only to Part D drugs and generally will not limit Inflectra costs. Federal rules bar manufacturer co-pay cards for Medicare patients.

Cash-pay strategies for Inflectra

Without insurance, the list price is about ~$932 per 100 mg vial (biosimilar), and infusion facility and administration fees are added on top. Because Inflectra is given in a clinic rather than picked up at a retail pharmacy, pharmacy discount cards usually do not apply; ask the infusion center for a self-pay estimate and about payment plans or financial assistance. Comparing prices across infusion sites can make a meaningful difference.

Cheaper alternatives to Inflectra

Inflectra is itself a lower-cost biosimilar of Remicade; the reference product and other infliximab products may be options depending on your insurer's preferred list. Other TNF inhibitors and advanced therapies exist, and your prescriber can help choose one your plan covers.

I tell patients to ask for a benefits verification before the first infusion, because the site of care can change the bill as much as the drug itself. I also remind commercially insured patients to enroll in the co-pay program before treatment starts, and Medicare patients to review their Part B and supplemental coverage instead, since co-pay cards do not apply to them.

Inflectra cost — frequently asked questions

How much does Inflectra cost with the co-pay card?

With commercial insurance, the Inflectra Pfizer enCompass Co-Pay Program can lower your cost to as little as $0 per prescription, up to a program maximum of $3,000–$12,000/yr.

Is there a generic or biosimilar for Inflectra?

Inflectra is itself a biosimilar of Remicade (infliximab). Biologic drugs do not have traditional generics; biosimilars serve that role.

How much does Inflectra cost without insurance?

The list price is about ~$932 per 100 mg vial (biosimilar). Most doses require more than one vial, and infusion facility and administration fees are extra, so the total per infusion is higher.

Does Medicare cover Inflectra, and can I use the co-pay card?

Inflectra is usually covered under Medicare Part B because it is infused in a clinic, so the $2,100 2026 Part D cap generally does not apply. The co-pay card cannot be used with Medicare or Medicaid.

How do I enroll in the Inflectra co-pay program?

Enroll through Pfizer enCompass at https://www.pfizerencompass.com/patient/inflectra/copay-support. You must have commercial insurance; Medicare and Medicaid patients are not eligible.
