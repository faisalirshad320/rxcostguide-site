Benlysta cost, coupon & copay card

Benlysta (belimumab) is a biologic BLyS-specific inhibitor from GSK used alongside standard therapy for adults and children with systemic lupus erythematosus or lupus nephritis.

The short answer

Benlysta's list price is ~$1,300/wk SC (about $5,200/mo), but people with commercial insurance may pay as little as $0 per prescription with the Benlysta Co-pay Program (an annual maximum applies). On Medicare Part D, the copay card can't be used, but total out-of-pocket drug spending is capped at $2,100 in 2026. Uninsured or underinsured patients can apply for help through Together with GSK.

Benlysta is given either as a weekly subcutaneous self-injection at home or as an intravenous infusion in a clinic, and it is used long term alongside standard lupus treatments. Because it is a brand-name biologic with no generic or biosimilar, the list price is high, and what you actually pay depends heavily on your insurance type, deductible, and whether you get the self-injected or infused form.

The Benlysta copay card

Commercially insured patients may pay as little as $0 per prescription with the Benlysta Co-pay Program.

The Benlysta Co-pay Program helps cover out-of-pocket costs for eligible patients with commercial or private insurance. An annual maximum applies, so once the program's yearly benefit is used up, you may owe your plan's usual cost share. The program is not available to people with Medicare, Medicaid, or other government insurance.

Benlysta cost with Medicare

Benlysta is not on the list of drugs with a Medicare-negotiated price. The self-injected form is generally covered under Part D, where your costs vary by plan but total out-of-pocket drug spending is capped at $2,100 in 2026; the IV form given in a clinic is typically billed under Part B instead. Federal rules bar manufacturer copay cards for Medicare patients, so those who need more help should look at Extra Help (Low-Income Subsidy) or Together with GSK.

Patient assistance for Benlysta

Together with GSK offers support for Benlysta patients, including help understanding insurance coverage and assistance options for those who are uninsured or underinsured. Eligibility depends on factors such as insurance status and household income, so check the program site or ask your rheumatology office to help you apply.

Cash-pay strategies for Benlysta

Without insurance, Benlysta's list price is ~$1,300/wk SC (about $5,200/mo), and pharmacy discount cards rarely make a meaningful dent in the cost of specialty biologics like this one. The best first step for uninsured patients is usually applying to Together with GSK. Because there is no generic or biosimilar, there is currently no cheaper version of belimumab.

Cheaper alternatives to Benlysta

There is no generic or biosimilar belimumab, so switching usually means moving to a different lupus therapy rather than a cheaper copy. Other biologic and non-biologic treatments for lupus and lupus nephritis exist, and your rheumatologist or nephrologist can weigh their costs and suitability for you.

I tell patients to sort out coverage before the first dose, because prior authorization for Benlysta is common and the specialty pharmacy or infusion center often won't proceed without it. If you have commercial insurance, enroll in the copay program right away and keep an eye on the annual maximum so a late-year bill doesn't catch you off guard. If you're on Medicare, remember the copay card won't apply, but the 2026 out-of-pocket cap and Together with GSK can still make treatment affordable.

Benlysta cost — frequently asked questions

How much does Benlysta cost with the copay card?

With commercial insurance, the Benlysta Co-pay Program can lower your cost to as little as $0 per prescription. An annual maximum applies, and the program is not available to Medicare or Medicaid patients.

Is there a generic or biosimilar for Benlysta?

No. Benlysta (belimumab) is a brand-name biologic, and there is currently no generic or biosimilar version available.

How much is Benlysta without insurance?

The list price is ~$1,300/wk SC (about $5,200/mo). Uninsured patients should look into Together with GSK, which may provide assistance to those who qualify.

Does Medicare cover Benlysta?

Medicare generally covers Benlysta: the self-injected form under Part D and the IV infusion typically under Part B. Part D costs vary by plan, but out-of-pocket drug spending is capped at $2,100 in 2026. The manufacturer copay card cannot be used with Medicare.

How do I enroll in the Benlysta Co-pay Program?

You can enroll online at https://www.benlystacopayprogram.com/patient-co-pay-card-enroll. You must have commercial insurance; people with Medicare or Medicaid are not eligible.
