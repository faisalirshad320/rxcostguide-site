Otezla cost, coupon & copay card

Otezla (apremilast) is an oral pill (not a biologic) for psoriasis and psoriatic arthritis — with a Medicare-negotiated price coming in 2027.

The short answer

Otezla costs about $4,200 a month at list price. Commercially insured patients can pay as little as $0 with the Otezla copay card. Otezla is in the second Medicare negotiation cycle, with a negotiated price effective 2027; until then Medicare patients rely on the $2,100 Part D cap.

Otezla cost with Medicare

Otezla is in the second Medicare negotiation cycle, with a negotiated price effective 1 January 2027. Until then, Part D members pay plan cost-share up to the $2,100 annual cap. Copay cards cannot be used with Medicare.

Patient assistance for Otezla

Amgen provides Otezla free to uninsured, income-eligible patients through its Safety Net Foundation.

Amgen SupportPlus / Safety Net Foundation

Cash-pay strategies for Otezla

Uninsured, income-eligible patients should apply to the Amgen Safety Net Foundation. Because Otezla is oral and doesn't need lab monitoring, it's sometimes chosen partly for lower overall treatment cost versus biologics — discuss with your dermatologist.

Cheaper alternatives to Otezla

Biologics (Cosentyx, Taltz, Skyrizi) are more potent for moderate-to-severe disease but more expensive and injected. Otezla's appeal is oral dosing and no routine monitoring; the trade-off is generally more modest efficacy.

Otezla occupies a useful middle ground — an oral drug without the monitoring burden of biologics. For patients wary of injections or frequent blood tests, it can lower the *total* cost and hassle of treatment even if the drug itself isn't cheap. That whole-picture view is worth raising with the prescriber.

Otezla cost — frequently asked questions

How much is Otezla without insurance?

About $4,200 a month at list price. Uninsured, income-eligible patients can get it free through the Amgen Safety Net Foundation.

How do I get Otezla for $0?

With commercial insurance, the Otezla copay card can bring you to as little as $0. Not usable with Medicare or Medicaid.

When will Otezla have a Medicare price?

Otezla is in the second negotiation cycle, with a Medicare-negotiated price effective 1 January 2027. Until then, the $2,100 Part D cap applies.

Is there a generic for Otezla?

Not yet widely in the US. The copay card, Part D cap, or patient assistance are the routes to lower cost.

Is Otezla a biologic?

No. Otezla is an oral small-molecule PDE4 inhibitor, not an injectable biologic, and it doesn't require the routine lab monitoring some biologics do.
