Kevzara cost, coupon & copay card

Kevzara (sarilumab) is a self-injected IL-6 receptor antagonist used to treat adults with rheumatoid arthritis and polymyalgia rheumatica.

The short answer

Kevzara has a list price of ~$2,094 per dose (every 2 weeks), so the cash cost is high. With commercial insurance, the KevzaraConnect Copay Card can bring your cost down to as little as $0 per month (up to $15,000/yr, 12 uses). On Medicare Part D, copay cards can't be used, but your annual out-of-pocket drug spending is capped at $2,100 in 2026.

Kevzara is a biologic given as a subcutaneous injection every 2 weeks, typically for people whose rheumatoid arthritis has not responded well enough to other treatments, or for polymyalgia rheumatica when steroids alone are not enough. Because it is a brand-name biologic with no biosimilar, its list price is high, and what you actually pay depends mostly on your insurance type, your plan's specialty-drug tier, and whether you can use the manufacturer's copay card.

The Kevzara copay card

Commercially insured patients may pay as little as $0 per month with the KevzaraConnect Copay Card.

The KevzaraConnect Copay Card helps cover out-of-pocket costs for Kevzara for people with commercial (private or employer) insurance. The program benefit is up to $15,000/yr, 12 uses. It is not available to people with Medicare, Medicaid, or other government insurance. Kevzara cost with Medicare

Kevzara is not among the drugs with a Medicare-negotiated price, so your cost depends on your Part D plan's formulary and tier. In 2026, Medicare Part D caps total out-of-pocket drug spending at $2,100 per year, which limits what you pay for a high-cost biologic like Kevzara. Federal rules bar manufacturer copay cards for Medicare patients, but you may be able to spread costs across the year with the Medicare Prescription Payment Plan or check eligibility for Extra Help.

Patient assistance for Kevzara

KevzaraConnect is the manufacturer's support program for Kevzara. It can help with insurance verification, copay support for eligible commercially insured patients, and information about other financial assistance options for people who are uninsured or underinsured. Eligibility rules apply, so contact the program directly to review your situation.

Cash-pay strategies for Kevzara

Without insurance, Kevzara's list price is ~$2,094 per dose (every 2 weeks), and pharmacy discount cards usually offer limited savings on specialty biologics. Your best first step is to contact KevzaraConnect about financial assistance. You can also ask your rheumatologist whether a lower-cost treatment option is appropriate for you.

Cheaper alternatives to Kevzara

There is no biosimilar for Kevzara. Alternatives may include another IL-6 receptor antagonist, other biologic or targeted oral therapies for rheumatoid arthritis, or conventional DMARDs, and your rheumatologist can help compare cost and coverage on your plan.

I tell patients to enroll in the copay card and confirm prior authorization before the first shipment, because specialty biologics are often delayed by paperwork rather than cost. I also remind them that Kevzara requires periodic blood work, such as blood counts, liver tests, and lipids, so keeping lab appointments helps avoid treatment interruptions. If you switch to Medicare, plan ahead because the copay card will no longer apply.

Kevzara cost — frequently asked questions

How much does Kevzara cost with the copay card?

With commercial insurance, you may pay as little as $0 per month with the KevzaraConnect Copay Card. The benefit is up to $15,000/yr, 12 uses.

Is there a generic or biosimilar for Kevzara?

No. There is currently no generic or biosimilar version of Kevzara (sarilumab).

How much does Kevzara cost without insurance?

The list price is ~$2,094 per dose (every 2 weeks). If you are uninsured, contact KevzaraConnect to ask about financial assistance.

Does Medicare cover Kevzara, and can I use the copay card?

Many Medicare Part D plans cover Kevzara, but your cost varies by plan. Out-of-pocket drug costs are capped at $2,100 in 2026. The KevzaraConnect Copay Card cannot be used with Medicare or Medicaid.

How do I enroll in the KevzaraConnect Copay Card?

Enroll online at https://www.kevzara.com/ra/resources/kevzaraconnect-copay-card. You must have commercial insurance; Medicare and Medicaid patients are not eligible.
