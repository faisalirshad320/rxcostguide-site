Amjevita cost, coupon & copay card

Amjevita (adalimumab-atto) is an Amgen biosimilar of Humira, a subcutaneous TNF inhibitor used for rheumatoid arthritis, psoriatic arthritis, Crohn's disease, plaque psoriasis, and ulcerative colitis.

The short answer

Amjevita has a list price of ~$684 per 40 mg pen (biosimilar; varies). With commercial insurance, eligible patients may pay as little as $0 per monthly fill with Amjevita SupportPlus. On Medicare Part D, the copay card cannot be used, but your out-of-pocket drug spending is capped at $2,100 in 2026.

Amjevita is a self-injected TNF inhibitor that is typically taken on an ongoing basis to control autoimmune inflammation, so costs recur throughout the year. Your actual price depends mainly on your insurance type, your plan's formulary placement of Humira biosimilars, and whether you qualify for the manufacturer's copay program.

The Amjevita copay card

The Amjevita SupportPlus copay card can lower your cost to as little as $0 per monthly fill.

Amgen's Amjevita SupportPlus program helps commercially insured patients reduce their out-of-pocket cost for Amjevita. Eligibility and benefit terms are set by the manufacturer and may change, so confirm the current details when you enroll. The program is not available to people with Medicare, Medicaid, or other government insurance.

Amjevita cost with Medicare

Amjevita is not among the drugs with a Medicare-negotiated price. Coverage and cost-sharing vary by Part D plan, but in 2026 your total out-of-pocket spending on covered Part D drugs is capped at $2,100 for the year. Federal rules bar manufacturer copay cards for Medicare beneficiaries, so Amjevita SupportPlus cannot be used with Medicare coverage.

Cash-pay strategies for Amjevita

Without insurance, Amjevita's list price is about ~$684 per 40 mg pen (biosimilar; varies), so the yearly cost can be high. Pharmacy discount cards may lower the cash price somewhat, and because no manufacturer free-drug program is listed, it is worth asking your prescriber whether another adalimumab product or covered alternative is less expensive for you.

Cheaper alternatives to Amjevita

Amjevita is one of several Humira (adalimumab) biosimilars, and insurers often prefer one adalimumab product over others. If Amjevita is not on your plan's formulary, ask your prescriber about the preferred adalimumab product or another TNF inhibitor your plan covers.

I tell patients that with adalimumab biosimilars, the best price usually comes from matching the product to what their insurer prefers, not from the list price. If you have commercial insurance, enroll in the copay program before your first fill so you are not surprised at the pharmacy. If you are on Medicare, plan for the $2,100 annual out-of-pocket cap, since copay cards cannot help you.

Amjevita cost — frequently asked questions

How much does Amjevita cost with the copay card?

With commercial insurance, eligible patients may pay as little as $0 per monthly fill with Amjevita SupportPlus. The card is not available to Medicare or Medicaid patients.

Is Amjevita a generic or biosimilar?

Amjevita (adalimumab-atto) is a biosimilar of Humira (adalimumab). Biologics do not have true generics; biosimilars are their lower-cost counterparts.

How much is Amjevita without insurance?

The list price is ~$684 per 40 mg pen (biosimilar; varies). No manufacturer patient-assistance (free-drug) program is listed, so ask your prescriber or pharmacist about discount cards or lower-cost covered alternatives.

Does Medicare cover Amjevita, and what will I pay?

Coverage and cost-sharing vary by Part D plan. Amjevita does not have a Medicare-negotiated price, but in 2026 your Part D out-of-pocket costs are capped at $2,100 for the year. The copay card cannot be used with Medicare.

How do I enroll in the Amjevita copay card?

You can enroll through the official Amjevita SupportPlus page at https://www.amjevita.com/amjevita-copay-card. You must have commercial insurance; Medicare and Medicaid patients are not eligible.
