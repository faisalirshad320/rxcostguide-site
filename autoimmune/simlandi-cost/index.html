Simlandi cost, coupon & copay card

Simlandi (adalimumab-ryvk) is an interchangeable biosimilar of Humira, a self-injected TNF inhibitor for adults and eligible patients with rheumatoid arthritis, psoriatic arthritis, ankylosing spondylitis, Crohn's disease, ulcerative colitis, and plaque psoriasis.

The short answer

Simlandi's list price is ~$551 per kit (biosimilar; below Humira). With commercial insurance, the Simlandi Savings Program can bring your cost to as little as $0 per prescription, subject to program terms. On Medicare Part D, the copay card cannot be used, but your annual out-of-pocket drug costs are capped at $2,100 in 2026.

Simlandi is used the same way as Humira: it is injected under the skin to calm the overactive immune response behind several autoimmune conditions, usually as long-term maintenance therapy. Because it is an interchangeable biosimilar, pharmacists may be able to substitute it for Humira where state law allows. What you actually pay depends mostly on your insurance type, your plan's formulary placement and deductible, and whether you can use the manufacturer savings program.

The Simlandi copay card

Commercially insured patients may pay as little as $0 per prescription with the Simlandi Savings Program.

The Simlandi Savings Program is the manufacturer's copay assistance for patients whose prescriptions are covered by commercial or private insurance. Enrollment is done through the official Simlandi website, and the card is then presented at the pharmacy along with your insurance. Benefit limits and other conditions are set by the program, so review the current terms when you enroll. It cannot be used by patients with Medicare, Medicaid, or other government insurance.

Simlandi cost with Medicare

Simlandi is not among the drugs with a Medicare-negotiated price. Coverage and cost-sharing vary by Part D plan, but in 2026 your total out-of-pocket spending on covered Part D drugs is capped at $2,100 for the year. Federal rules bar manufacturer copay cards, including the Simlandi Savings Program, for people with Medicare. You may be able to spread costs across the year with the Medicare Prescription Payment Plan, and independent foundations may offer additional help.

Cash-pay strategies for Simlandi

Without insurance, Simlandi's list price is ~$551 per kit (biosimilar; below Humira), and actual pharmacy cash prices can vary. Ask several pharmacies for a cash quote and check prescription discount cards. Independent foundations (e.g. HealthWell) may help with costs, but there is no manufacturer free-drug program listed. Because Simlandi is already a lower-cost biosimilar, other adalimumab biosimilars are the main comparison point, and prices differ among them.

Cheaper alternatives to Simlandi

Simlandi is one of several adalimumab biosimilars that work like Humira. Other biosimilars, or different classes of biologic and targeted therapies, may be preferred by your insurer's formulary, so ask your prescriber which option your plan covers best.

I tell patients that an interchangeable biosimilar like Simlandi uses the same active molecule and works the same way as Humira, so a switch is usually about cost and coverage rather than a change in treatment. I also remind them to check which adalimumab product their plan prefers before the first fill, since formulary placement often matters more than list price. Anyone with commercial insurance should enroll in the savings program before picking up the prescription.

Simlandi cost — frequently asked questions

How much does Simlandi cost with the copay card?

With commercial insurance, the Simlandi Savings Program can lower your cost to as little as $0 per prescription. Program terms apply, and the card cannot be used with Medicare or Medicaid.

Is Simlandi a generic or biosimilar of Humira?

Simlandi (adalimumab-ryvk) is an interchangeable biosimilar of Humira. Biologics like adalimumab do not have true generics; biosimilars are the equivalent lower-cost option. The "interchangeable" designation means a pharmacist may substitute it for Humira where state law allows.

How much is Simlandi without insurance?

The list price is ~$551 per kit (biosimilar; below Humira). Actual cash prices vary by pharmacy, so compare quotes and check discount cards. Independent foundations (e.g. HealthWell) may also help; no manufacturer free-drug program is listed.

Does Medicare cover Simlandi, and what will I pay?

Coverage and cost-sharing depend on your Part D plan. Simlandi does not have a Medicare-negotiated price. In 2026, your out-of-pocket spending on covered Part D drugs is capped at $2,100 for the year. The manufacturer copay card cannot be used with Medicare.

How do I enroll in the Simlandi Savings Program?

Enroll online at https://www.simlandi.com/savings-program. You must have commercial insurance; Medicare and Medicaid patients are not eligible. After enrolling, give the card information to your pharmacy along with your insurance.
