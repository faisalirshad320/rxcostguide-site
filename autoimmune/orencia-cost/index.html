Orencia cost, coupon & copay card

Orencia (abatacept) is a biologic T-cell costimulation modulator, given as a weekly self-injection or by IV infusion, for adults with rheumatoid arthritis or psoriatic arthritis and for children with polyarticular juvenile idiopathic arthritis.

The short answer

Orencia has a list price of ~$5,000+/mo, and there is no biosimilar or generic yet. With commercial insurance, the Orencia On Call Co-pay Card can lower your cost to as little as $5 per month (not $0), with up to $15,000/yr in support. On Medicare, copay cards are not allowed, but Part D out-of-pocket drug costs are capped at $2,100 in 2026, and the Bristol Myers Squibb Patient Assistance Foundation may help eligible patients.

Orencia is a long-term maintenance biologic, usually started when conventional disease-modifying drugs such as methotrexate have not controlled inflammatory arthritis well enough. Because it is a branded biologic with no biosimilar competition, the list price is high, and what you actually pay depends heavily on your insurance type, your plan's specialty-tier coinsurance, and whether you use the self-injected or IV form.

The Orencia copay card

The Orencia On Call Co-pay Card can bring your cost down to as little as $5 per month. It does not bring it to $0.

If you have commercial (private or employer) insurance that covers Orencia, the Orencia On Call Co-pay Card can lower your out-of-pocket cost to as little as $5 per month, with up to $15,000/yr in support. The card is not valid for people with Medicare, Medicaid, or other government insurance. Enroll through the official Orencia support and savings page, and check whether your plan uses a copay accumulator or maximizer program, because these can affect how long the annual support lasts.

Orencia cost with Medicare

Orencia is not on the list of drugs with a Medicare-negotiated price. Federal rules bar people on Medicare from using manufacturer copay cards. For self-injected Orencia covered under Part D, your total out-of-pocket drug spending is capped at $2,100 in 2026, and the Medicare Prescription Payment Plan lets you spread those costs into monthly payments. IV Orencia given in a clinic is generally covered under Part B, where your cost depends on your coinsurance and any supplemental (Medigap) coverage. Medicare patients with limited income may also qualify for the BMS Patient Assistance Foundation or Extra Help (Low-Income Subsidy).

Patient assistance for Orencia

The Bristol Myers Squibb Patient Assistance Foundation may provide Orencia at no cost to eligible patients who are uninsured, underinsured, or on Medicare and who meet income requirements. You and your prescriber usually complete the application together, and you will need to submit proof of income. Check bmspaf.org for current eligibility criteria and forms.

Cash-pay strategies for Orencia

Without insurance, Orencia costs about ~$5,000+/mo at list price, and retail discount cards typically make little difference for specialty biologics like this. The most realistic route for uninsured patients is the Bristol Myers Squibb Patient Assistance Foundation. Because no biosimilar exists, also ask your rheumatologist whether a lower-cost treatment option is clinically appropriate for you.

Cheaper alternatives to Orencia

Orencia has no biosimilar or generic. Depending on your diagnosis and treatment history, your rheumatologist may consider other biologics or targeted oral therapies used for inflammatory arthritis, some of which now have lower-cost biosimilar versions.

I tell patients to sort out coverage before the first dose, because prior authorization and specialty-pharmacy routing often take a few weeks. If you have commercial insurance, enroll in the copay card right away and expect a small monthly cost, not a free one. If you're on Medicare, keep the 2026 Part D cap and the BMS foundation in mind, since copay cards are off the table for you.

Orencia cost — frequently asked questions

How much does Orencia cost with the copay card?

With commercial insurance, the Orencia On Call Co-pay Card can lower your cost to as little as $5 per month. It does not bring it to $0. The card provides up to $15,000/yr in support.

Is there a generic or biosimilar for Orencia?

No. There is currently no generic or biosimilar version of abatacept available, so Orencia is only sold as the brand-name product.

How much does Orencia cost without insurance?

The list price is about ~$5,000+/mo. Uninsured patients should look into the Bristol Myers Squibb Patient Assistance Foundation (bmspaf.org), which may provide Orencia at no cost to people who qualify.

Does Medicare cover Orencia, and can I use the copay card?

Medicare generally covers Orencia: Part D for the self-injected form and Part B for clinic-administered IV infusions. You cannot use the manufacturer copay card on Medicare. Part D out-of-pocket drug costs are capped at $2,100 in 2026, and you may also qualify for the BMS Patient Assistance Foundation.

How do I enroll in the Orencia copay program?

Visit the official Orencia support and savings page at https://www.orencia.com/support-savings to enroll in the Orencia On Call Co-pay Card. You must have commercial insurance. People with Medicare or Medicaid are not eligible.
