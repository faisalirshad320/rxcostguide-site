Rinvoq cost, coupon & copay card

Rinvoq (upadacitinib) is an oral JAK inhibitor used across a wide range of autoimmune conditions.

The short answer

Rinvoq costs about $6,900 a month at list price. Commercially insured patients can pay as little as $5 a month with the Rinvoq Complete copay card. Medicare patients pay plan cost-share up to the $2,100 Part D cap; uninsured patients can apply for free medication through AbbVie.

Rinvoq cost with Medicare

Rinvoq is not on the Medicare negotiated-price list. Part D members pay plan cost-share, but the $2,100 annual out-of-pocket cap is the key protection for such an expensive drug. Copay cards cannot be used with Medicare.

Patient assistance for Rinvoq

AbbVie provides Rinvoq free to uninsured, income-eligible patients through myAbbVie Assist.

Cash-pay strategies for Rinvoq

No one should pay the ~$6,900 list price out of pocket if it can be avoided. Uninsured, income-eligible patients should apply to myAbbVie Assist for free medication. Because JAK inhibitors carry boxed warnings, insurers usually require other treatments first — that step-therapy record is often what a prior authorization needs.

Cheaper alternatives to Rinvoq

Other targeted options for these conditions include the IL-17 inhibitors (Cosentyx, Taltz) and TNF inhibitors (Enbrel, Humira and its biosimilars). Which class fits depends on your specific condition and history — a clinical decision for your specialist.

A $6,900 sticker price frightens patients, but the number that matters for a Medicare patient is $2,100 — the annual cap — and for a commercial patient it's $5 with the card. My job is usually to move the conversation from the terrifying list price to the actual out-of-pocket figure, which is almost always manageable once the right program is in place.

Rinvoq cost — frequently asked questions

How much is Rinvoq without insurance?

About $6,900 a month at list price. Uninsured, income-eligible patients can get it free through myAbbVie Assist — very few people pay the list price.

How do I get Rinvoq for $5?

With commercial insurance, the Rinvoq Complete copay card can bring you to as little as $5 a month. Not usable with Medicare or Medicaid.

How do Medicare patients afford Rinvoq?

The 2026 Part D out-of-pocket cap of $2,100 limits your total annual spending on covered drugs, which makes even a drug this expensive predictable on Medicare.

Is there a generic for Rinvoq?

No. Rinvoq is under patent; the copay card, the Part D cap, or patient assistance are the routes to lower cost.

Why does Rinvoq need prior authorization?

As a JAK inhibitor with boxed safety warnings, insurers require documentation that other treatments were tried first. That step-therapy record is usually what a prior authorization needs.
