Cosentyx cost, coupon & copay card

Cosentyx (secukinumab) is an IL-17A inhibitor biologic for psoriasis, psoriatic arthritis and ankylosing spondylitis.

The short answer

Cosentyx costs several thousand dollars a month at list price (roughly $7,000/month-equivalent depending on dosing schedule). Commercially insured patients can pay as little as $0 with the Cosentyx Connect copay card. Medicare patients are protected by the $2,100 Part D cap.

Cosentyx is dosed with a loading phase then maintenance injections, so the monthly-equivalent cost varies through the year. As with all biologics, the list price is rarely what anyone with coverage actually pays.

Cosentyx cost with Medicare

Cosentyx is not on the current Medicare negotiated-price list. Part D members pay plan cost-share up to the $2,100 annual cap, which is the key protection here. Copay cards cannot be used with Medicare.

Patient assistance for Cosentyx

Novartis provides Cosentyx free to uninsured, income-eligible patients through its patient-assistance foundation.

Novartis Patient Assistance Foundation

Cash-pay strategies for Cosentyx

Uninsured, income-eligible patients should apply to the Novartis foundation rather than attempt the list price. Because dosing includes a loading phase, ask how your first few months are priced — the copay card should still cap your share.

Cheaper alternatives to Cosentyx

Taltz (ixekizumab) is another IL-17 inhibitor; TNF inhibitors (Enbrel, Humira biosimilars) and IL-23 inhibitors (Skyrizi, Stelara) are alternatives for overlapping conditions. Choice depends on your diagnosis and history.

With biologics dosed in phases, patients sometimes panic at the loading-dose cost. The copay card caps your share regardless of how many injections a given month contains, so the loading phase shouldn't change what a commercial patient pays. Confirm that with Cosentyx Connect if a big first bill appears.

Cosentyx cost — frequently asked questions

How much is Cosentyx without insurance?

Several thousand dollars a month at list price, varying with the dosing schedule. Uninsured, income-eligible patients can get it free through the Novartis Patient Assistance Foundation.

How do I get Cosentyx for $0?

With commercial insurance, the Cosentyx Connect copay card can bring you to as little as $0. Not usable with Medicare or Medicaid.

Does Medicare cover Cosentyx?

Generally under Part D with prior authorization. The $2,100 annual cap limits your out-of-pocket cost; copay cards are not usable on Medicare.

Is there a biosimilar for Cosentyx?

Not yet widely in the US. For now the copay card, Part D cap, or patient assistance are the routes to lower cost.

Cosentyx or Taltz — which is cheaper?

Both are IL-17 inhibitors whose copay cards reach $0 for commercial patients, so cost is usually similar; coverage on your plan and clinical fit decide it.
