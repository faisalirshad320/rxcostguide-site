Sotyktu cost, coupon & copay card

Sotyktu (deucravacitinib) is a once-daily oral TYK2 inhibitor from Bristol Myers Squibb used to treat adults with plaque psoriasis.

The short answer

Sotyktu has a list price of about ~$7,000/mo, but commercially insured patients can pay $0 per 30-day supply with the Sotyktu 360 Support Copay Program. Medicare Part D enrollees cannot use the copay card, but their out-of-pocket drug costs are capped at $2,100 in 2026. Uninsured or underinsured patients can apply to the Bristol Myers Squibb Patient Assistance Foundation.

Sotyktu is taken as a pill once a day, which makes it an alternative to injectable biologics for people with plaque psoriasis. Its cost is driven by its brand-only status, since no generic or biosimilar version is available, and by insurance factors such as formulary tier, prior authorization requirements, and deductibles. What you actually pay depends far more on your coverage type than on the list price.

The Sotyktu copay card

The Sotyktu 360 Support Copay Program can lower your cost to $0 per 30-day supply if you have commercial insurance.

The copay program is offered by Bristol Myers Squibb to patients whose Sotyktu is covered by a commercial (private or employer) insurance plan. It helps cover the out-of-pocket portion your plan leaves you with, subject to program terms and an annual limit. It cannot be used with Medicare, Medicaid, or other government insurance. Enroll through the official Sotyktu savings page before filling your prescription.

Sotyktu cost with Medicare

Sotyktu is not among the drugs with a Medicare-negotiated price, so there is no maximum fair price in effect. Medicare beneficiaries cannot use manufacturer copay cards under federal rules, but the 2026 Medicare Part D out-of-pocket cap of $2,100 limits what you pay for covered drugs in a calendar year. Your actual cost depends on your plan's formulary and coverage phase, and the Medicare Prescription Payment Plan can spread costs into monthly payments. Low-income beneficiaries may also qualify for Extra Help.

Patient assistance for Sotyktu

The Bristol Myers Squibb Patient Assistance Foundation may provide Sotyktu at no cost to eligible patients who are uninsured or underinsured and meet income requirements. You and your prescriber typically complete an application together. Check the foundation's website for current eligibility criteria and required documents.

Cash-pay strategies for Sotyktu

Without insurance, Sotyktu's list price is high, at about ~$7,000/mo, and pharmacy discount cards usually reduce brand-only specialty drugs only modestly. The Bristol Myers Squibb Patient Assistance Foundation is often the most meaningful option for uninsured patients. Ask your clinician whether a lower-cost treatment is appropriate for you.

Cheaper alternatives to Sotyktu

There is no generic or biosimilar version of Sotyktu. Other plaque psoriasis options include topical therapies, phototherapy, other oral medications, and injectable biologics. Coverage and cost vary by plan, so your clinician can help compare them against your formulary.

I tell patients to enroll in the copay program before their first fill, because a pharmacy counter surprise is the most common reason people abandon a new prescription. Many plans require prior authorization for Sotyktu, so I recommend confirming approval status with your prescriber's office if the pharmacy reports a coverage problem. If you're on Medicare, budget around the annual Part D cap rather than expecting copay card help.

Sotyktu cost — frequently asked questions

How much does Sotyktu cost with the copay card?

With commercial insurance, you can pay $0 per 30-day supply with the Sotyktu 360 Support Copay Program, subject to program terms and annual limits. The program is not available to Medicare or Medicaid patients.

Is there a generic or biosimilar for Sotyktu?

No. Sotyktu (deucravacitinib) is available only as a brand-name medication, and no generic or biosimilar version is available.

How much is Sotyktu without insurance?

Sotyktu's list price is about ~$7,000/mo. Uninsured patients may qualify for free medication through the Bristol Myers Squibb Patient Assistance Foundation at https://www.bmspaf.org/.

Does Medicare cover Sotyktu, and can I use the copay card?

Many Medicare Part D plans cover Sotyktu, but coverage and cost vary by plan. Copay cards cannot be used with Medicare. In 2026, your out-of-pocket costs for covered Part D drugs are capped at $2,100 for the year.

How do I enroll in the Sotyktu copay program?

Enroll on the official Sotyktu savings page at https://www.sotyktu.com/psoriatic-arthritis/savings-and-resources/co-pay-savings. You must have commercial insurance, as the program excludes Medicare and Medicaid.
