Gammagard Liquid cost, coupon & copay card

Gammagard Liquid is a plasma-derived immune globulin (human) used to replace missing antibodies in primary immunodeficiency and to treat immune-mediated nerve disorders such as CIDP and multifocal motor neuropathy.

The short answer

Gammagard Liquid lists at about ~$180 per gram (total varies by dose/weight), so the cost of each infusion depends on your weight and prescribed dose. If you have commercial insurance, the Takeda Co-Pay Assistance Program may cover up to 100% of your out-of-pocket copay, with a maximum of up to $20,000/yr. If you have Medicare, copay cards are not allowed, but drugs billed under Part D count toward the $2,100 out-of-pocket cap in 2026.

Gammagard Liquid is given as an intravenous infusion, or as a subcutaneous infusion for primary immunodeficiency, usually every few weeks and often long term. Because it is priced per gram and dosed by body weight, total cost varies widely from patient to patient. Where the infusion is given (hospital, infusion center, or home) and how your plan covers it, under the medical or pharmacy benefit, also affect what you pay.

The Gammagard Liquid copay card

The Gammagard Takeda Co-Pay Assistance Program can sharply reduce out-of-pocket costs for commercially insured patients.

The Gammagard Takeda Co-Pay Assistance Program may cover up to 100% of out-of-pocket copay for commercially insured patients (not a flat $0). Assistance is capped at up to $20,000/yr. Once that limit is reached, your normal plan cost-sharing applies. Enrollment and program details are handled through Takeda Patient Support.

Gammagard Liquid cost with Medicare

Gammagard Liquid is not among the drugs with a Medicare-negotiated price. What you pay depends on whether your claim is billed under Part B or Part D, which varies with your diagnosis and where you receive the infusion. When it is covered under Part D, your out-of-pocket spending is capped at $2,100 in 2026. Federal rules bar manufacturer copay cards for Medicare patients, so Takeda's copay program cannot be used. Takeda Patient Support may still be able to help you review your coverage.

Patient assistance for Gammagard Liquid

Takeda Patient Support helps patients with benefits verification, coverage questions, and access to financial assistance for Gammagard Liquid. Uninsured or underinsured patients should contact the program directly to see which options they qualify for.

Cash-pay strategies for Gammagard Liquid

Without insurance, Gammagard Liquid costs about ~$180 per gram (total varies by dose/weight), and infusion or administration fees are often extra. Retail discount cards rarely apply to infused, plasma-derived products like this one, so contacting Takeda Patient Support is usually the most useful first step. No generic or biosimilar is available, but other immune globulin brands exist, and your prescriber may consider one if coverage or price is better.

Cheaper alternatives to Gammagard Liquid

There is no generic or biosimilar version of Gammagard Liquid, but several other intravenous and subcutaneous immune globulin products are available from different manufacturers. Your plan may prefer one brand over another, so ask your prescriber which products are covered.

I tell patients that immune globulin is priced per gram, so the dose your clinician sets by body weight is the biggest driver of your bill. I also recommend checking early whether your plan covers infusions under the medical or pharmacy benefit, because that determines your cost-sharing and whether Part D rules apply. If you have commercial insurance, enroll in the copay program before your first infusion so claims are covered from the start.

Gammagard Liquid cost — frequently asked questions

How much does Gammagard Liquid cost with the copay card?

For commercially insured patients, the Takeda Co-Pay Assistance Program may cover up to 100% of your out-of-pocket copay, up to $20,000/yr. It is not a guaranteed flat $0, and any costs beyond the annual maximum are your responsibility under your plan.

Is there a generic or biosimilar for Gammagard Liquid?

No. Gammagard Liquid has no generic or biosimilar. Other immune globulin brands are available, and your prescriber can tell you whether switching makes sense for you.

How much does Gammagard Liquid cost without insurance?

The list price is about ~$180 per gram (total varies by dose/weight). Because dosing is based on body weight, the total per infusion varies a lot. Infusion fees may add to the cost. Takeda Patient Support can review assistance options.

Does Medicare cover Gammagard Liquid, and can I use the copay card?

Medicare may cover it under Part B or Part D, depending on your diagnosis and infusion setting. When it is billed under Part D, out-of-pocket costs are capped at $2,100 in 2026. Manufacturer copay cards cannot be used with Medicare or Medicaid.

How do I enroll in the Gammagard copay program?

You can enroll through Takeda Patient Support at https://www.takedapatientsupport.com/s/. You must have commercial insurance. Patients with Medicare or Medicaid are not eligible.
