Trelegy Ellipta cost, coupon & copay card

Trelegy Ellipta is a once-daily triple-therapy inhaler for COPD and asthma — with a Medicare-negotiated price coming in 2027.

The short answer

Trelegy costs about $650 a month at list price. Commercially insured patients can pay as little as $0 with the Trelegy copay card. Trelegy is in the second Medicare negotiation cycle, with a negotiated price effective 2027; until then Medicare patients use Part D and the $2,100 cap.

Trelegy Ellipta cost with Medicare

Trelegy is in the second Medicare negotiation cycle, with a negotiated price effective 1 January 2027. Until then, Part D members pay plan cost-share up to the $2,100 annual cap. Copay cards cannot be used with Medicare.

Patient assistance for Trelegy Ellipta

GSK provides Trelegy free to uninsured, income-eligible patients through GSK for You.

GSK for You / GSK Patient Assistance

Cash-pay strategies for Trelegy Ellipta

Without insurance, apply to GSK for You if income-eligible, or compare discount cards. Some patients on separate inhalers are switched to Trelegy for convenience — if cost is tight, ask whether individual generic inhalers would be cheaper for you.

Cheaper alternatives to Trelegy Ellipta

Other COPD/asthma inhalers include Symbicort (which has a generic) and Breo Ellipta. Whether triple therapy is needed, or whether cheaper individual inhalers suffice, is a clinical decision for your doctor.

Convenience has a price. Trelegy puts three medicines in one daily puff, which is genuinely easier — but for a cost-sensitive patient, separate inhalers (some with generics) can be cheaper. It's a reasonable question to raise with your prescriber if the copay card isn't enough for your situation.

Trelegy Ellipta cost — frequently asked questions

How much is Trelegy without insurance?

About $650 a month at list price. Uninsured, income-eligible patients can get it free through GSK for You.

How do I get Trelegy for $0?

With commercial insurance, the Trelegy copay card can bring you to as little as $0. Not usable with Medicare or Medicaid.

When will Trelegy have a Medicare price?

Trelegy is in the second negotiation cycle, with a Medicare-negotiated price effective 1 January 2027. Until then the $2,100 Part D cap applies.

Is there a generic for Trelegy?

Not yet. The copay card, the coming 2027 Medicare price, or patient assistance are the routes to lower cost.

Is Trelegy cheaper than separate inhalers?

Not always. Trelegy is more convenient, but some individual inhalers have generics and can be cheaper. Ask your doctor if cost is a concern.
