Fasenra cost, coupon & copay card

Fasenra (benralizumab) is a biologic for severe eosinophilic asthma, given by injection every eight weeks after a loading phase.

The short answer

Fasenra costs about $5,000 per dose at list price (dosed every 8 weeks in maintenance). Commercially insured patients can pay as little as $0 with the Fasenra copay card. Medicare patients are protected by the $2,100 Part D cap.

Fasenra cost with Medicare

Fasenra is not on the Medicare negotiated-price list. Part D members pay plan cost-share up to the $2,100 annual cap. Copay cards cannot be used with Medicare.

Patient assistance for Fasenra

AstraZeneca provides Fasenra free to uninsured, income-eligible patients through AZ&Me.

AZ&Me Prescription Savings Program

Cash-pay strategies for Fasenra

Uninsured, income-eligible patients should apply to AZ&Me. Because Fasenra is dosed every 8 weeks, the per-dose price translates to fewer payments per year than monthly biologics — factor that in when comparing.

Cheaper alternatives to Fasenra

Other severe-asthma biologics include Nucala (mepolizumab), Xolair (omalizumab) and Dupixent (dupilumab), each targeting a different pathway. Which fits depends on your asthma type and biomarkers.

For severe asthma biologics, the eosinophil count and prior-treatment history are the gatekeepers. Coverage hinges on documenting that your asthma is the specific type the drug treats and that standard therapy failed. If Fasenra is denied, that biomarker documentation is nearly always what's needed.

Fasenra cost — frequently asked questions

How much is Fasenra without insurance?

About $5,000 per dose at list price, dosed every 8 weeks in maintenance. Uninsured, income-eligible patients can get it free through AZ&Me.

How do I get Fasenra for $0?

With commercial insurance, the Fasenra copay card can bring you to as little as $0. Not usable with Medicare or Medicaid.

Does Medicare cover Fasenra?

Generally under Part D with prior authorization. The $2,100 annual cap limits your cost; copay cards are not usable on Medicare.

How often is Fasenra given?

After a loading phase, Fasenra is given every 8 weeks — fewer injections than some other asthma biologics.

What if my Fasenra is denied?

Denials usually stem from missing documentation of eosinophil levels or prior treatments. Your specialist can supply this for a prior-authorization appeal.
