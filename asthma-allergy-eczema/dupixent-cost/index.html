Dupixent cost, coupon & copay card

Dupixent (dupilumab) is a biologic injection for eczema, asthma, chronic rhinosinusitis and COPD.

The short answer

Dupixent costs about $3,800 a month at list price. Commercially insured patients can pay as little as $0 with the Dupixent MyWay copay card. Medicare patients are protected by the $2,100 Part D cap; uninsured patients can apply for free medication.

Dupixent cost with Medicare

Dupixent is not on the current Medicare negotiated-price list. Part D members pay plan cost-share up to the $2,100 annual cap. Copay cards cannot be used with Medicare.

Patient assistance for Dupixent

Sanofi and Regeneron provide Dupixent free to uninsured, income-eligible patients through the MyWay program.

Dupixent MyWay Patient Assistance

Cash-pay strategies for Dupixent

Uninsured, income-eligible patients should apply through Dupixent MyWay rather than face the list price. Because Dupixent covers several conditions, make sure the prescription and prior authorization are written for your specific diagnosis — mismatches are a common denial reason.

Cheaper alternatives to Dupixent

For asthma specifically, other biologics (Fasenra, Xolair, Nucala) target different pathways. For eczema, newer options include oral JAK inhibitors. The right choice depends on your condition and biomarkers — a specialist decision.

Dupixent's breadth is a double-edged sword: it treats many conditions, but coverage is granted per indication. I've seen approvals stall simply because the paperwork cited the wrong diagnosis. Make sure the prior authorization matches exactly what you're being treated for, and let MyWay help your clinic assemble it.

Dupixent cost — frequently asked questions

How much is Dupixent without insurance?

About $3,800 a month at list price. Uninsured, income-eligible patients can get it free through Dupixent MyWay patient assistance.

How do I get Dupixent cheaper?

With commercial insurance, the Dupixent MyWay copay card can bring you to as little as $0. On Medicare, rely on Part D and the $2,100 cap. Uninsured: MyWay patient assistance.

How do I get Dupixent for eczema covered?

Coverage usually requires prior authorization documenting your diagnosis and prior treatments. MyWay helps assemble this; make sure it matches your specific condition.

Does Medicare cover Dupixent?

Generally under Part D with prior authorization. The $2,100 annual cap limits your cost; copay cards are not usable on Medicare.

Is there a biosimilar for Dupixent?

Not yet in the US. The copay card, Part D cap, or MyWay assistance are the current routes to lower cost.
