Symbicort cost, coupon & generic options

Symbicort (budesonide/formoterol) is a twice-daily maintenance inhaler for asthma and COPD — and it now has a cheaper generic.

Biosimilar availableManufacturer copay cardPatient assistance

The short answer

Branded Symbicort costs about $350 a month at list price, but a generic budesonide/formoterol is available and cheaper — often the best route. Commercially insured patients can also use the Symbicort savings card; uninsured patients should compare the generic with a discount card.

Symbicort cost with Medicare

Symbicort is not on the Medicare negotiated-price list. Part D members pay plan cost-share up to the $2,100 annual cap — and where the generic is stocked, a plan may price it lower. Copay cards cannot be used with Medicare.

Patient assistance for Symbicort

AstraZeneca provides Symbicort free to uninsured, income-eligible patients through AZ&Me.

Cash-pay strategies for Symbicort

The biggest lever is the generic: ask your pharmacist for generic budesonide/formoterol and price it with GoodRx or SingleCare — it usually beats the brand. This is one of the cheaper drugs in the category, so a discount card often suffices.

Cheaper alternatives to Symbicort

Other ICS/LABA inhalers include Breo Ellipta and Advair (which also has a generic). Trelegy adds a third medicine for patients who need it. Cost differences among these are smaller than in the biologic group.

Symbicort is a reminder that not every respiratory drug is a budget crisis. With a generic now available, most patients can get effective maintenance therapy cheaply. If you're paying brand prices out of habit, ask the pharmacist for the generic — it's the simplest saving on this whole page.

Symbicort cost — frequently asked questions

Is there a generic for Symbicort?

Yes — generic budesonide/formoterol is available in the US and is usually cheaper than branded Symbicort. Ask your pharmacist.

How much is Symbicort without insurance?

Branded Symbicort is about $350 a month; the generic is less. Compare the generic with a discount card for the lowest price.

Can I use the Symbicort savings card on Medicare?

No — commercial insurance only. Medicare patients use Part D and the $2,100 cap; the generic may be inexpensive on a Part D plan.

Is the generic as good as Symbicort?

Generic budesonide/formoterol contains the same active ingredients and meets FDA standards. Discuss any switch with your doctor, especially inhaler technique with a different device.

What is the cheapest asthma inhaler?

Among maintenance inhalers, generics like budesonide/formoterol or generic Advair are usually cheapest. The right one is a clinical decision, but generics are the place to start on cost.
