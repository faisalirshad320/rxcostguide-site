Xolair cost, coupon & copay card

Xolair (omalizumab) is an anti-IgE biologic for allergic asthma, chronic hives, food allergy and nasal polyps — its cost depends on your dose.

The short answer

Xolair costs roughly $1,500 to $3,000 a month depending on your dose and weight. Commercially insured patients can pay as little as $0 with the Xolair Co-pay Card. Medicare patients are protected by the $2,100 Part D cap.

Xolair is unusual in that its dose — and therefore its cost — is calculated from your body weight and IgE level, so the monthly figure varies by patient. The copay card caps a commercial patient's share regardless of dose.

Xolair cost with Medicare

Xolair is not on the Medicare negotiated-price list. Part D members pay plan cost-share up to the $2,100 annual cap. (Some Xolair given in a clinic may fall under Part B instead — ask how yours is billed.) Copay cards cannot be used with Medicare.

Patient assistance for Xolair

Genentech provides Xolair free to uninsured, income-eligible patients through Access Solutions.

Cash-pay strategies for Xolair

Uninsured, income-eligible patients should apply to Genentech Access Solutions. Ask whether your Xolair is billed under the pharmacy benefit or as a clinic-administered drug (medical benefit) — it changes which savings route applies.

Cheaper alternatives to Xolair

Other options depend on the condition: Dupixent, Fasenra and Nucala for asthma; different approaches for chronic hives. The choice is driven by your specific diagnosis and biomarkers.

Xolair is a good example of the pharmacy-versus-medical-benefit split. When a biologic is given in a clinic, it may be billed under the medical benefit (Part B on Medicare) rather than the pharmacy benefit, which changes the savings options entirely. Always ask how yours is billed — it determines which card or program applies.

Xolair cost — frequently asked questions

How much is Xolair without insurance?

Roughly $1,500 to $3,000 a month depending on your weight- and IgE-based dose. Uninsured, income-eligible patients can get it free through Genentech Access Solutions.

How do I get Xolair for $0?

With commercial insurance, the Xolair Co-pay Card can bring you to as little as $0. Not usable with Medicare or Medicaid.

Why does Xolair cost vary so much?

The dose is calculated from your body weight and IgE level, so the amount of drug — and the price — differs between patients.

Does Medicare cover Xolair?

Yes, but it may fall under Part B (if clinic-administered) or Part D (if self-injected). Ask how yours is billed; the $2,100 Part D cap applies to the pharmacy-benefit route.

Is there a biosimilar for Xolair?

Not yet widely in the US. The copay card, Part D cap, or patient assistance are the current routes to lower cost.
