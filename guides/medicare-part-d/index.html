Medicare Part D in 2026: what you actually pay

The biggest change to Medicare drug coverage in years landed in 2025–2026. Here is what Part D costs you now.

Reviewed by Dr. Faisal Irshad, MBBS, M.Phil Chemical Pathology PMDC 54440-SUpdated 2026-10-01
The short answer

In 2026, Medicare Part D has a hard $2,100 annual out-of-pocket cap on covered drugs — once you spend that, covered prescriptions cost you $0 for the rest of the year. The old "donut hole" coverage gap is gone, and insulin is capped at $35 a month.

The $2,100 out-of-pocket cap

This is the headline. Once your out-of-pocket spending on covered Part D drugs reaches $2,100 in 2026, you pay nothing more for them that year. For anyone on expensive medication, this cap — not the drug's list price — is now the number that determines annual spending.

Spreading payments (M3P)

The Medicare Prescription Payment Plan lets you spread your out-of-pocket costs across the year in monthly installments instead of paying a big amount up front. It doesn't lower the total, but it smooths the cash-flow hit. You opt in through your plan.

Insulin and vaccines

Covered insulin is capped at $35 per month per product under Part D, and recommended adult vaccines (like shingles) are $0. These apply regardless of the deductible.

Negotiated prices

From 2026, the first ten negotiated drugs (including Eliquis, Xarelto, Jardiance, Farxiga, Entresto) have Medicare Maximum Fair Prices applied automatically at the counter. See our [negotiated-prices guide](/guides/medicare-negotiated-prices/).

How to lower your Part D costs

Review your plan every open enrollment — formularies change yearly. Ask whether generics or negotiated-price drugs can replace pricier ones. Apply for Extra Help (the Low-Income Subsidy) if your income is limited; it can eliminate premiums and slash copays.

FAQ

What is the Medicare Part D cap in 2026?

About $2,100 in out-of-pocket spending on covered drugs. After you reach it, covered prescriptions cost you $0 for the rest of the calendar year.

Is the Medicare donut hole gone?

Yes. The coverage-gap "donut hole" was eliminated as part of the same reforms. Part D now has a simpler deductible → 25% cost-share → $2,100 cap structure.

How much is insulin on Medicare in 2026?

Covered insulin is capped at $35 per month per product, with no deductible applied to it.
