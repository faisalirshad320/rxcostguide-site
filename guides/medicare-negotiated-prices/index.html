Medicare-negotiated drug prices: the 2026 and 2027 lists

For the first time, Medicare negotiates what it pays for some of the costliest drugs. Here is the full list and what each now costs.

Reviewed by Dr. Faisal Irshad, MBBS, M.Phil Chemical Pathology PMDC 54440-SUpdated 2026-10-01
The short answer

The first ten Medicare-negotiated prices took effect 1 January 2026, cutting list prices by 38–79%. A second group of fifteen drugs — including Ozempic, Wegovy, Trelegy and Otezla — has negotiated prices effective 1 January 2027. The negotiated "Maximum Fair Price" applies automatically at the pharmacy for Part D members.

The 2026 list (in effect now)

These ten drugs have Medicare Maximum Fair Prices effective 1 January 2026: Eliquis (~$231), Jardiance (~$197), Xarelto (~$197), Januvia (~$113), Farxiga (~$178.50), Entresto (~$295), Enbrel (~$2,355), Imbruvica, Stelara (~$4,695), and Fiasp/NovoLog insulin (~$119). Figures are per 30-day equivalent and applied automatically at the counter.

The 2027 list (effective next)

A second group of fifteen drugs has negotiated prices effective 1 January 2027. It includes the semaglutide products Ozempic, Wegovy and Rybelsus, plus Trelegy, Otezla, Xtandi, Calquence, Pomalyst, Ibrance, Ofev, Linzess, Austedo, Breo Ellipta, Tradjenta and Vraylar. Until 2027, these are priced normally — so copay cards and assistance still matter most for them.

How the price reaches you

You do nothing. The Maximum Fair Price becomes what your Part D plan pays at the pharmacy. Your own cost-share is based on that lower price, and everything counts toward the $2,100 annual out-of-pocket cap. If your pharmacy seems to charge against the old price, raise it with them or your plan.

FAQ

Which drugs have Medicare-negotiated prices in 2026?

Eliquis, Jardiance, Xarelto, Januvia, Farxiga, Entresto, Enbrel, Imbruvica, Stelara and Fiasp/NovoLog insulin — effective 1 January 2026.

Is Ozempic price negotiated by Medicare?

Ozempic (and Wegovy/Rybelsus) are in the second negotiation cycle, with a Medicare price effective 1 January 2027 — not yet in 2026.

Do I have to apply for the negotiated price?

No. It applies automatically at the pharmacy for Medicare Part D members. There is nothing to sign up for.
