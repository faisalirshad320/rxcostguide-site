Contrave cost, coupon & savings program

Contrave (naltrexone/bupropion) is an oral weight-loss pill — a lower-cost, non-injection alternative to the GLP-1 drugs.

The short answer

Contrave lists at about $700 a month, but the manufacturer's Contrave Now / cash program offers it far cheaper — often a fixed low monthly price regardless of insurance. That makes it one of the more affordable prescription weight-loss options.

Contrave cost with Medicare

Medicare does not cover Contrave for weight loss. The manufacturer cash program is available regardless of insurance status, which is often the practical route for Medicare and uninsured patients alike.

Patient assistance for Contrave

The manufacturer's cash/home-delivery program is the main affordability route and is open to most patients regardless of coverage.

Contrave Now cash program

Cash-pay strategies for Contrave

The manufacturer cash program is usually the cheapest route and is open regardless of insurance. Because Contrave combines two inexpensive generics, some prescribers also discuss taking generic naltrexone and bupropion separately — ask your doctor whether that is appropriate and cheaper for you.

Cheaper alternatives to Contrave

The GLP-1 injections (Wegovy, Zepbound) are more effective on average but far more expensive and require injections. Contrave's trade-off is lower cost and oral dosing for generally more modest weight loss.

Contrave is worth knowing about precisely because it's cheap and oral. Not everyone needs — or can afford — a GLP-1 injection. For a cost-sensitive patient who wants a pharmacological option, the manufacturer cash program makes Contrave genuinely accessible, and the two components are old, well-understood generics.

Contrave cost — frequently asked questions

How much is Contrave per month?

Retail list is about $700, but the manufacturer cash program offers a low fixed monthly price that is usually far cheaper and open regardless of insurance.

Does insurance cover Contrave?

Some commercial plans do; Medicare does not cover it for weight loss. Many patients use the manufacturer cash program instead of insurance because it is cheaper.

Is Contrave cheaper than Wegovy or Zepbound?

Generally yes. Contrave's cash program is well below the GLP-1 injections, though the GLP-1 drugs produce greater average weight loss.

Can I take generic naltrexone and bupropion instead?

Some prescribers discuss this, since Contrave combines two older generics. Whether it is appropriate and cheaper for you is a decision for your doctor.

Is Contrave a GLP-1 drug?

No. Contrave is an oral combination of naltrexone and bupropion, a different mechanism from the GLP-1 injections.
