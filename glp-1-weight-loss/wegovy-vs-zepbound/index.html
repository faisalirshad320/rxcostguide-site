Wegovy vs Zepbound: cost, coverage & which is right for you

The two leading weight-loss injectables, compared on what actually drives your out-of-pocket cost in 2026.

Reviewed by Dr. Faisal Irshad, MBBS, M.Phil Chemical Pathology PMDC 54440-SUpdated 2026-10-01
The short answer

Wegovy (semaglutide) lists near $1,350/month, Zepbound (tirzepatide) near $1,060/month. Both have self-pay routes below list (NovoCare for Wegovy, LillyDirect vials for Zepbound) and both savings cards reach $0–$25 where a commercial plan covers weight loss. Neither is covered by Medicare for weight loss.

When Wegovy tends to be the choice

Wegovy (semaglutide) has extensive cardiovascular-outcome data and, for some patients, may be covered by Medicare specifically to reduce heart-attack and stroke risk — a route Zepbound does not share. Its card can reach $0 with covering commercial plans.

When Zepbound tends to be the choice

Zepbound (tirzepatide) generally produces greater average weight loss in trials and has a slightly lower list price plus widely used self-pay vials, which can make the cash route cheaper.

Which is cheaper for you?

Commercial (covered): Wegovy can reach $0, Zepbound ~$25 — check which your plan covers. Cash/uninsured: compare NovoCare (Wegovy) against LillyDirect vials (Zepbound); the tirzepatide vials are often the lower cash price. Medicare: neither is covered for weight loss, though Wegovy may be covered for cardiovascular risk. Choose on clinical fit with your doctor, not a small price gap.

FAQ

Is Wegovy or Zepbound cheaper?

Zepbound has a lower list price (~$1,060 vs ~$1,350). Wegovy's card can reach $0 with covering plans; Zepbound's reaches ~$25. For cash payers, LillyDirect (Zepbound) vials are often cheapest.

Which causes more weight loss?

Trials generally show greater average weight loss with tirzepatide (Zepbound) than semaglutide (Wegovy), but individual results vary. The choice is your doctor's; this page compares cost.

Does Medicare cover either for weight loss?

No. Neither is covered by Medicare for weight loss. Wegovy may be covered specifically to reduce cardiovascular risk in eligible patients.
