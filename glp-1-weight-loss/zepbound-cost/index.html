Zepbound cost, coupon & savings card

Zepbound (tirzepatide) is a once-weekly injection for weight loss and obstructive sleep apnea — the same molecule as Mounjaro, approved for weight management.

The short answer

Zepbound costs about $1,060 a month at list price for the pens, but self-pay single-dose vials through LillyDirect are cheaper. Commercially insured patients with coverage can pay as little as $25 with the Zepbound savings card. Medicare does not cover it for weight loss.

The Zepbound copay card

The Zepbound savings card can bring commercially insured patients whose plan covers Zepbound to as little as $25 per month.

If your commercial plan covers Zepbound, the card can bring you to about $25 a month. If it doesn't, Lilly's self-pay vials through LillyDirect are the cheaper route at a set cash price below the pen list. Commercial insurance only — not usable with Medicare or Medicaid.

Zepbound cost with Medicare

Medicare does not cover Zepbound for weight loss. Coverage for obstructive sleep apnea may vary by plan. The tirzepatide molecule (as Mounjaro) has a Medicare-negotiated price effective 2027 for diabetes, not weight loss. Copay cards cannot be used with Medicare.

Patient assistance for Zepbound

LillyDirect self-pay vials are the main lower-cost cash option. Income-eligible uninsured patients may qualify for free medication through the Lilly Cares Foundation.

Cash-pay strategies for Zepbound

The LillyDirect self-pay vials are usually the cheapest legitimate route — well below the ~$1,060 pen list price. As with semaglutide, avoid unregulated compounded tirzepatide now that approved supply is stable; the safety risk outweighs the shrinking price gap. Check LillyDirect for current vial pricing.

Cheaper alternatives to Zepbound

Wegovy (semaglutide) is the main alternative for weight loss; trials generally show greater average weight loss with tirzepatide, but individual response varies. See our Wegovy vs Zepbound comparison.

Zepbound is the clearest example of self-pay vials changing the maths. A patient whose plan excludes weight-loss drugs used to face the full pen price; now the LillyDirect vial is a far more reasonable cash figure. I point uninsured and excluded-plan patients straight to the manufacturer vial programs — not to the grey market.

Zepbound cost — frequently asked questions

How much is Zepbound without insurance?

About $1,060 a month for the pens at list price, but LillyDirect self-pay single-dose vials are cheaper. The vials are usually the cheapest legitimate cash route.

How much is Zepbound with the savings card?

As little as $25 a month if your commercial plan covers it. If your plan doesn't cover it, use LillyDirect self-pay vials instead. Not usable with Medicare.

Does Medicare cover Zepbound?

Not for weight loss. Coverage for obstructive sleep apnea may vary by plan; check your Part D coverage.

Is Zepbound or Wegovy more effective?

Clinical trials generally show greater average weight loss with tirzepatide (Zepbound) than semaglutide (Wegovy), but individual results vary and the choice is your doctor's. This page compares cost, not clinical outcomes.

Are Zepbound self-pay vials safe?

Yes — they are the FDA-approved product sold directly by the manufacturer. That is quite different from unregulated compounded tirzepatide, which should be avoided now that approved supply is stable.
