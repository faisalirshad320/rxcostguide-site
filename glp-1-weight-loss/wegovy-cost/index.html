Wegovy cost, coupon & savings card

Wegovy (semaglutide) is a once-weekly injection for chronic weight management — the same molecule as Ozempic, approved specifically for weight loss.

The short answer

Wegovy costs about $1,350 a month at list price. Commercially insured patients with coverage can pay as little as $0 with the Wegovy savings card. For cash payers, NovoCare self-pay offers Wegovy well below list price. Medicare does not cover Wegovy for weight loss (it may cover it to reduce cardiovascular risk in specific patients).

Wegovy cost with Medicare

Medicare does not cover Wegovy for weight loss. It may be covered under Part D specifically to reduce the risk of heart attack and stroke in adults with cardiovascular disease and obesity. The semaglutide molecule (as Ozempic) is in the second Medicare negotiation cycle with a price effective 2027, but that applies to the diabetes indication. Copay cards cannot be used with Medicare.

Patient assistance for Wegovy

Novo Nordisk's NovoCare provides self-pay pricing and, for income-eligible uninsured patients, a patient-assistance program. Start at NovoCare to see which applies to you.

Cash-pay strategies for Wegovy

For cash payers, the NovoCare self-pay price is the cheapest legitimate route — well below the ~$1,350 pen list price. Avoid unregulated "compounded semaglutide" sellers; with FDA-approved supply restored, compounded versions are no longer permitted in most cases and carry real safety risks. Ask NovoCare about current self-pay pricing.

Cheaper alternatives to Wegovy

Zepbound (tirzepatide) is the main alternative for weight loss and often produces greater average weight loss in trials; it also has self-pay vials. See our Wegovy vs Zepbound comparison. Older oral options (Contrave) cost less but work differently.

The compounded-semaglutide question comes up constantly. My blunt advice: now that FDA-approved supply is stable, stick to the branded product or the manufacturer's own self-pay vials. The price gap that made compounding tempting has narrowed, and an unregulated injectable is not a risk worth taking for a modest saving. NovoCare self-pay is the honest cheap route.

Wegovy cost — frequently asked questions

How much is Wegovy without insurance?

About $1,350 a month at list price for the pens, but NovoCare self-pay offers it well below that. Check NovoCare for the current cash price, which is usually the cheapest legitimate option.

Does Medicare cover Wegovy?

Not for weight loss. Medicare may cover Wegovy specifically to reduce cardiovascular risk in adults with heart disease and obesity. Check your Part D plan.

How do I get Wegovy for $0?

If you have commercial insurance that covers Wegovy, the manufacturer savings card can bring you to as little as $0 a month. If your plan doesn't cover it, look at NovoCare self-pay instead.

Is Wegovy the same as Ozempic?

Both are semaglutide. Wegovy is dosed and approved for weight loss; Ozempic is approved for type 2 diabetes. Coverage and pricing differ by indication.

Is compounded semaglutide cheaper?

It was during shortages, but with FDA-approved supply restored, compounded semaglutide is largely no longer permitted and carries safety risks. Manufacturer self-pay vials are the safe low-cost route now.
