Trintellix cost, coupon & copay card

Trintellix (vortioxetine) is a once-daily branded antidepressant for major depressive disorder, with no generic yet available.

The short answer

Trintellix costs about $570 a month at list price. Commercially insured patients can usually pay as little as $10 a month with the Trintellix savings card. There is no generic yet. Because many cheap generic antidepressants exist, it is worth asking whether one suits you before paying brand prices.

Trintellix is a long-term, once-daily antidepressant. Unlike older antidepressants — almost all of which are cheap generics — it is brand-only, so cost depends heavily on your insurance and whether a generic alternative is appropriate for you.

Trintellix cost with Medicare

Trintellix has no Medicare-negotiated price. For 2026, Medicare patients pay their Part D plan's cost-share, capped by the $2,100 annual out-of-pocket maximum. Copay cards cannot be used with Medicare. Because effective generic antidepressants exist, a Part D plan may strongly prefer one of those.

Patient assistance for Trintellix

Takeda provides Trintellix free to uninsured, income-eligible patients through its Help At Hand program. Proof of income and a prescription are required.

Takeda Help At Hand

Cash-pay strategies for Trintellix

There is no generic vortioxetine yet, so cash payers face the full brand price unless they qualify for assistance. Compare discount-card prices, apply to Takeda Help At Hand if uninsured and income-eligible, and ask your prescriber whether a generic antidepressant (there are many effective ones) is appropriate for you.

Cheaper alternatives to Trintellix

Nearly every older antidepressant — SSRIs like sertraline and escitalopram, SNRIs like venlafaxine and duloxetine, and bupropion — is available as an inexpensive generic. Trintellix works differently and may be chosen for specific reasons, but for cost, a generic is worth discussing first. Any switch is a clinical decision.

Antidepressants are the category where the generic conversation matters most, because the generics are genuinely excellent and cost almost nothing. Trintellix has a distinct mechanism and real advantages for some patients, but I always want the prescriber to have weighed whether a $4 generic would do the same job before a patient commits to a brand-only drug long term.

Trintellix cost — frequently asked questions

Is there a generic for Trintellix?

Not yet — vortioxetine is still brand-only in the US. Many other antidepressants are available as cheap generics, though they work differently.

How much is Trintellix with the savings card?

As little as $10 a month for commercially insured patients. The card cannot be used with Medicare or Medicaid.

What does Trintellix cost without insurance?

About $570 a month at list price. Uninsured, income-eligible patients can get it free through Takeda Help At Hand.

Is Trintellix better than a generic antidepressant?

It has a distinct mechanism and suits some patients, but it is not automatically better than cheap, effective generics. Which antidepressant is right for you is a clinical decision for your prescriber.

Can I use the Trintellix card on Medicare?

No — commercial insurance only. Medicare patients pay plan cost-share under the $2,100 Part D cap.
