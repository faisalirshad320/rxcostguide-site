Austedo cost, coupon & copay card

Austedo (deutetrabenazine) is an oral VMAT2 inhibitor used to treat tardive dyskinesia and the chorea of Huntington's disease in adults.

The short answer

Austedo has a list price of ~$5,163+/mo, but commercially insured patients may pay as little as $0 per monthly fill with the Austedo Teva Shared Solutions Copay Program. On Medicare Part D, copay cards are not allowed, but your out-of-pocket drug spending is capped at $2,100 in 2026. If you are uninsured or underinsured, the Teva Cares Foundation may be able to help.

Austedo is taken by mouth on a daily schedule to reduce the involuntary movements of tardive dyskinesia or Huntington's disease chorea, so it is typically a long-term, recurring cost. It is a brand-only drug with no generic currently available, and the dose is titrated to effect, which drives a high monthly list price. What you actually pay depends mostly on your insurance type, your plan's formulary tier, and whether you qualify for manufacturer support.

The Austedo copay card

Commercially insured patients may pay as little as $0 per monthly fill with the Austedo Teva Shared Solutions Copay Program.

The Austedo Teva Shared Solutions Copay Program helps lower out-of-pocket costs for patients whose prescription is covered by commercial or private insurance. Program benefits are subject to an annual maximum per prescription per year, so review the program terms when you enroll. The card cannot be used by patients with Medicare, Medicaid, or other government insurance.

Austedo cost with Medicare

Austedo is not on the list of drugs with a Medicare negotiated price, so your cost depends on your Part D plan's formulary tier and coverage phase. In 2026, Part D out-of-pocket drug spending is capped at $2,100 per year, which limits what a high-cost medicine like Austedo can cost you annually. Federal rules bar manufacturer copay cards for Medicare beneficiaries, so the Teva copay program cannot be used; ask your plan about spreading costs over the year with the Medicare Prescription Payment Plan.

Patient assistance for Austedo

The Teva Cares Foundation is Teva's patient assistance program for people who cannot afford their medication. Eligibility generally depends on insurance status and household income, so check the foundation's website for current criteria and the application process.

Cash-pay strategies for Austedo

Without insurance, Austedo's list price is high at roughly ~$5,163+/mo, and pharmacy discount cards typically offer only modest savings on brand-only specialty drugs. Uninsured patients should apply to the Teva Cares Foundation first. It is also worth asking your prescriber whether another treatment option could be a lower-cost fit for your situation.

Cheaper alternatives to Austedo

Other VMAT2 inhibitors are used for tardive dyskinesia and Huntington's disease chorea, including an older agent available as a generic for Huntington's chorea. Your prescriber can help weigh differences in dosing, side effects, and coverage.

I tell patients that the sticker price of Austedo rarely reflects what they will pay, so the first step is confirming their insurance type and enrolling in the copay program or applying for assistance before the first fill. Because the dose is adjusted gradually, I remind them that the monthly cost can change as we find the right dose. For patients with Huntington's disease, I also stress reporting any new depression or mood changes right away, since that is an important safety consideration with this medication.

Austedo cost — frequently asked questions

How much does Austedo cost with the copay card?

With commercial insurance, you may pay as little as $0 per monthly fill with the Austedo Teva Shared Solutions Copay Program. An annual program maximum applies per prescription per year.

Is there a generic or biosimilar for Austedo?

No. Austedo (deutetrabenazine) is a small-molecule drug, so biosimilars do not apply, and no generic version is currently available.

How much is Austedo without insurance?

The list price is approximately ~$5,163+/mo. Uninsured patients may qualify for help through the Teva Cares Foundation.

Does Medicare cover Austedo, and can I use the copay card?

Coverage and cost vary by Part D plan, and Austedo does not have a Medicare negotiated price. You cannot use the copay card with Medicare, but your 2026 Part D out-of-pocket spending is capped at $2,100.

How do I enroll in the Austedo copay program?

Visit the official Austedo support page at https://www.austedo.com/tardive-dyskinesia/support to check eligibility and enroll. The program is for commercial insurance only, not Medicare or Medicaid.
