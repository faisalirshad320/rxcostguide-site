Ingrezza cost, coupon & copay card

Ingrezza (valbenazine) is a once-daily oral VMAT2 inhibitor used to treat adults with tardive dyskinesia or chorea caused by Huntington's disease.

The short answer

Ingrezza has a list price of about ~$7,900/mo. People with commercial insurance may pay as little as $0 per monthly fill with the Ingrezza Savings Program (annual maximum applies). Medicare Part D enrollees can't use the copay card, but their 2026 out-of-pocket drug costs are capped at $2,100 for the year. Uninsured or underinsured patients can ask about the INBRACE Support Program.

Ingrezza is taken by mouth once daily to reduce the involuntary movements of tardive dyskinesia and Huntington's chorea, and many patients stay on it long term. Because it is a brand-name specialty medication with a high list price, what you actually pay depends heavily on your insurance type, your plan's formulary tier, and whether you use manufacturer support programs. A generic valbenazine is FDA-approved but not widely available, so most patients still fill the brand.

The Ingrezza copay card

Commercially insured patients may pay as little as $0 per monthly fill with the Ingrezza Savings Program.

The Ingrezza Savings Program is a manufacturer copay card from Neurocrine Biosciences that lowers out-of-pocket costs for patients whose commercial or private insurance covers Ingrezza. An annual maximum applies, so the savings can run out before the end of the year for some patients. The program is for commercial insurance only and cannot be used with Medicare, Medicaid, or other government insurance.

Ingrezza cost with Medicare

Ingrezza does not have a Medicare negotiated price (maximum fair price) in effect. Most Part D plans cover it, though tier placement, prior authorization, and cost sharing vary by plan. In 2026, the Medicare Part D out-of-pocket cap is $2,100 for the year, which limits what enrollees pay for covered drugs, including Ingrezza. Federal rules bar manufacturer copay cards for Medicare beneficiaries, so the Ingrezza Savings Program cannot be used; ask about the INBRACE Support Program or other assistance instead.

Patient assistance for Ingrezza

The INBRACE Support Program is Neurocrine Biosciences' patient support service for Ingrezza. It can help with insurance coverage questions, prior authorizations, and affordability options, including for patients who are uninsured or cannot use the copay card. Eligibility is determined by the program, so contact INBRACE directly to see what you qualify for.

Cash-pay strategies for Ingrezza

Without insurance, Ingrezza is expensive, with a list price of about ~$7,900/mo, and pharmacy discount cards typically offer only limited savings on brand specialty drugs like this. The best first step is to contact the INBRACE Support Program to ask about financial assistance. A generic valbenazine is FDA-approved but not widely available, so ask your pharmacist whether it can be obtained and what it would cost.

Cheaper alternatives to Ingrezza

Another VMAT2 inhibitor may be an option for tardive dyskinesia or Huntington's chorea, and coverage and cost can differ between them depending on your plan's formulary. Talk with your prescriber about whether a different agent makes sense clinically and financially.

I tell patients with commercial insurance to enroll in the Ingrezza Savings Program before their first fill, because it can bring the monthly cost down dramatically, and to keep track of the annual maximum. For my Medicare patients, I remind them that the copay card is off-limits but that the 2026 $2,100 Part D cap puts a ceiling on their yearly drug spending. If coverage is denied or a prior authorization stalls, I point them to the INBRACE Support Program, which can help work through the paperwork.

Ingrezza cost — frequently asked questions

How much does Ingrezza cost with the copay card?

With commercial insurance, you may pay as little as $0 per monthly fill with the Ingrezza Savings Program. An annual maximum applies, and the card is for commercial insurance only, not Medicare or Medicaid.

Is there a generic or biosimilar for Ingrezza?

Ingrezza is a small-molecule pill, so biosimilars don't apply. A generic valbenazine is FDA-approved but not widely available, so most patients still receive brand-name Ingrezza.

How much is Ingrezza without insurance?

Ingrezza's list price is about ~$7,900/mo. Uninsured patients should contact the INBRACE Support Program at https://www.neurocrineaccesssupport.com/ to ask about financial assistance.

Does Medicare cover Ingrezza, and what will I pay?

Many Medicare Part D plans cover Ingrezza, but cost sharing varies by plan. There is no Medicare negotiated price in effect, and the copay card can't be used with Medicare. In 2026, your Part D out-of-pocket costs are capped at $2,100 for the year.

How do I enroll in the Ingrezza Savings Program?

You can enroll through the official financial support page at https://ingrezza.neurocrineaccesssupport.com/patient/financial-support/. You must have commercial insurance; Medicare and Medicaid patients are not eligible.
