Lybalvi cost, coupon & copay card

Lybalvi (olanzapine/samidorphan) is a once-daily oral atypical antipsychotic combination from Alkermes for adults with schizophrenia or bipolar I disorder.

The short answer

Lybalvi has a list price of about ~$1,697/mo without insurance. With commercial insurance and the Lybalvi Co-pay Savings Program, you may pay $0 for the first 3 fills, then $20 per 30-day supply (up to $450 off per fill). On Medicare Part D, the card cannot be used, but your total out-of-pocket drug costs are capped at $2,100 in 2026.

Lybalvi combines olanzapine, a well-established antipsychotic, with samidorphan, an opioid antagonist added to help limit the weight gain associated with olanzapine. It is a brand-only product with no generic equivalent, so the list price is high and your actual cost depends mostly on your insurance type, your plan's formulary tier, and whether you qualify for the manufacturer's co-pay or support programs.

The Lybalvi copay card

Commercially insured patients may pay $0 for the first 3 fills, then $20 per 30-day supply.

The Lybalvi Co-pay Savings Program lowers out-of-pocket costs for patients with commercial (private or employer) insurance. It is not flat $0: the first 3 fills may cost $0, after which you pay $20 per 30-day supply, with the program covering up to $450 off per fill. If your plan's cost share exceeds the program maximum, you may owe the difference. The card cannot be used with Medicare, Medicaid, or other government insurance.

Lybalvi cost with Medicare

Lybalvi is not among the drugs with a Medicare negotiated price. Coverage and cost sharing vary by Part D plan and formulary tier. Federal rules bar manufacturer co-pay cards for Medicare beneficiaries, but in 2026 Part D caps your total annual out-of-pocket drug costs at $2,100, and you can spread payments across the year through the Medicare Prescription Payment Plan.

Patient assistance for Lybalvi

Lybalvi Care Support is the manufacturer's support program. It can help with insurance questions, prior authorization, and identifying savings or assistance options. Patients who are uninsured, underinsured, or on government insurance should contact the program directly to see what help is available.

Cash-pay strategies for Lybalvi

Without insurance, Lybalvi is expensive at roughly ~$1,697/mo, and pharmacy discount cards typically offer limited savings on brand-only drugs. Contact Lybalvi Care Support to ask about assistance, and talk with your prescriber about whether a lower-cost antipsychotic, such as generic olanzapine alone, could be clinically appropriate for you.

Cheaper alternatives to Lybalvi

There is no generic version of Lybalvi, though olanzapine alone and several other atypical antipsychotics are available as lower-cost generics. Your prescriber can weigh effectiveness, metabolic side effects, and cost when considering alternatives.

I tell patients that the main reason to choose Lybalvi over plain olanzapine is to reduce weight gain, so cost should be weighed against that benefit together with their prescriber. Because samidorphan blocks opioids, I remind patients that Lybalvi must not be taken with opioid pain medicines or during opioid withdrawal, and to tell every clinician they see that they take it. If you have commercial insurance, enroll in the co-pay program before your first fill so the $0 fills aren't missed.

Lybalvi cost — frequently asked questions

How much does Lybalvi cost with the co-pay card?

With commercial insurance and the Lybalvi Co-pay Savings Program, you may pay $0 for the first 3 fills, then $20 per 30-day supply, with up to $450 off per fill. It is not flat $0 after the first 3 fills.

Is there a generic or biosimilar for Lybalvi?

No. Lybalvi (olanzapine/samidorphan) is available only as a brand-name product. Biosimilars apply to biologic drugs, not oral tablets like Lybalvi. Olanzapine alone is available as a generic, but it does not contain samidorphan.

How much does Lybalvi cost without insurance?

The list price is about ~$1,697/mo. Actual cash prices can vary by pharmacy. Lybalvi Care Support may be able to help you explore assistance options.

Does Medicare cover Lybalvi, and can I use the co-pay card?

Many Part D plans may cover Lybalvi, but cost sharing varies by plan. The co-pay card cannot be used with Medicare or Medicaid. In 2026, Part D caps your annual out-of-pocket drug costs at $2,100.

How do I enroll in the Lybalvi co-pay program?

You can enroll on the official Lybalvi Co-pay Savings Program page at https://www.lybalvi.com/bipolar-1-disorder/co-pay-savings-program. You must have commercial insurance. Patients with Medicare or Medicaid are not eligible.
