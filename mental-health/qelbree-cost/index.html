Qelbree cost, coupon & copay card

Qelbree (viloxazine) is a once-daily, non-stimulant oral medication from Supernus Pharmaceuticals used to treat ADHD in children, adolescents, and adults.

The short answer

Qelbree has a list price of about ~$400/mo. With commercial insurance, the Qelbree Savings Card can lower your cost to as little as $20 per prescription (not $0), subject to program terms. On Medicare, the card cannot be used, but your 2026 Part D out-of-pocket drug costs are capped at $2,100 for the year.

Qelbree is taken by mouth once daily and is often chosen when a non-stimulant option is preferred. Because it is a brand-name medication, what you actually pay depends heavily on your insurance plan's formulary tier, deductible, and whether prior authorization is required. Manufacturer savings and support programs can substantially reduce costs for eligible patients.

The Qelbree copay card

Eligible commercially insured patients may pay as little as $20 per prescription with the Qelbree Savings Card.

The Qelbree Savings Card is a manufacturer copay program for patients with commercial (private or employer) insurance. It lowers the out-of-pocket cost to as little as $20 per prescription; it does not reduce the cost to $0. It cannot be used with Medicare, Medicaid, or other government insurance. Program terms apply, so review the current terms when you enroll.

Qelbree cost with Medicare

Qelbree is not among the drugs with a Medicare-negotiated price, so there is no maximum fair price. Coverage and cost-sharing vary by Part D plan. Federal rules bar manufacturer copay cards for Medicare beneficiaries, so the Qelbree Savings Card cannot be used. However, in 2026 your total Part D out-of-pocket spending on covered drugs is capped at $2,100 for the year.

Patient assistance for Qelbree

Supernus Support is the manufacturer's cost-support resource for Qelbree. Patients who are uninsured, underinsured, or having trouble with coverage can review available options there or ask their prescriber's office to help them get started.

Cash-pay strategies for Qelbree

Without insurance, Qelbree's list price is about ~$400/mo, and pharmacy cash prices can vary. Free prescription discount cards may lower the cash price, and Supernus Support can help you explore manufacturer cost-support options. If cost remains a barrier, ask your prescriber whether a lower-cost generic ADHD medication would be appropriate for you.

Cheaper alternatives to Qelbree

No generic version of Qelbree is currently available in the US. Other non-stimulant ADHD medications, as well as stimulant options, are available as lower-cost generics. Your prescriber can help determine whether one of these is a reasonable substitute.

I tell patients to ask about insurance coverage before their first fill, because brand-name ADHD medications often need prior authorization, and sorting that out early prevents surprise costs at the pharmacy counter. I also remind commercially insured patients that the savings card brings the cost down to as little as $20 per prescription, not zero, so they can budget accordingly. For anyone on Medicare, I point out that the copay card won't work but the 2026 Part D out-of-pocket cap still limits their total yearly spending.

Qelbree cost — frequently asked questions

How much does Qelbree cost with the savings card?

With commercial insurance, the Qelbree Savings Card can lower your cost to as little as $20 per prescription. It does not make the medication free, and program terms apply.

Is there a generic version of Qelbree?

No. There is currently no generic viloxazine available in the US, so Qelbree is available only as a brand-name medication.

How much is Qelbree without insurance?

Qelbree's list price is about ~$400/mo. Cash prices vary by pharmacy, and discount cards or Supernus Support may help lower what you pay.

Does Medicare cover Qelbree, and can I use the savings card?

Coverage depends on your Part D plan. The Qelbree Savings Card cannot be used with Medicare or Medicaid, but in 2026 Part D out-of-pocket costs are capped at $2,100 per year.

How do I enroll in the Qelbree Savings Card?

You can enroll through the official Qelbree website at https://www.qelbree.com. You must have commercial insurance to be eligible.
